## Tenzo and the "crouch to see footsteps" hunch

The source's rock and lava hunter is Tetra. Her passive **Stonesight** reads: "Sneak to detect enemies in front of you, even if they're out of vision." The wiki says nothing about footsteps, range or how detected enemies are shown, so "see footsteps" is the owner's memory of the effect, not the wording. **Tenzo already has this mechanic** as Tremor Sense: crouch, and a forward cone that widens over 2s marks enemy hunters through walls and fog every 0.5s. He also has Molten Guard. Recommendation: **keep it; nothing to add.** It already suits a blind hunter who feels the ground. If the owner wants the footsteps idea, the only change would be a look change: draw the ping as fading footprints/ground ripples at each enemy's feet instead of a ring. Under PARITY FIRST that is a suggestion only, not a build.

## Recommendations (DIFFERENT and stale items)

- **R1, Albert:** finish the owner's cut (merge `b2e942eb4`). Add foe-into-foe Crush back (the current wiki has it, and so does our tooltip). Optionally narrow the trigger to Shield Bash plus the full-charge Smash.
- **R2, Meeko:** adopt the source passive. Add a carry shield that refills on each pickup, drop and self-stun only when it breaks or on CC, and a 20% carry slow. This reverses the 11-07 "ours-only rider" call, so the owner should confirm, ideally in-game, because the Nov 2 tooltip and the wiki disagree.
- **R3, Vex:** keep ours (one flat 6% level). Reason: the in-game tooltip (Nov 2) shows a flat buff, and stacking lifesteal on a rifle hunter snowballs. If a fresh in-game read says "stacking", allow 2-3 stacks.
- **R4, Lace:** keep ours, with a proposed reason: an uncapped reveal needs fog-of-war vision we don't have, so 22m and 50% define "injured" and "in range" for our maps. Mention the climb in the tooltip and fix archer.md:52.
- **R5, Sparkle:** text fix only: drop "heals you for 15%" from mage.json passive.desc.
- **R0, all:** the flat 250/500 HP knock heal is replaced by the shared 5% + 5% DashReset heal with no doc reason. Keep it and give the reason as "percent heals scale with our smaller health pools". Record that in docs/ARCHETYPES.md.
