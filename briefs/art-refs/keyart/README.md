# Key art

## The Thief's View (final)
`keyart_thief_view_final.jpg`

**The shot:**
- First-person view through Sable's eyes from a balcony.
- She tosses the Wyrmheart (a glass anatomical heart).
- Below her, the Street Crew (Vex, Sparkle, Tenzo) and the Tyrant's Former Court (Kael, Cinder, Lace) stop fighting and stare up at her.

**How it was made:**
- Fresh generation, then edits for the toss, the POV body and Cinder's skin.
- Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4K.
- Local restore: purple cast neutralised and sharpened.

`keyart_thief_view_wip.jpg` is the pre-restore checkpoint.

## The Face-off (final)
`keyart_faceoff_final.jpg`

**The shot:**
- Kael (left, fire behind him) and Vex (right, smoke and silhouettes behind her), shown from the chest up.
- The glass Wyrmheart floats between them.
- His greatsword, with white-hot cracks, angles up from his fist, and its tip rests under her chin.
- She grins up at him, unafraid. Her choker, coin, shoulder and tally marks are all visible.

**How it was made:**
- Base: the original face-off generation (Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4K).
- Edits, each a single change: minigun removed, crystal swapped for the heart (the cracks went white-hot in the same pass), duplicate upright blade removed.
- Local restore: colours matched back to the original, then sharpened.
- Rejected versions are in `explorations/`.

## The Last Light (final)
`keyart_last_light_final.jpg`

**The shot:**
- Mossy plants his figurehead shield against Motley, who lunges from the void in a storm of burning cards.
- Neve readies her spear behind the shield, and Meeko's sun blazes.
- Grimble cowers behind Mossy, clutching his loot.
- Together with the other two pieces, this completes the key art for all 12 heroes.

**How it was made:**
- A single fresh generation (Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4K). Refs: front views of Mossy, Neve, Meeko, Motley and Grimble, plus Meeko's companions sheet.
- Local pass: clarity and sharpening.
