# Malgrave, the Beloved: look guide (Nov 8, starting point for the owner's art)

Story in one line: an ancient dragon who ruled Solmere for 300 years as an ageless, beautiful queen behind the first mask. She hid her soul in the Wyrmheart and fed it her people. Kael wounded her, her body died in the Hollowing, and she lives on inside the cracked Heart.

## Her mask: LOCKED (owner, Nov 7, 23:07 ET)
File: `villain/malgrave_mask.jpg`.

**The mask:** a white porcelain masquerade eye-mask. It covers only her brow, eyes and upper cheekbones, so her face shows (the owner: she must be seductive).
- Gold dragon-scale crest on the brow, rising to one sharp point like a small crown.
- Slender upswept wing tips at the outer edges.
- Glossy crimson swirls curling from the eye holes.

**Worn:** the crimson tear-swirls continue down her real cheeks like painted tears. That is part of her face, not the mask.

**Lore:** this is the first mask. The Hunters' porcelain masks copy it.

**Superseded:** the full-face "Blood Tears" mask (`explorations/mask_full_blood_tears.jpg`) and the red-lacquer draft.

## Human form: LOCKED (owner, Nov 7, 23:36 ET)
This section overrides everything below where they conflict.

**Main design:** `malgrave_human.jpg`.
- **The tail:** her pearl-white serpent tail (fine scales, crimson fin ridge) loops into a huge infinity sign that frames her whole body. This is her signature silhouette, the "Undying Queen". Keep it in every pose where it fits.
- **The gown:** a clinging pearl-white silk gown with a neckline plunging to her navel, and a crimson scale belt at her tiny waist.
- **Collar and sleeves:** a tall fan of white feathers tipped in pink rises behind her head like a crest, and long sheer blush-pink sleeves pool on the floor.
- **Hands:** gold claw tips.
- **Pose:** serene and upright, with open hands.

**Alternates:**
- **Alternate pose:** `malgrave_human_alt_throne.jpg`. She sits on her own coils with her legs crossed, in the same plunging gown with crimson rose-patterned thigh-high heeled boots and a white feather mantle. This is the hot and classy version for splash art.
- **Second outfit:** `malgrave_human_alt_silk.jpg`. She reclines on her coils in two crossed silk bands with a sheer pink skirt and the rose boots. This is the most revealing version.

**Character:** she is not visibly evil. She wants to look as radiant, good and desirable as possible: adored, gracious and angelic, in pearl white, blush pink and crimson.

**Body:** an idealized pin-up figure. She is tall and slender, with a huge full bust, a tiny cinched waist and wide curvy hips.

**Mask:** the porcelain eye-mask (gold scale crest, crimson swirls), drawn small and close to the face. The wing tips are short or absent at this size, and the owner accepts this.

**Dropped:**
- The halo (the owner thinks it looks dumb).
- The stacked gold neck rings (the owner hates them).

**Next:** a turnaround of the main design.

## Human form: direction (owner, Nov 7; superseded by the lock above)

**Palette:** pearl-white silk, blush-pink sheer fabric and crimson accents. This separates her from Cinder, who is black and orange. The owner allows Solmere's colours to shift this way.

**Body and face:**
- A seductive hourglass figure; her face is visible below the mask (full crimson lips, a knowing smile, glowing pink-red eyes).
- Voluminous wavy dark-crimson hair. No crown and no horns.

**Dragon traits:**
- A pearl-white serpent tail (fine scales, a crimson fin ridge) coiling around her feet. The owner: humanoid animals exist in this world, so a real tail is fine.
- Crimson scales at her collarbone, wrists and leg, and gold claw rings.

**Glow:** always the Heart's pink-red, never fire or orange, so she never reads as Cinder.

**Base:** `explorations/malgrave_human_masquerade_D.jpg`.

**True form:** a wyrm is a serpent-dragon, so her true form can be a colossal, graceful serpent (wings optional). Keep the name Wyrmheart.

## Human form (the queen)
- Very tall (about a head taller than Kael), slender, ageless, impossibly graceful. Long neck, upright and still.
- Hair: long and dark red, almost black at the roots, worn up under the crown with one long fall down the back.
- Crown: tall and thin, gold spires that look like dragon horns, set with small crimson-glass stones that echo the Heart.
- Gown and armour: deep red layered gown with gold-plated gorget, bodice and pauldrons shaped like overlapping scales. A very long train that flows like a tail.
- Hands: elegant, with long gold claw-tip rings on every finger. The fingers are a little too long.
- Something inhuman, kept subtle: scales showing at the collarbone and wrists, a slight sheen on her skin, and her shadow is a dragon's.
- Colours: red and gold only (Solmere's colours), plus the pink-red Heart glow. No purple, no black armour.
- Feel: serene, regal, motherly-cold. She smiles at her subjects the way you smile at food.

## True form (the Wyrm)
- A huge, long, graceful dragon: more serpent and swan than brute. Long neck, slim head, swept-back horns that match the crown.
- Deep crimson scales, with the mask's gold pattern repeated across her face plates, spine and wing edges.
- Eyes the same soft pink-red glow as the Heart.
- Wing membranes deep red, light glowing through them like stained glass.
- A deep scar across her chest where Kael's blade went in, healed rough and darker than the rest.
- Where her heart should be, a hollow under the scales, with faint pink-red light seeping through.

## The Heart (locked, key art)
- A real anatomical heart of glossy crimson glass, soft pink-red glow inside, no gold veins, no visible crack. Ref: briefs/art-refs/villain/wyrmheart_ref_from_keyart.jpg.

## Must / must not
- Must: female, beautiful and calm, unmistakably royal, unmistakably dragon underneath. Red and gold. Same glow colour as the Heart.
- Owner (Nov 7): she has a very seductive figure (full hourglass) because she chose the most alluring human form to rule, but she is fully and formally dressed like a queen at court, seductive through presence, not exposure.
- Must not: monstrous or snarling in human form; skimpy armour; a copy of any existing game's dragon queen.
