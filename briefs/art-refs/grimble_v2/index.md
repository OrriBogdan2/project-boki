Model: Nano Banana Pro (no seed exposed).
Base: the original `briefs/art-refs/grimble_turnaround.png`. The old "Dredge" sheet is the correct character with its old name. Editing the original kept his face and build; fresh generations drifted.

## Locked turnaround (Nov 6, 19:35 ET)
`grimble_v2_turnaround.jpg`.

**Base design:** lanky, hunched grey brute; monocle; wide grin; hoard of chests, barrels, coins, bells and lanterns on his back.

**Right arm (bone hook):** huge bruised-purple mutated arm whose split forearm grows into a jagged bone hook. It replaces the iron hook and chain; in game, the arm itself hooks.

**Corruption:** purple veins spread from the arm over his shoulder, chest, back and neck, with bone spurs along the same path (shoulders, spine).

**Discord fixes:**
- scrap-metal teeth (nails, bolts) among rotten teeth;
- swollen purple gums;
- chewed ears with rings;
- purple-black sludge dripping and pooling.

**Explorations:** arm options A–E (bone hook, claw, tendril, junk graft, pincer). The anchor and pincer options were rejected because they overlap Breaker.
