## Style for all items
- Stylized painterly fantasy game concept art: bold readable shapes and a hand-painted look, matching the existing Boki and Hunter art.
- Red and gold are the villain's colours (the fallen kingdom of Solmere).
- Reference sheets: 16:9, plain light background, nothing cut off, multiple views in a row at the same size.
- No text.

## Story context
- Malgrave was an ancient Wyrm (a dragon). He ruled the kingdom of Solmere for 300 years, disguised as a tall, beautiful, ageless king wearing a red-and-gold mask.
- His mask was the first mask; every Hunter's Mask copies it.
- He sealed his soul in the Wyrmheart, a red crystal heart, and fed it the souls of his subjects.
- On the night of the Hollowing he was killed and the kingdom was flung into the sky. The cracked Heart fell into the bottomless pit below (the Hollow), still alive, with Malgrave inside.
- Anyone who holds the Heart becomes powerful but corrupted.
