# Garden balance: 30 simulated days (GARDEN_MODERN sections 2, 3, 4.4, 5)

`tools/garden_sim.py` (headless, no Godot) plays 30 garden days for each player in `content/rules/garden_sim.json`, 200 seeds each. `python3 tools/garden_sim.py --garden-sim-check` asserts its invariants (stats inside the 240 budget and the 100 cap, no run XP for home players, more garden XP at 30 minutes than at 5, deterministic) and that the tables below are current.

## What the sim stands on

- **Built or READY, read as data:** stat budget (care.json `stat_budget`, ext/garden-stat-budget 7d1acb8c6), item engine and families (garden_items.json, ext/garden-items c1efd93b1), prize tables, first-win items and cups (garden_prizes.json, ext/garden-prizes-cups 9f2fdb16f), path meter pips for Sporcupine (evo_paths.json, ext/garden-path-meter c1275d53a), Wayseed price (market.json, ext/garden-wayseed). This branch is on ext/garden-integrated (main has no base_life.gd), where those files are not yet merged, so the sim reads the copies in `garden_sim.json fallback` and switches to the live files by itself once they land.
- **Not built anywhere, simulated from the brief's numbers:** garden XP (2.5: 8 a game, 15 a win, 45 a first win, 3 a first care), today's wishes and away gifts (5, ranks 1 and 3), golden ripenings (1.1: 1 in 6).
- **Sim guesses** (marked `assume` in the JSON): minutes per action (a game 1.5, arrival 1, care 1, a wish 0.5), one toy play every 2.5 minutes present, win chance 0.6 against an even rival, 30 gems a day from home selling, a run = 10 minutes, 70 XP, 40 gems, a Wayseed 1 time in 4.
- **The players:** 5 minutes a day plus one run; 30 minutes plus two runs; home only at 30 minutes; and home only at 5 minutes (the hard case for the 5-minute flag). One tracked Boki gets every item (an upper bound; a real garden spreads them). "First evolution" = level 8, the first real form swap in evo_paths.json (Echolop); levels 4 (today's Evo.on_level form step) and 16 (the other finals) are shown too. Lean and trait pips are not modelled on the meter (no lean or trait drift in the sim), so the meter numbers count stat lead, tokens, ribbons and toy habit only.

## Findings

1. **Grindy at 5 minutes (flagged).** On garden XP alone a 5-minute player reaches level 8 on day 27 (home only: day 29). The 5-minute loop (arrive, three cares, two games) earns about 65 XP a day, and level 8 costs 2,800 XP on the 100 x level curve. With a run a day it is day 17. A home-raised Boki is the case section 2.5 exists for, so this is the number to fix.
2. **The budget is not trivial anywhere.** No player fills 240 inside 3 days. At 30 minutes it fills on day 10 (with runs) or 13 (home only); at 5 minutes it is near full (209 to 236) by day 30. Most points come from first-win items, wish Sunsaps and away training, not from repeat prize rolls (capping rolls at 2 or 3 wins a game a day moved the fill day by under a day, so it is not proposed).
3. **Repeat play pays like a run.** At 30 minutes the home player earns 304 garden XP a day against 210 for an expedition-only player with the same time: grinding one game (8 or 15 XP per 1.5 minutes) out-earns a run per minute. The brief wants "one game twenty times in a row gives little".
4. **Items per family** (30 days, one Boki): stat bites dominate (77 to 388; Sunsaps count here), path tokens 3 to 35, lean 3 to 27, looks 0 to 22 (the 5-minute player never wins a Crown, so looks and ribbons stay near zero), personality 2 to 18, respec 1 to 10 (the weekly trader and wishes). Nest items have no source in the built games yet.
5. **Path meter flips** 0.2 times a month for a player who plans (wears only Keepstones, skips Power bites) and ends on the chosen end 100% of runs; 1.4 to 2.1 times a month for one who feeds whatever comes. That is calm enough to read, and the planning player always gets what they aim for, so no change is proposed. The casual player lands on Ringrove only 2 to 18% of the time, because Cinderstep (Stump Shove: Thumpnut first-win) is the second zone and Power bites pile up: zone order quietly picks the form, which the meter at least shows.
6. **Zones for home players hang on gems.** The trader's Wayseed (300 gems a week) is the only way in without runs. At 30 home gems a day all four zones open by day 30; at 0 the 5-minute home player stays in Bramblebank (one game) for the whole month. The proposal still gets that player to level 8 on day 14 with one zone.

## Proposal (now the sim's own numbers; the live balance pass is in docs/GARDEN.md, Balance)

Garden XP gets its own rules file when backlog item 5 is built; the level curve moves out of game.gd `_after_match` (`100 * level`, twice) into the same file. Moving the curve changes expedition levelling too, so it needs a decision note (AGENTS.md 1).

```diff
--- /dev/null
+++ content/rules/garden_xp.json
+{
+	"play": 3,
+	"win": 5,
+	"first_win": 60,
+	"care_first": 5,
+	"care_kinds": 3,
+	"wish": 3,
+	"doc": "Garden XP (GARDEN_MODERN 2.5, balance in briefs/design/GARDEN_MODERN_BALANCE.md): repeat games pay little, the first win of the day in each game pays most, the first care of each kind 5.",
+	"level_curve": {"per_level": 50, "flat_from": 20},
+	"level_curve_doc": "XP from level n to n + 1: per_level x n, then per_level x flat_from (1,000) a level from level 20. Was 100 x n with no flat part."
+}
```

Against the brief's 2.5: a game 8 to 3, a win 15 to 5, a first win 45 to 60, a first care 3 to 5; Sunsap stays 40 (garden_items.json unchanged). Effect (tables above): the 5-minute garden-only first evolution comes on day 12 (home only: 14, right on the line); home 30-minute XP falls from 304 to 238 a day, close to the 210 of a runner with the same time; the budget still takes 9 to 11 days at 30 minutes and is not touched. Alternative kept open: leave the curve and raise first_win to 90; that alone gets the 5-minute player to day 18 (home only: 21), still over 14.

## The level 1 to 100 curve for home players

Today level 100 costs 495,000 XP. At its rate a 30-minute home player needs about 1,600 days, a 5-minute one about 8,000, and even a 30-minute runner over 1,000: level 100 is not a goal anyone reaches. Stat caps stop at level 15 and the last finals come at 16, so levels past about 20 only buy the number. My view: keep the steep part short and make the tail flat. With 50 x n up to 20 and 1,000 a level after (89,500 XP in total), a 30-minute home player gets to 100 in about a year (377 days) and a runner with two runs a day in about 200 days, and every level past 20 costs the same 4 to 5 days at 30 minutes, which reads as steady progress. Home and expedition players stay level with each other per minute played (238 vs 210 XP a day), so neither way of playing is the slow road.
