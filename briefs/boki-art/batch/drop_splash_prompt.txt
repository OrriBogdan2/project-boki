A dynamic fantasy game splash illustration, 4:3 landscape, 2400x1800.

SCENE
Three heroes ("Hunters") have just leapt from an airship and are diving
together toward a cluster of floating islands far below. The picture reads
top to bottom in three layers: the airship above, the squad in the middle,
the islands below. The camera is beside and slightly above the squad,
tilted down, so we see the ship they came from and where they are going
in the same frame.

THE AIRSHIP (top of the frame, partly cropped by the top edge)
The underside and open drop bay of a weathered fantasy airship, wood and
brass hull, warm lantern light spilling out of the bay the squad just
jumped from. A loose rope or two trailing in the wind. It sits behind and
above the squad and takes up no more than the top fifth of the image. It
is the backdrop, never the subject.

THE THREE HUNTERS (large, the clear subject, diving as one tight group
in a loose triangle, all facing down toward the islands)
1. LACE, lowest and closest to us, leading the dive. A pale porcelain
   doll woman with a stitched scar across her face and fine cracks in her
   skin, black hair in a bun pinned with two giant sewing needles,
   wine-red corset, ball-jointed doll arms, a cracked white mask strapped
   to her shoulder, red thread trailing behind her in the wind. She dives
   head first with a longbow in one hand, calm and smiling.
2. ALBERT, upper left, the tank. A huge knight in a rusted deep-sea
   diving suit covered in barnacles and seaweed, round helmet with glowing
   cyan eyes, a compass set in his chest, a small teddy bear tied to his
   belt. He drops feet first behind a big wooden shield, war hammer in
   the other hand.
3. VEX, upper right, the gunslinger. A young woman with green hair in a
   long braid whipping upward, golden eyes, freckles, lime-green jacket,
   one metal prosthetic arm. She is laughing mid-dive with a large rifle
   held one-handed.

THEIR BOKI (small creature companions, one per Hunter, clearly smaller
than the Hunters, diving right beside them)
- With Lace: a purple fox-like creature with a segmented scorpion tail
  tipped with a glowing green droplet.
- With Albert: a round hedgehog with teal mushrooms growing from its
  back, clinging to the top of his shield.
- With Vex: a pink and white striped axolotl with small crystals on its
  back, limbs spread, delighted.

THE WORLD BELOW (bottom third of the frame)
Broken floating islands connected by stone bridges, seen from high above
through gaps in dark clouds. Ruined castle walls, pine trees, patches of
snow on one island and glowing orange autumn ground on another. The
island directly under the squad is lit brighter than the rest, with a
faint red glow at its centre. Far off, one or two other airships with
tiny streaks of light falling from them show other squads dropping too.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Night sky in deep navy and indigo. Warm lantern light from the airship
on the Hunters' backs, teal and violet rim light on their edges, warm
orange light rising from the island below. Speed lines, torn cloud wisps
and a few embers rushing upward. Strong silhouettes so each character
reads at thumbnail size.

STYLE
Painterly, polished hero-shooter splash art, the same style as the
attached reference images: semi-realistic stylised characters, rich
saturated colour, soft brushwork, dramatic lighting. The Boki keep their
cute rounded cartoon look from the references.

COMPOSITION RULES
Keep all three Hunters and all three Boki inside the middle 80% of the
frame. Nothing important in the outer 10% on any side. The squad takes
up about 60% of the image. No giant creatures or bosses anywhere in the
picture. No text, no logo, no interface, no border, no watermark.

AVOID
Parachutes, jetpacks, wingsuits, modern aircraft. The Hunters standing on
the ship or still inside it. Modern or sci-fi clothing. Extra characters
near the squad. Daylight or blue sky. Flat poster or pixel styles. Extra
fingers or limbs. Any lettering.
