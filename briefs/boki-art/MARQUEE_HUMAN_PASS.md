# Marquee Boki: human paint-over list (V2)

23 Boki get a human pass: the 9 starters, the 11 legendaries with art, Gongrowl (owner flag), and 2 anchor-only pieces. They are the Boki players see first and longest (starter pick, boss banners, the site, store capsules), and a human-finished image is also the stronger position on authorship. Rules: STYLE_CANON_V2.md. Glacione joins when its set lands. Next if budget allows: Ermink (the mystery-egg hatch) and the three lair bosses Cairnback, Hivara, Zephyrak.

## What the artist does
- Paints over the best V2 render of the **card**, to the canon's numbers. Redraws the line by hand (this is where a hand shows most), designs the shade shapes, fixes construction, locks counts.
- Writes the Boki's **counts line** (legs, horns, crystals, stripes) and marks one intended quirk. The sheet and portrait are then re-rendered from the painted card and checked against it.
- Delivers a layered file (line, flats, shade, highlights), a flat PNG on #E8E8EE, and a process export (time-lapse or 4 or 5 stage saves). Keep these: they are the answer when someone asks whether the art is AI.
- My estimate, not a quote: 2 to 3 hours per card, 5 or more for the six with design problems (Gongrowl, Siegephant, Ringrove, Glaivyx, Wayshell, Sphinxblight).
- **Any store capsule, key art or trailer still should be composed and drawn by the artist from these cards, not generated.** Steam asks for disclosure of generated marketing art.

## Order
1. The six anchors (marked A): everything else is rendered against them.
2. Gongrowl and Siegephant after their redesign is approved (hold list, RERENDER_PROMPTS.md section 7).
3. The other starters, then the other legendaries.
