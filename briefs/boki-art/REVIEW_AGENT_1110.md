# Independent art review, 11-10

Reviewer: fresh-eyes agent. Scope: all 259 main cards (25 line pages), the 72 px icon sheet, the look-alike page, the Pokemon comparison page, the sheet-vs-card page, plus 42 sheets and 42 portraits opened directly. Nothing was edited.

Main finding: rule 1 is the roster's biggest problem. About one Boki in three is "animal plus a manufactured object" (anvil, vault door, telescope, bucket, zipper, snow globe, chimney, gold bands). These are not grown parts and no amount of re-rendering fixes them; the concepts need changing. Second finding: about a quarter of the sheets I opened have ghost copies. Third: three legendaries read as common babies and one is a person in a robe.

## 1. Top 20 problems (worst first)

1. **Palloth** (rule 2, 9). It is a hooded human figure in a fur robe with human hands and a mask face. Fix: redraw as a real moth body with a moth head, six insect legs, no robe or hood.
2. **Rollguin** (rules 6, 7, 8). It is the Pinguin drawing with the lower half painted as a glossy black ball; same pose, same face, same size. Fix: new drawing, different pose and facing, larger build, a new grown feature (for example a rolling belly posture and a short crest that leads to Kingpinguin).
3. **Latchling, Lockatara, Vaultara** (rule 1). Padlock shackle, steel key tail, a bank vault door with riveted straps; all are metal hardware. Fix: turn lock parts into horn/shell growths (keyhole-shaped scale pattern, key-toothed tail spines of bone) or retire the line.
4. **Bucketmander, Barrelmander, Cisternmander** (rules 1, 4, 8). An axolotl wearing a roped bucket, then a barrel, then a strapped stone cistern. Fix: make the vessel a grown shell (gourd or barnacle shape), no staves, hoops, rope or strap.
5. **Lensling, Scopeleon, Domeleon** (rule 1). A brass telescope replaces the eye and Domeleon wears a brass observatory helmet. Fix: a long tubular chameleon eye turret made of scales in body colour; the dome becomes a casque crest.
6. **Rampartoad, and Buckleron, Bulwartoad** (rules 1, 4, 8, 11). Two separate toad lines both carry fortifications (stone castle, riveted iron helmet/shell); they break rule 1 and duplicate each other. Fix: keep one toad-fort line, make the armour warty bone plates, delete or re-concept the other.
7. **Crofthorn, Crofthollow** (rules 4, 1). A literal cottage, dry-stone wall and tree sit on a highland cow; this is exactly what rule 4 forbids. Fix: drop the building; keep moss-and-heather fleece and vine-wrapped horns as the idea.
8. **Glomworth** (rules 3, 10). A dripping melted slime pile with a sagging face; multi-colour striped sludge reads as Alolan Muk. Fix: keep it a solid creature with the marbled-paint swirl as a tall crest, no puddle, no drips.
9. **Chainipede** (rules 1, 3, 6). The body is a literal black iron chain, green venom drips from the jaws, and nothing of green Clinkpede is left but the head. Fix: green interlocking segment plates that suggest links, no drips, keep Clinkpede's colour.
10. **Ermink family (Ermink + 9 finals)** (rules 10, 5, 6, 1). One cute mammal with many elemental branches is the Eevee idea; Flarmink, Voltmink, Rimink are element-only; Flarmink, Voltmink, Prismink look unrelated to Ermink; Lunink reads as Espeon. Fix: tie every branch to the ink-brush idea (ink, wash, gold leaf, blossom painting), keep the mink body and black tail tip in all, cut the element-only ones.
11. **Rimekit, Rimeshrew, Rimefang** (rules 1, 4, 5, 6). A glass snow globe with a village inside sits on three different animals (bear cub, shrew, wolf). Fix: one animal throughout; replace the globe with a grown clear ice blister with frost patterns, no buildings.
12. **Mortarfly** (rules 1, 2, 12). Brass organ pipes and gear-toothed wing edges are machinery, and its portrait and sheet give it a human mask face. Fix: wing veins shaped like pipes in chitin colour, moth face on all three assets.
13. **Canopony, Carrousteed** (rules 6, 7, 1, 10). Canopony is Foalaround with a longer tail in the same pose; Carrousteed has a built carousel canopy on a pole with lanterns; the cream-and-pastel unicorn is close to Galarian Ponyta/Rapidash. Fix: new pose for Canopony, canopy becomes a grown striped mane-frill, change palette away from cream and pink.
14. **Bathilus, Torpecone, Dreadmonite** (rules 1, 6, 10). Riveted steel plates, portholes and cannons; the middle stage is a one-eyed fish that is not a nautilus; Bathilus reads as Omanyte. Fix: shell with natural chamber ridges, no rivets or guns, keep the cephalopod body in all three.
15. **Sheets with ghost copies** (rule 12): Sloggoth, Glomwort, Tesselamb, Dreadnoyak, Stamproo, Siegephant, Hydrangor, Glaciammoth, Ghoulamari, Cindermaw, Calderoise. A faded second row of heads sits above the four views. Fix: regenerate or crop and repaint the top band; then check every sheet, since 11 of 42 sampled had it.
16. **Twinklup, Lockwren, Luckhop** (rule 9, 1). They read as common babies or starters, not legendaries; Lockwren has a padlock chest and Luckhop a gold coin tail. Fix: adult proportions, one strong rare feature each, a clearer floating halo, remove lock and coin.
17. **Zipling, Zippelith, Seamwyrm** (rules 1, 8). A metal zipper with a pull tab is sewn hardware; all three are glossy and Seamwyrm glows inside. Fix: a toothed seam of interlocking bone or scale, no slider, no pull tab, flat shading.
18. **Anvilbrock, Forgebrock** (rules 1, 2). A steel anvil sits on the back, then is strapped on with a metal harness over a furnace chest on an upright bear. Fix: an anvil-shaped horn plate grown from the shoulders, no straps, four-legged pose.
19. **Starcap, Medushroom** (rules 2, 7, 6). Human-like sleepy faces on a mushroom cap; all three stages are front-facing floats; Medushroom looks smaller and simpler than Starcap. Fix: jellyfish or slug face with no nose-and-lips reading, vary poses, make the final bigger with a new feature.
20. **Gilabacus, Pipewurm** (rule 3). Green drool or slime visibly drips from the mouth and nozzle. Fix: remove the drips; Pipewurm's metal nozzle also has to become a horn snout.

Close behind: Howlop, Chuggamus, Lodestaff, Tesselamb, Siegephant, Cetorgan, Barokit line, Shinobant, Pagoguana, Cairnback.

## 8. What I could not check

- Sheets and portraits for the other 208 Boki (217 minus the 9 extra sheets). Given 11 ghost sheets in 42, expect roughly 50 more across the roster; this is an estimate, not a count.
- Female forms: none opened.
- Hero cutouts: none opened.
- Full-resolution cards: I judged from the line pages (380 px per card). Outline weight, small gradients and tiny artefacts may be misjudged; rule 8 calls marked "borderline" are the least certain.
- Pokemon similarity is from memory plus the supplied comparison page; I did not look up any other references.
- Rule 5 for some lines depends on design intent I cannot see (for example whether Malletary or Tinbrock has an idea the art fails to show).
- Names and typing were not reviewed, except where a name clashes (Dragonkite) or mismatches the animal (Terrasloth).
- I did not check that the files in LIVE_MANIFEST.json are the ones shown in game.
