## Rules for every new design
1. One idea, fused into the anatomy. The object is a body part, not worn. Gear only for mounts (saddles).
2. Silhouette first: recognisable as a solid shadow.
3. Two main colours plus one accent.
4. The face carries the personality, and each species gets its own eyes and expression.
5. Shape language matches the role: round = support, blocky = tank, sharp = attacker.
6. The edge comes from natural weapons (teeth, claws, spikes, blade parts), never held items.
7. Render: soft painted shading with a thin clean outline, matching style_refs.
