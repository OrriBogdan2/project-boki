# Project Boki art rules (the owner's, collected 11-09 to 11-10). Score every Boki against these.
1. BORN-WITH PARTS (owner's reading, 11-10): an object GROWN INTO the body is fine (a shell shaped like a teapot, a tail that is a telescope, an anvil-shaped head, a snow-globe belly). Only fail parts that read as WORN, STRAPPED, BELTED, HELD, CARRIED or sitting loose on the creature (hats, gauntlets, ropes, saddles, a separate machine it rides or stands in).
2. ANIMAL FACE: an animal's face, not a person in a costume. Exception: a legendary may be somewhat humanoid if it still reads as a creature (Palloth is approved as is: humanoid, creepy).
3. NO GROSS: no dripping slime, drool or venom, except the Blight ink/slime line (Blotlet > Slog > Sloggoth).
4. NOT "ROCKS, TREES OR BUILDINGS ON A CREATURE" as the whole idea (the roster already has many; flag ones where that is all there is).
5. ONE IDEA PER LINE that visibly grows through the stages. An animal plus an element only ("ice wolf", "fire bird") is too basic.
6. EVOLUTION: stages of a line look related (same colours, face, signature part) AND each stage is clearly a step up, not the previous stage made bigger. A middle stage keeps one thing from the baby and shows one thing of the final.
7. POSES: stages of the same line must not share a pose or facing.
8. HOUSE STYLE ("V2 flat cel"): flat colour, one hard shade, bold even outline in a dark shade of the local colour (never thin, pale or black), no gradients/gloss/glow/sparkles, detail only on the face, the signature part and one pattern, plain pale grey backdrop.
9. LEGENDARIES (19) must look clearly legendary and carry a HALO CROWN: a ring of the creature's own material floating above its head. Cute legendaries are allowed but must still read as rare.
10. NOT A POKEMON: no design that reads as an existing Pokemon.
11. Reads at small size: recognisable and distinct from other Boki as a 72 px icon.
12. Sheets: exactly four views (front, three-quarter, side, back) in one row, same design and colours as the card, no ground line, no ghost copies. Portraits: same design as the card, ideally a different pose.
