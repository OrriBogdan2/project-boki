# Boki Batch 2 prompts, v2 (designer pass, Nov 8)
Nano Banana Pro, new generation. Image 1 is style_refs/boki_style_ref_2d.png. 16:9, 4K, 2 runs each.
Every prompt = the BLOCK below, then the creature paragraph.

BLOCK:
Creature-collector monster design for a fantasy action game: an endearing but dangerous creature built from one clear idea, a real animal fused with an object so that the object is part of its body. Simple bold shapes, a slightly oversized head, a strong iconic silhouette that reads even as a solid shadow, a memorable face with its own expression, and some bite: teeth, claws, spikes, or even real weapons like a sword, axe, spear or mace grown out of its body as if it was born with them, never held or strapped on. Clearly an animal: no clothes, no human posture, nothing held in its hands. Render it exactly like image 1: soft hand-painted shading with a clean, fairly thin dark outline, not thick cartoon outlines, not flat cel shading, not realistic. Do not copy image 1's creatures, faces or colours. Turnaround sheet: exactly four views in one row at the same scale (front, three-quarter, side, back), flat even light, plain pale grey background, whole body in frame, no floor, no shadow, no reflection, no text.

11 Bubblisk: A slim, quick lizard running on long back legs, with a tall rounded crest fan on its head that shimmers like soap film. Milky opal scales flash pink and green. Its long tail ends in a thick brass loop like a bubble wand, and its forelegs end in small needle claws. Face: big round wet-looking eyes and a cheeky, pointed grin, a prankster. Main colours: opal white and soft pink, with a brass accent. Avoid: floating bubbles, wings.
12 Anvilbrock: A low, broad honey badger whose whole back is a dark iron blacksmith's anvil grown out of its body, the pointed horn of the anvil jutting forward over its head like a ram, the flat face of the anvil glowing dull orange with heat. Black fur with a bold white stripe from crown to shoulders, thick legs with big digging claws. Face: small fierce eyes, a scrunched snarl showing sharp teeth, fearless and grumpy. Main colours: black and white, with iron grey and an orange glow accent. Avoid: a hammer, flames.
13 Skateron: A tall slate-grey heron with a white chest and an S-curved neck, whose long legs end in curved ice blades grown from the shins instead of feet, like built-in skates, and whose long straight beak is a dagger of clear blue ice. Swept-back frost plumes on its head, wings folded into a sharp wedge. Face: narrow, cool, half-lidded eyes, aloof and elegant. Main colours: slate grey and white, with an ice-blue accent. Avoid: boots, shoes, straps, standing on one leg, fish.
14 Barokat: A small sandy meerkat standing alert on its hind legs, its tail propping it up, with a round brass barometer dial grown into its round belly, the single needle glowing yellow. A ragged storm-cloud ruff circles its shoulders and crackles with tiny sparks, and its fingers end in short black hooked claws. Face: huge dark eye patches, wide twitchy eyes, an anxious, always-on-watch expression. Main colours: sandy tan and storm grey, with a brass and yellow accent. Avoid: clock numbers, lightning bolts in the air.
15 Rampartoad: A wide, squat, heavy toad whose back is a curved castle wall of grey stone blocks with a row of square battlements along the ridge, moss in the cracks. A broad flat head with a huge wide mouth and a small underbite of stubby tusks, fat warty legs. Face: sleepy, heavy-lidded amber eyes, stubborn and immovable. Main colours: slate grey and moss green, with an amber accent. Avoid: towers, flags, doors.
16 Terrasloth: A shaggy tan sloth whose back has grown into a small domed glasshouse of pale green panes in a brass frame, with pink orchids and moss inside and a soft mist inside the glass. Long arms ending in long curved hook claws, a short stub tail. Face: a round, sleepy, kindly face with dark eye stripes and a slow little smile. Main colours: warm tan and pale green glass, with a pink accent. Avoid: carrying anything, branches, flowers on the fur.
17 Pipewurm: A plump, soft, deep-violet velvet worm on many stubby leg nubs. Its head is a pleated piping bag shape ending in two tin star-shaped icing nozzles like a double snout, oozing a glossy toxic-green icing. Neat lilac icing swirls run along its back like ridges. Face: two small beady eyes above the nozzles and a smug, mischievous look. Main colours: deep violet and lilac, with a toxic-green accent. Avoid: cake, cherries, sprinkles.
18 Anchorbill: A heavy black hornbill with a cream chest and a huge pale curved bill, on top of which a rusted iron ship's anchor is fused as a crest, its flukes curving back over the head like horns and its ring at the front. Its short tail ends in three fat iron chain links. Face: deep-set, stern red eyes and a downturned bill, a brooding bruiser. Main colours: black and cream, with a rust-iron accent. Avoid: rope, a long chain, toucan colours.
19 Lanterloris: A small, round slow loris whose whole body is a ribbed paper lantern glowing warm yellow from inside, with short furry arms and legs sticking out and tiny needle claws. A cap of white moonflowers sits on its head. Face: enormous round amber eyes in dark rings, a tiny mouth, shy, wide-eyed and a little spooky. Main colours: cream and warm yellow, with a dark amber accent. Avoid: a candle, a string, fire.
20 Murmurling: A small, glossy black starling with a green-purple sheen and white speckles, whose tail is a fan of six reed pan-pipes of stepped lengths grown from its rump. A sharp yellow beak like a stiletto. Face: bright, sharp, beady white-ringed eyes, a cocky little trickster. Main colours: glossy black and reed tan, with a yellow accent. Avoid: other birds, thin pipes, musical notes.
21 Knotwyrm: A long legless serpent whose body is thick twisted rope, tied into three fat knots along its length, with a round brown mushroom cap growing from the top of each knot and a frayed rope tassel for a tail. Its head is a smooth bronze-scaled snake head with two curved fangs. Face: narrow, glowing teal slit eyes and a sly, patient smile. Main colours: hemp tan and brown, with a teal glow accent. Avoid: legs, loose threads.
22 Frigatee: A rotund grey-blue manatee whose whole back is a pale oak antique icebox grown into its body, with four brass-handled drawers along its sides, frost rime on the edges and a cold cyan glow leaking from the drawer gaps. Whiskered snout, two paddle flippers and a wide round tail paddle. Face: tiny, kind, droopy eyes and a big gentle whiskery smile, a slow guardian. Main colours: grey-blue and pale oak, with a cyan accent. Avoid: straps, legs, open drawers, water.
23 Buckleron: A giant limpet whose low cone-shaped shell is a dark iron round shield with a raised central boss spike and a ring of big rivets, crusted with lichen. Under the rim, a thick muscular foot and two stout eye stalks. Face: two round, wary eyes on the stalks, peeking out from under the shield, stubborn and suspicious. Main colours: dark iron and grey-green lichen, with an amber glow on the boss. Avoid: arms, legs, straps.
24 Spirevark: A big pink-tan aardvark with long ears, a long tube snout, strong digging claws and a thick tapering tail, with a tall ochre clay termite spire growing from the middle of its back, shaped like a cathedral tower with buttresses and an open arched belfry ledge near the top, tiny vent holes glowing amber. Face: small, calm, sleepy eyes and a patient expression. Main colours: pink-tan and ochre, with an amber accent. Avoid: a bell, flags, insects.
25 Barrelmander: A long, flat, wrinkly giant salamander, brown-black with storm-blue spots and frilly skin folds, whose back has grown into a fat oak rain barrel with black iron hoops, a brass tap low on one side and a short copper lightning rod on the lid crackling with yellow static. Broad flat tail. Face: tiny bead eyes and a huge wide frog grin full of small sharp teeth. Main colours: brown-black and oak, with a storm-blue accent. Avoid: ropes, straps, pouring water.
26 Prowyak: A bulky long-haired yak with a dark brown coat hanging to the ground, its lower coat frozen into blue-white ice sheets with icicle tips. Its horns sweep forward and fuse into one riveted black iron wedge like a ship's bow. A broad flat back with a simple saddle for a rider. Face: eyes hidden under a heavy fringe, steaming breath, a relentless, unstoppable look. Main colours: dark brown and ice white, with a black iron accent. Avoid: separate curved horns, snow, a rider.
27 Boltstrich: A long-legged ostrich whose round body plumage is a dark storm cloud flickering with small yellow lightning, a long pale pink neck wrapped in a coil of bright copper wire, and thick, glossy, yellow rubbery legs and feet grown from its body like insulation, with big dark talons. A simple saddle on its back. Face: wild, wide, staring eyes with tiny pupils and messy crackling head feathers, frantic and hyper. Main colours: storm grey and yellow, with a copper accent. Avoid: boots, shoes, clothing, rain, a rider.
28 Snuffapi: A graceful plum-brown okapi with large ears and a long neck, white stripes on its upper legs that fade into dark smoke at the hooves, and a brass candelabra growing from between its ears like antlers, with five short black candles burning with small violet flames. A simple saddle on its back. Face: soft, dark, mysterious eyes, calm and a little eerie. Main colours: plum brown and white, with a violet and brass accent. Avoid: wax drips, thin candelabra arms, a rider.
29 Crofthorn: A huge, shaggy ginger highland cow with a heavy fringe over its eyes and wide, sharp, upward-curved horns, carrying on its broad back a low oval dry-stone wall like a raised garden bed, filled with purple heather and thorny bramble, with a gap at the shoulders for a rider to sit. Face: eyes hidden under the fringe, a big wet nose, a calm but protective, don't-test-me stance. Main colours: ginger and grey stone, with a heather-purple accent. Avoid: trees, fences, a rider.
