## Owner art direction (Nov 8, 14:50 ET)
The Boki are a creature-collector roster. Each one is cute, unique and endearing, not realistic: a fantasy creature or a mix of a real animal with an object or element. Some are cuter and some tougher, but all must feel like the same universe and the same game style. Never name another franchise in prompts.

Style block for every Boki prompt:
"Creature-collector monster design: a cute, endearing fantasy creature that blends a real animal with an object or element, with simplified rounded shapes, a slightly oversized head, big readable features, a clear iconic silhouette and its own personality; stylised and toy-like, not realistic anatomy or realistic fur and feathers. It must look like it belongs in the same game as the creatures in image 1."

Too realistic, so redo with the style block: Gongibbon, Bellowary, Canvastross (and the Effigeetle and Umbrathere redos). They fit as they are: Zippelith, Mycroll, Chuggamus, Soakybara, Lockatara, Mirrolin.

## Owner art direction update (Nov 8, 14:51 ET): edgier
The game is more violent than a typical creature collector, so the Boki can be more aggressive and interesting: sharp teeth, claws, armour, and blades or weapons built into their bodies (blade tails, sword horns, spiked shells). They must not be too anthropomorphic, because some heroes are already humanoid creatures: no clothes, no standing or posing like a person, no holding weapons in their hands. Weapons are part of the creature or strapped on as gear, never wielded.

The style block now reads:
"Creature-collector monster design with an edge: an endearing but dangerous fantasy creature that blends a real animal with an object or element, with simplified bold shapes, a slightly oversized head, big readable features, a clear iconic silhouette and a strong personality, plus some bite: sharp teeth, claws, armour or blade-like parts grown into its body. Stylised and toy-like, not realistic anatomy or realistic fur and feathers. Clearly an animal, not a humanoid: no clothes, no human posing, no holding weapons in its hands. It must look like it belongs in the same game as the creatures in image 1."
