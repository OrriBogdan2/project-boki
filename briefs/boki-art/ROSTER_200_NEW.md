# Roster 200: 77 new species (draft for owner review, Nov 8)

Grows the roster from 124 (ROSTER_100.html, array `G`) to **201**. Design pass only: no art, no game data yet.

How this set was built:
- **Owner note (concept pass):** every species was brainstormed with at least three animal + idea mixes, and the most surprising one that still reads in one sentence was kept. Ideas pair far-apart worlds (games, sports, buildings, machines, food processes, household objects, occupations, myths), and obvious pairings were dropped: no ice drill on a narwhal, no battery on a Storm hamster, no plain fungus on a Spore elk or katydid, no glacier on the ice mammoth. Each entry has a **Concept** one-liner and the two **Rejected ideas**.
- **Rules followed:** BOKI_DESIGN_RULES.md (one idea grown through the whole body, shadow test, two colours plus an accent, own face, born-with weapon, clearly an animal on all fours), GRADES.md (uniqueness against the whole roster; every evolution a new body plan, only face, eyes and palette carry over), GAME_RULES_NOV8.md (1 or 2 types, 3 stages default, at most one special rule per line).
- **Shape:** 12 three-stage lines (one of them branches, so 37 species), 15 two-stage lines (30), 6 rares and 4 legendaries (single stage) = **77 species in 37 lines**.
- **Eggs:** **25 lines are egg-only** (hatched, never found in the wild). Each has a one-line egg look that hints at what is inside. The 7 found lines and Reelacine list where they turn up. Legendaries come from bosses and events, not eggs.
- **Special rules (kept rare):** 1 branching final (Gravigrub line, stat-picked like the existing four), 2 male/female lines (Grainspin line, Pavonazar), both based on real sex differences. No line has both.
- **Types:** weighted to the types the roster was thin on. New type slots: Normal 13, Dragon 11, Fairy 10, Psychic 10, Bug 10, Sound 10, Spore 10, Rock 9, Poison 9, Dark 7, Crystal 6, Steel 6, Ice 6, Electric 4, Water 2, Flying 2, Grass 2, Fire 0. Afterwards every type has 16 to 22 species except Water (33); before this pass the spread was 4 to 31.
- **Base animals:** none reuse a roster animal. Skipped on purpose: stoats, otters, ratites, sloths, lorises, toads, capybaras, axolotls, black corvids. Spread across 37 lines: insects and arachnids 7, sea and deep-sea life 9, reptiles 6, amphibians 2 (olm, dart frog), rodents 2, marsupials and monotremes 3 (wombat, thylacine, platypus), other mammals 2 (tapir, fruit bat), birds 2, extinct animals 4 (pterosaur, Irish elk, woolly mammoth, plus the ammonite forms), mythic 1 (hydra).

Every look line is written to drop into the standard sheet prompt (BOKI_50_DESIGNS.md shared rules: four-view turnaround, flat light, pale grey background, hand-painted style). Every species stands, crawls, swims or flies like an animal in every view; none stand or pose like a person.

# Three-stage lines

## 1. Lensling line: chameleon + brass telescope (egg-only)
**Egg:** brass-banded shell with one round glass lens window, as if something inside is peering out.
**Ability link:** spots and marks far targets; the final aims its telescope crest like a cannon.

### Lensling (1 of 3) · Fairy
- **Concept:** a baby chameleon curled up like a rolled star chart, peering through one brass lens eye.
- **Rejected ideas:** gecko + magnifying glass; chameleon + paint swatch book.
- **Mix:** chameleon + telescope lens
- **Look:** A bean-shaped baby chameleon lying low on all fours, its tail curled into a tight flat spiral like a rolled star chart. One eye is an oversized stubby brass lens tube, the other a small normal eye; brass ring bands repeat on its toes and tail tip. Pale silver crescent spots dot its back.
- **Face:** one huge squinting lens eye, one small eye half shut, mouth pursed in concentration.
- **Colours:** dusk blue and brass; accent silver moon spots.
- **Avoid:** held telescope, tripod, stars or sparkle particles, upright pose.
- **Archetype:** cute

### Scopeleon (2 of 3) · Fairy
- **Concept:** a chameleon whose swivelling eyes are two brass spyglasses.
- **Rejected ideas:** chameleon + periscope (too close to Torpecone); chameleon + kaleidoscope.
- **Mix:** chameleon + extending spyglass
- **Look:** A long, flat-sided branch-walker stretched forward on four clamp-shaped gripping feet, body narrow and leaf-thin. Both eyes are now long jointed brass telescope barrels that swivel separately, and the tail is a segmented sliding spyglass that extends and collapses. The lenses at the eye tips throw small rainbow glints.
- **Face:** two telescope eyes pointing different ways, a thin smug sideways grin.
- **Colours:** deep navy and brass; accent rainbow lens glints.
- **Avoid:** straps or harness, lenses floating, a copy of Lensling's spiral pose, upright.
- **Archetype:** sleek

### Domeleon (3 of 3) · Fairy, Crystal
- **Concept:** a giant chameleon that is a living observatory, its crest a great telescope.
- **Rejected ideas:** iguana + planetarium projector; chameleon + lighthouse (lamp clash with Lanterloris).
- **Mix:** chameleon + observatory telescope
- **Look:** A huge, heavy, low chameleon whose back and flanks are a riveted brass observatory dome split down the middle. Out of the split its casque has grown into one great cannon-like telescope barrel that aims forward over its head; legs are thick pillars ending in splayed three-toed clamp feet, and the tail coils into a heavy brass spiral on the ground. Silver crescent markings circle the dome.
- **Face:** ancient half-lidded stargazer stare, one eye magnified huge behind a lens.
- **Colours:** midnight blue and aged brass; accent silver moonlight on the dome.
- **Avoid:** windows, doors, a building on its back, stars floating, person-like stance.
- **Archetype:** majestic

## 4. Bathilus line: nautilus + submarine (egg-only)
**Egg:** riveted steel-grey shell with a small round porthole and a spiral weld seam.
**Ability link:** dives and surfaces: a bubble shield, then a torpedo ram, then a broadside.
**Fundamental changes:** the line evolves through deep time: coiled nautilus, straight-shelled orthocone, giant ammonite.

### Bathilus (1 of 3) · Psychic
- **Concept:** a nautilus that is a tiny bathysphere.
- **Rejected ideas:** nautilus + pocket watch; nautilus + spiral staircase.
- **Mix:** nautilus + bathysphere
- **Look:** A small round nautilus whose coiled shell is a riveted iron bathysphere with one round porthole where its eye peers out. A short skirt of fat stubby tentacles bobs beneath it. Rust-orange tiger stripes follow the coil.
- **Face:** one big round eye behind porthole glass, wide-eyed wonder.
- **Colours:** gunmetal grey and rust orange; accent pale porthole glow.
- **Avoid:** propeller, periscope, held objects, thin tentacles.
- **Archetype:** cute

### Torpecone (2 of 3) · Psychic
- **Concept:** a living torpedo whose seeker nose is Bathilus's porthole eye and whose arms have fused into its tail fins.
- **Rejected ideas:** orthocone + traffic cone; orthocone + space rocket.
- **Mix:** orthocone (extinct straight-shelled nautiloid) + torpedo (the eye is the nose, the arms are the fins)
- **Look:** A chubby, short, bullet-shaped straight shell racing forward level through a burst of foam. The rounded front is its face, with Bathilus's big round porthole eye set dead centre in the nose under a riveted brow ridge. The riveted gunmetal shell tapers back from there, and at the rear its arms have fused into four short, thick fin blades in an X like a torpedo's tail. Bathilus's rust tiger stripes run straight along the hull.
- **Face:** one big porthole eye narrowed under a stern brow, a small determined beak mouth just below it.
- **Colours:** gunmetal grey and rust orange; accent foam white.
- **Avoid:** loose, trailing or knotted tentacles, a coiled shell like Bathilus, rings around the hull, a long thin tube, upright.
- **Redesign (01:25 11-09):** the arms kept trailing, knotting or looping into a band, and the face sat behind the hull like a squid in a tube (6.9-8.5, never three seats together). With the eye as the nose and the arms as the fins, the creature is the torpedo with nothing loose, and the short chubby hull adds toy-like appeal.
- **Archetype:** fast

### Dreadmonite (3 of 3) · Psychic, Water
- **Concept:** a giant ammonite that is a battleship.
- **Rejected ideas:** ammonite + grandfather-clock gears; ammonite + carousel (wheel trap).
- **Mix:** giant ammonite + dreadnought battleship
- **Look:** A colossal ammonite crawling low on a mass of thick tentacle arms, its shell re-coiled into a huge ridged iron spiral with deck-plate ribs like a battleship hull. Two pairs of short cannon-barrel ribs jut from the spiral's sides, and a foam-crested wave curl breaks over its brow.
- **Face:** heavy-lidded admiral stare, stern down-turned mouth among the arms.
- **Colours:** dark iron and deep sea blue; accent foam white.
- **Avoid:** flags, deck buildings, a straight shell, floating parts.
- **Archetype:** majestic
