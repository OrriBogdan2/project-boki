# Boki wisps: art brief (owner decision 2026-11-09; full rules in DECISIONS.md)

**What changed.** In matches and expeditions, your Boki no longer walks beside you. It rides as a small glowing **wisp**: its soul, orbiting at shoulder height. When the Hunter presses **Summon** (long cooldown), the wisp bursts into the full-size Boki for a short timed window, then shrinks back into a wisp. At home and on the farm, nothing changes: Boki walk around full-size as now.

## What each Boki now needs (add to every sheet going forward)
1. **Wisp silhouette.** A small glowing critter shape built from that species' most recognisable bits (ears, horns, fins, tail tip). It must read as *that* Boki at thumbnail size, from above, in motion. Think "glowing cookie-cutter of its head and signature feature", not a shrunk model.
2. **Wisp colours.** Use the Boki's own palette, plus a shiny variant in its shiny colours. White-hot core, coloured edge, sparkle trail in the Boki's accent colour.
3. **Personality in motion.** Quick, bouncy and chirpy, in the Boki's own voice and character (a heavy Boki bobs slower, a spiky one jitters). Note this in a line on the sheet.
4. **Summon burst and recall.** One frame or note on how the wisp blooms into the full Boki and collapses back. It should be species-flavoured (petals, sparks, water, shadow...).
5. **Ready vs cooldown.** The wisp is dim while on cooldown and bright when the summon is ready.

## The one hard rule: round = Hunter, critter = Boki
- **Hunter Wisp** (a downed player): a ROUND person-shaped outline in team colour with a white core. Slow, heavy, low to the ground, steady glow. Never stealable.
- **Boki wisp:** a CRITTER outline in the Boki's own colours. Quick, bouncy, shoulder height, sparkle trail.
- **Never** draw a Hunter Wisp with critter features, and never draw a Boki wisp as a plain round orb. Players must tell them apart instantly.

## Gameplay states art has to show
- **Stolen wisp:** when a Hunter is downed, their wisps break loose and float, and anyone can grab them. A stolen wisp gets the **thief team's colour ring** plus a **tether line** to its carrier. Carriers must be visible targets. Stolen wisps go home to their owner at match end.
- **Guardian summon (likely):** if you go down without having summoned, your Boki bursts out by itself to protect you. Same burst visual, framed as protecting you.
- **Summon timer:** each Boki has its own summon length and cooldown (short and strong vs long and supportive is part of its identity). The HUD shows a ring timer, and the Field Book card shows the duration. Leave room on the card design.

## Cosmetics (design space, original designs only)
- Boki wisps get their own cosmetic line: trails, sparkle colours, orbit effects.
- Hunter Wisp cosmetics stay a separate line and must keep the round outline.
- No franchise look-alikes (Pokemon or any other IP).

## Not decided yet (don't lock art on these)
These are open in the wisp design pass (WISP_DESIGN_OPEN.md):
- how many summons at once (one by default, maybe more by dungeon tier)
- exactly what happens when a summoned Boki is killed (it turns back into a wisp, then either returns to its owner, revealing his location, or becomes stealable if he is down)
- stolen-wisp summons
- the ult's merged form

## Lore line (for flavour text)
A Hunter and their Boki are soul-linked. In the wilds the Boki travels as a soul-light riding with you, and takes form when called. When a Hunter falls, the bond loosens and the soul-light can be caught by another, but a linked soul always finds its way home.
