# Legendary tiers (proposal, owner idea 11-09 14:37 ET)

Owner: "different levels of legendaries" and "like 10 legendaries in the game to start, maybe a bit more".
Status: PROPOSAL. The art session wrote the art side; game rules (spawns, announcements, catching) are for the owner and main.

## The four tiers

| Tier | Where and how | How it should read in art |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Caught in the world, rare spawns, usually smaller | Normal Boki scale, one standout feature (a glow, a crest, an unusual material) |
| Boss | Lives in a boss area; identifiable at the start of a match | Huge, planted, armoured, arena-scale silhouette that reads from a match-start banner icon |
| Ultra | Rare mid-match spawn that players travel to fight and find | Colossal, with a presence around it (sky, weather, glow); something seen from far away |
| Blight | Blight-touched legendaries | The Blight look (PANEL_BRIEF "The Blight"): bruised purple-black sludge, purple veins, bone spurs, white-hot cracks. Purple-black is the game's one menacing colour, so this is the top danger tier |
