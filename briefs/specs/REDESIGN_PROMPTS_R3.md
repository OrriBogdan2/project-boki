## 1. Ostra, the Void Jester (male)
```
Redesign Ostra (Void Jester), male. Make 2 options.
LORE: Ostra was the Tyrant's court jester, the only person allowed to mock the king. He told one joke too many: the night the Tyrant sealed the Wardens' souls in masks, Ostra laughed, so the Tyrant sealed him in his own jester's mask. The mask kept smiling; the man behind it fell into the Void between the isles. Now he pulls people through that Void. Every time he swaps places with someone, he takes a little of their laughter. A ruff collar, layered violet and black ribbons that dissolve into the wisp, long spidery gloved fingers, playing-card-like shards of void orbiting him, one hand cradling a void orb. Lean forward, head tilted; the stillness should unsettle.
Avoid: green hair, a purple suit, painted scar-smiles, or any famous clown villain's features. Original design only.
DELIVER per option: splash 16:9 (floating over the abyss at night, pulling a victim through a void portal, bells ringing), portrait 1:1, turnaround front / 3-4 / back + a true SIDE profile (flat light, plain grey, the wisp hovering above the ground line), and a before/after.
```
