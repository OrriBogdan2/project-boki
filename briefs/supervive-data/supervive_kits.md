# SUPERVIVE hunter kits (read from in-game tooltips, offline server, Nov 2 2026)

Format: Passive / LMB / RMB / Shift (dash) / Air dash (if separate) / E / R. CD = cooldown, mana cost in ().
Upgrade tiers II/III/IV are per-ability level-ups (R has II/III only).

## Elluna (support/healer)
- Passive, Soulpack: can pick up and carry allied Wisps (downed allies). Taking too much damage or being stunned drops them, can't re-pick for 4s.
- LMB, Crescent Bolt: shot that deals more damage on each subsequent hit; restores mana on hit; after not firing briefly, next shot is Empowered.
- RMB, Darkside Binding (11.5s, 40): shot dealing 49 dmg on hit, then Roots target and nearby enemies after a delay. Upg: +40% dmg / +0.3s root / 200% detonation radius, -3s CD, +50% AP ratio on delayed hit.
- Shift, Lunar Dash (7s, 10): dash forward, further if airborne. Lunar Dash and Lunar Air Dash have separate cooldowns.
- Air Dash, Lunar Air Dash (14s, 25): same as above. Upg (shared): -0.75s (-1.5s air) CD x2 / air dash CD refreshed on picking up a wisp.
- E, Moonlight Blessing (12s, 46): healing zone 2.5s with burst at end; reactivate to throw zone to target location; zone attaches to any ally it passes through. Upg: +22.5% / +27.5% healing / -3s CD, healing increase after throwing doubled for 1s.
- R, (ult) (60s, 50) [title cut off in capture]: channel a massive heal on an ally or Wisp. If ally is knocked during channel, spell continues, reviving them and pulling them to Elluna on completion. Canceled if Elluna is stunned, knocked, or moves too far. Upg: +25% heal -5s CD x2.


## Brall (Juggernaut source)
- Passive, Blazing Spirit: deal damage to become Empowered. Heal when you knock an enemy or kill a minion.
- LMB, Caldera, Molten Greatsword: PRESS for a series of quick melee attacks, 43 dmg, center hits do more. HOLD to charge a ranged fireblast, 107 dmg.
- RMB, Adaptive Strike (2.5s, 15): context-sensitive.
  - Execution Slam (PRESS): 130 dmg in front; edge hits do more; bonus dmg to low-health targets; knocking an enemy resets Volcanic Rebuke (R).
  - Fiery Lunge (HOLD): charge and release a dash attack, 84 dmg.
  - Obsidian Slam (WHILE AIRBORNE): strike down, Slow + 88 dmg; center hits Stun and take more dmg.
  - Scorching Spin (DURING Feysteel Chain pull): spin attack 99 dmg + Anti-Heal.
  - Upg: +20% / +20% dmg / -3s Air Slam CD.
- Air Slam slot, Obsidian Slam (9s, 15): the airborne RMB variant has its own cooldown slot.
- Shift, Flame Step (13s, 10): dash attack in facing direction, 49 dmg; multiple charges; refunds mana on hit. Upg: +1 charge -2s charge time / -2s charge time / dodging shots builds empowerment, -1s charge time.
- E, Feysteel Chain (16s, 15): tether that attaches to enemies or walls, 30 dmg; reactivate to pull yourself to the target. Upg: -2s CD x3.
- R, Volcanic Rebuke (45s, 20): become Invulnerable, then strike back for 230 dmg and heal for a % of damage dealt. Killing a living enemy with it resets its CD. Upg: +30 dmg, +5% AP ratio, -5s CD x2.

## Celeste (ice mage)
- Passive, Shatter: damaging abilities apply Chill for 4s. If an enemy hunter is knocked or a minion killed while Chilled, they shatter after a short delay, 324 dmg to nearby enemies.
- LMB, Ice Darts: HOLD to fire a barrage of semi-homing darts; at end of burst, 3 larger darts (double dmg, faster, longer range). Hitting 8 darts on one target triggers Shatter (bonus AoE).
- RMB, Cryo Spear (12s, 12): icy spear, 156 dmg small AoE + Slow; after a short delay detonates for 190 more. 2 charges. Upg: +15% dmg -1.75s CD x2 / +45% explosion dmg.
- Shift, Skate (13s, 15): HOLD to skate, release to stop. Upg: -1.5s x2 / creates ice islands when dashing over Abyss.
- E, Frozen Barricade (20s, 30): wall that pushes enemies and blocks projectiles; recast to freeze it in place. Upg: -2.5s x2 / +1000 wall HP.
- R, Chilling Finale (80s, 50): become Invulnerable and create a Slowing zone that periodically applies Shatter stacks for up to 3.5s; then explode for 155 dmg + Stun in radius. Reactivate early to cancel. Upg: +40% dmg -10s CD / -10s CD.

- RMB, Embry Bolt (9s, 15): fire bolt 106 dmg + Slow; applies Ignite or upgrades to Level 2; bonus dmg vs Ignited. Upg: +25% dmg -1.5s x2 / +1 charge.

- E, Heat Shield (13s, 15): spin to Pull in all nearby enemies, 76.8 dmg + Anti-Heal; shield per enemy hit; builds Ignite; knocking an enemy resets its CD. Upg: +25% dmg/+25% shield/-2s x2 / +30% shield.
- R, Flight of the Firefox (45s, 25): fly forward, leaving burning ground: 239.2 dmg/s + Slow inside; applies Ignite. Upg: -15s CD / +25% dmg.

## Practice-mode / system notes
- Hunter level raised via training menu "Adjust Hunter Level" (each click +1). Ability ranks are bought with Left Alt + ability key (Alt+E, Alt+R, Alt+Shift); the HUD shows "Ability upgrades available - N".

- Bramble hedges on the west island apply SLOWED while you're in them.

- Abyss (round 3): after walking off without gliding, a red mesh/net appears under the hunter plus a red marker pin above, and death follows within about a second of that showing. With the glider opened right at the edge, she glided back north and landed on the ring (recovery works if the glider opens before the red net appears).
