## How Masks work
- The Wyrm stole everyone's selves. That's why the player character, the Unmasked, is blank.
- The fallen Wardens' selves were sealed into the one object each Warden was most bound to: their **relic**. Only some relics are literal masks; others are weapons, keepsakes or tools.
- People call them all **Masks** because of what they do. When you take one up, that Warden's face, body and memories settle over you like a mask, so you wear the Warden (in a match you look about 90% like them). The relic is the key, and the transformation is the Mask.
- The put-on beat: you touch the relic, a ghostly porcelain face flickers over yours, then the Warden's form settles in. This works even when the relic is a compass.
- The relic is always visible on the hero as their signature prop.
- The more you wear a Mask, the more its Warden seeps into you, even at home (the "masks take hold" idea, kept mask features).
- **Getting Masks:** you unlock a Mask through challenges or rewards first, then buy it. Vex is free at the first portal, and the second Mask is one of Zedran, Hem or Cinder. The shop and economy pass set the prices.

## Warden stories (current designs)
- **Vex, Hired Gun:** a foundry kid who swept brass casings off the Tyrant's war-foundry floors. A siege engine took her arm in the Sundering; she bolted its rotary cannon to her own shoulder. She's now the Skylands' most famous gun-for-hire, keeps every bounty she cashed, and adds a tally mark for each one.
- **Zedran, Arena King:** the Tyrant's undefeated champion, a beast-blooded slave chained to the arena floor for a hundred fights. In the hundredth he tore his shackle out of the wall, and the chain is his weapon. The Blight burned the magma cracks into him. The eye in his cleaver belongs to the executioner who came for him, and it blinks when he scores a kill.
- **Hem, Dark Archer (was Pin):** the Tyrant's royal mask-stitcher, who sewed the Wardens' selves into their Masks. She deserted and now hunts the Tyrant's loyalists, leaving a red-threaded needle at every kill. Her own shadow came loose when she stitched her first Mask; it's only a wrong-looking shadow, not a pet.
- **Breaker, Frozen Bulwark (was Bjorn):** a ferryman who carried refugees across the ice the night of the Sundering and went down with his ship so they could reach shore. The frozen sea remade him into a sea-ogre. He doesn't remember his old name, only the children's faces. The teddy bear belonged to the last child he saved.
- **Ryzz, Storm Slayer (was Vireo):** a street kid struck by the Wyrm's storm in the market square. The bolt took her right eye (a lightning scar) and left the storm inside her. She steals storms, catches lightning in jars, and swears she'll give the first bolt back to the Wyrm.
- **Cinder, Mad Pyromancer (was Linwick):** the Tyrant's court alchemist, ordered to distil the Wyrm's blood into fire. She drank a vial instead; it made her fireproof and a little mad. She brews "bottled Blight" with doodled-skull labels.
- **Dredge, Broken Abomination:** a broken diving machine haunted by whatever drowned inside it; it hooks the living down to where it was lost.
- **Pip, Fox Cub (was Moosh Moosh / Burple):** a fox-kit who tended the hearth of a Boki shelter. The hearth's fire lives in her lantern now, and she heals with it.
- **Claire, Icy Dancer (was Tamsin):** the star show-skater of the winter canal festival. The night the ice broke she skated the crowd to safety, and the frost took her: a frost demoness with a sweet face and a demonic body.
- **Tarek, Rock Elementalist (was Breah):** a blind cliff-runner who sees through stone; his three floating seeing-stones are his eyes.
- **Ostra, Void Jester:** the Tyrant's court jester. He laughed the night the Wardens were sealed, so the Tyrant sealed him in his own jester mask. The man behind it fell into the Void; he steals his victims' laughter, and his bells ring only when he swaps.
- **Sable, Silver Thief (was Dancer):** the thief who reached the Wyrmheart in the hoard. It woke the Wyrm and set off the Sundering.
