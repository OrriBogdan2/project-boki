## The premise (owner's words, Nov 4; this is the voice for all lore text)
> The Wyrm came, and stole our essence. In time, humans, beasts and skyfolk alike were born without faces. We became a new race: **the Empty**.
> Our only salvation became the cursed fossils we once tried to destroy: **Masks**, each carrying the soul of a fallen Hunter of old.
- Masks are old, cursed relics dug from the ruins. People feared them and tried to destroy them; now they're the only way to have a self again. (Where they came from: the Hunters who fought the Wyrm were sealed into their relics when they fell. Lace stitched many of them for Malgrave.)
- Not every Mask has a face: a relic can be a compass, a jar, a pair of skates. Wearing it gives you that Hunter's face, form and memories.

## How Masks work
- **One villain (owner, Nov 4):** Malgrave, the king who ruled Solmere and never aged, IS the Wyrm in human shape. When the thief woke it, the Wyrm broke the land and tore away everyone's selves.
- The Wyrm stole everyone's selves. That's why the people (the Empty, and you) are blank.
- The fallen Hunters' selves were sealed into the one object each Hunter was most bound to: their **relic**. Only some relics are literal masks; others are weapons, keepsakes or tools.
- People call them all **Masks** because of what they do. When you take one up, that Hunter's face, body and memories settle over you like a mask, so you wear the Hunter (in a match you look about 90% like them). The relic is the key, and the transformation is the Mask.
- The put-on beat: you touch the relic, a ghostly porcelain face flickers over yours, then the Hunter's form settles in. This works even when the relic is a compass.
- The relic is always visible on the hero as their signature prop.
- The more you wear a Mask, the more its Hunter seeps into you, even at home (the "masks take hold" idea, kept mask features).
- **Getting Masks:** you unlock a Mask through challenges or rewards first, then buy it. Vex is free at the first portal, and after the tutorial the second Mask is a pick of Kael, Cinder or Meeko, in that order (Lace is an early challenge unlock). The shop and economy pass set the prices.

## Hunter stories (current designs, Nov 7; canonical order)
**The Street Crew**
- **Vex, the Hired Gun:** a foundry kid who swept brass casings off Malgrave's war-foundry floors. A siege engine took her arm in the Hollowing, and the gatling she bolted to her shoulder is her right arm now. She's the Skylands' most famous gun-for-hire and keeps a tally mark for every bounty she cashed. Her loyalty is technically for sale, but she sticks with the crew. Starter Mask: the first Hunter players meet.
- **Sparkle, the Storm Chaser (was Ryzz):** a street kid struck by the Wyrm's storm in the market square. The bolt took her right eye (a lightning scar) and left the storm inside her. Her lightning burns hot pink. She climbs the highest spires to chase storms, throws lightning straight from her hands, and swears she'll give the Wyrm's bolt back to it someday.
- **Tenzo, the Stone Seer (was Tarek):** a young cliff-runner, blind since birth, who ran messages along the cliff paths by the feel of the rock. When the Hollowing cracked the cliffs, the stone started answering him: three seeing-stones float around him and are his eyes. He isn't fully grown yet. He's the youngest of the crew and the calm one, who hears trouble through the ground before Vex sees it.

**The Fallen Court**
- **Kael, the Unbroken:** Malgrave's undefeated arena champion, a slave chained to the arena floor for a hundred fights. In the hundredth he tore his shackle out of the wall, and the chain became his weapon. The Blight burned white-hot cracks into his sword arm (the Blight Cinder distilled, so he doesn't fully trust her). A demon lives in his blade and in his mind, and it must be fed: he kills out of necessity, not joy. Grim, cold, never cocky; the scarf never comes off.
- **Cinder, the Flame Queen:** Malgrave's court alchemist, ordered to distil the Wyrm's blood into fire. She drank it instead. Now fire answers her bare hands, and she no longer needs a tank, a flame-gun or goggles. She served the throne for years, and she wants it for herself. She is not mad, just certain.
- **Lace, the Red Stitch (was Hem):** Malgrave's royal mask-stitcher, who sewed the Hunters' selves into their Masks. She deserted and now hunts Malgrave's loyalists, leaving a red-threaded needle at every kill. Her own shadow came loose when she stitched her first Mask; it's only a wrong-looking shadow, not a pet.

**The Protectors**
- **Mossy, the Barnacle Knight (was Breaker):** a ferryman who carried refugees across the ice the night of the Hollowing and sank his own ship so they could reach shore. The sea remade him: his hull became his armour, his brass diving helmet never comes off, and the ship's compass is set in his chest, with the sea's cyan light glowing between the hull planks. He doesn't remember his old name, only the children's faces. The teddy bear belonged to the last child he saved.
- **Neve, the Snowglide (was Claire):** the star show-skater of the winter canal festival. The night the ice broke she skated the festival crowd to safety, and the frost took her. She glides on the skates that saved them and fights with a candy-cane spear from the festival.
- **Meeko, the Dawnfox (was Pip):** a fox-kit who kept the hearth of a Boki shelter alive through the night everything broke. The fire lives in her hearthstone now, and it grew into a tiny sun that follows her; a shy raincloud, the storm that tried to drown the hearth, came along too. She heals through the two of them.

**The Thieves**
- **Sable, the Silver Thief:** the thief who reached the Wyrmheart in Malgrave's vault. Touching it woke the Wyrm and set off the Hollowing. Everyone has a reason to hate her, and she knows it. She's still after the treasure.
- **Grimble, the Scrap King (was Dredge):** a scavenger who crawled into Malgrave's vault after it woke and never came out poor again. The Blight sludge seeping through the vault mutated his right arm into a bruised-purple bone hook and spread purple veins up his chest. One eye is stitched shut; the other squints through a brass monocle at whatever he's pricing. His teeth are scrap metal, with one gold tooth. He carries his hoard on his back (chests, coins, bells, lanterns) and calls himself king of whatever's left.

**The Solo Demon**
- **Motley, the Void Jester (was Ostra):** Malgrave's court jester. He laughed the night the Hunters were sealed, so Malgrave sealed him in his own jester mask. The man behind it fell into the Void and came back as a jack-in-the-box: a jester on a brass spring, rising from a hovering card box. He steals his victims' laughter, his bells ring only when he swaps, and he belongs to no one.

## Terms (owner, Nov 4; replaces the earlier list)
- **The Empty:** the people the Wyrm emptied, and you at home without a Mask (this replaces "the Unmasked" in player text; the code may keep `unmasked` ids).
- **Hunter:** what you become when you put on a Mask, and every player in a match. The great figures sealed in the Masks are the **fallen Hunters** (the old Hunters).
- **Mask:** the hero you pick and unlock ("Choose your first mask", the Masks tab, unlocks and shop).
- **Malgrave / the Wyrm:** the same villain. **Wyrmheart:** the wish. **Blight:** the Wyrm's blood.
- **Fusion** (key: Fuse): a Hunter merged with their Boki.
- Retired in player text: **Warden**, **Mantle**, **Bearer**, **Merge**, **Unmasked**. Internal ids and code names (warden_*, kin, unmasked) can stay.
