# Audio: sound sets, loudness and buses

How the game's sound effects are made, named, levelled and played. Music and ambience are in `tools/compose.py`, `tools/gen_ambience.py` and `tools/gen_garden_ambience.py`.

## Making sounds
- Sounds are **synthesised in code** (numpy/scipy, `pip install numpy scipy`), one script per group: `tools/gen_sfx_<group>.py` (e.g. `gen_sfx_vex.py`, `gen_sfx_boki.py`, `gen_sfx_ui.py`). Each one imports `tools/sfx_lib.py`, calls `S.reseed(<its own seed>)` and writes `audio/<name>.wav` (44.1 kHz mono 16-bit). Then `godot --headless --import`.
- `sfx_lib.py` re-uses the primitives in `tools/sfx_combat.py` (filters, envelopes, kick, click, crack, sub, tail, reflections, ring, grains) and adds `voice()` (wordless grunts and cries through vowel formants), `chime()`, `creak()`, `fm()`, `sweep()`, `rattle()`, `hiss()`.
- Exception: the ElevenLabs pilot (`tools/gen_sfx_el.py`, docs/SFX_PILOT.md) generates some kit sounds through the API under the owner's plan.
- Outside sources only if **CC0**, logged in `docs/CREDITS.md` under the group's own section (source URL, author, licence, which file). No sounds from SUPERVIVE or any other game.
- Review: `python3 tools/sfx_look.py out.png audio/<names>*.wav` draws waveform + spectrogram + levels per sound (matplotlib). Use it to judge layering, clicks and harshness, and as the READY screenshot.
- Held sounds (a minigun spin, a charge hum): write them with `S.save_loop(name, x, cat)`, which cross-fades the tail into the head and skips the end fades, list them under `"loops"`, and play them with `Sfx.loop_start` / `loop_stop`.
- Variety: write takes with `S.takes_cat(name, fn, 3, cat)` (`name.wav` + `name_1..3.wav`); `Sfx` picks a different take each time.

## Loudness
Every sound is levelled to its **category** by `S.save_cat` / `S.takes_cat` (table: `content/rules/sfx_levels.json`): RMS over the audible part, peak ceiling -1 dBFS. The `vol_db` at the call site then only says intent (a quiet tell, a big ult), never fixes a file that is too loud.

| category | RMS dBFS | for |
|---|---|---|
| heavy_impact | -14.5 | a damage carry's hits (Vex, Kael, Grimble's blunderbuss, Mossy's hammer) |
| heavy_fire | -15.5 | a damage carry's weapon: a little louder than the lighter Hunters |
| impact | -16 | an ability or weapon landing |
| fire, signature | -17 | a shot or swing; a Hunter's recognisable sound |
| cast, grunt | -18 | an ability starting; hurt / downed / death voice |
| boki | -19 | Boki voices and moves |
| dash | -20 | |
| loop | -21 | held sounds |
| ui | -22 | menus and HUD |
| step | -23 | footsteps |

## Monsters
`content/sfx/mobs.json` (`tools/gen_sfx_mobs.py`): `mdie_<kind>` is each voiced kind's death in its `v_<kind>` family (bosses use the Watcher's, pitched by body; a Shrubling keeps `die_small`), `mstag_open` ticks when a stagger window opens and `mstag_hit` lands the stagger. `MobSfx` (scripts/match/mob_sfx.gd) plays them at the monster and caps a pack dying at once to 3 death cues per 200 ms across kinds. `--mob-sfx-check` asserts the kind -> cue map and the cap.

## Mix pass: loudness across all cues and ducking
The files above are levelled by RMS at generation; cues added one branch at a time still drifted (a 12 dB spread inside "move"). `python3 tools/loudness_report.py` measures every cue in `audio/` (ITU-R BS.1770 integrated loudness, 400 ms gated blocks, K-weighted; sample peak; takes as one cue; standard library only, about 2 s) and groups them: weapon, ability, impact, boki, voice, move, ui, ambience. Targets, tolerance (+-2 LU) and the name patterns per category are in `content/rules/sfx_mix.json`. An outlier is brought in **through the cue table** `content/rules/sfx_cues.json` (dB added in `Sfx.play` and `loop_start`; files are untouched), at most 12 dB down / 3 dB up, boosts never past -1 dBFS peak. Ambience beds are measured but not trimmed (they play at -14 dB in `play_ambience`).
- `--write` rebuilds the table, `--check` fails when it is stale (run after adding or regenerating a sound), `--outliers` lists the cues outside tolerance, `--md` prints the table below, `--tails NAME..` gives a cue's length, end level and decay time.
- What is left outside tolerance after the table is peak-limited (a sound whose file peak is already -1 dBFS but whose body is quiet, e.g. `jug_meteor`, `vv_sky_strike`) or capped; fixing those means regenerating the sound with more body.

Ducking: `Sfx.duck` now dips the Music **and** Ambience buses (a bus effect, so it works for one track, the stems and the beds). A rival's ult (wind-up, cast, or an X ult; `MusicDir._ult_duck`) within 45 m dips them 9 dB, a knock call within 45 m (`Juice.knock`) 6 dB; both ease in in 0.12 s, hold 0.7 s / 0.35 s and come back in 1.0 s (`duck` in `sfx_mix.json`). Your own knock keeps its dip in `Match.knock`. `--match-audio-check` fires them and measures the buses.

## Playing and buses
- **Positional one-shots pan.** With a position and a 3D camera, `Sfx` plays them from an `AudioStreamPlayer3D` placed at the sound, so you hear which side it is on; the level is still `Sfx`'s own falloff (Godot's attenuation is off, `Sfx.PAN` sets how hard it pans). UI sounds (no position) and keyed loops stay unpanned.
- Always through `Sfx.play(name, pos, vol_db)` / `m.sfx(...)` (one-shots, pooled, distance falloff, far sounds muffled) or `Sfx.loop_start(key, name, pos)` / `loop_set` / `loop_stop` (held sounds). Never a new `AudioStreamPlayer`.
- They play on the **SFX** bus (compressor, a little room, limiter; far ones via **SFXFar**; ones behind terrain via **SFXMuffled**, a 700 Hz low-pass and -4 dB: `SfxOcclusion` runs one heightmap line test per positional sound, cached 100 ms, never within 3.5 m so your own Hunter and UI sounds stay clean; `--sfx-occlusion-check`). A match's sounds are torn down with the match (item 103, `Sfx.reset_scene_audio` stops keyed loops and pool voices on the match leaving the tree), so every held sound must be a keyed loop and stopped by its owner too.

- **Voice cap** (`content/rules/sfx_voices.json`, `SfxVoices`): a 30-player expedition is ~60 animated characters, so the pool has a per-bus budget: at most `cap.SFX` (12), `cap.SFXFar` (4) and `cap.SFXMuffled` (4, behind terrain) one-shot voices at once. ## Held fire (`scripts/core/fire_sfx.gd`)
A weapon under 0.15 s a round (`FireSfx.HELD_RATE`: the Quill Rifle, rifle, SMG, minigun, flamethrower's weapon row, and Neve's darts once they run that fast) plays its shot through `FireSfx.shot`, not one sample per round. At 0.08 s (about 12 rounds a second) the old path stacked 4 to 10 pool voices per Hunter.
- **First shot of a burst** (a gap over 0.3 s): the base take, no pitch jitter, full level. **The rest**: the other three takes in turn (never the same twice running), pitch +-3%, volume +-1 dB, `Sfx.STACK_DB` under the first, which is the level the stacking rule gave them already.
- **Cap**: one emitter keeps at most 3 takes sounding (`FireSfx.VOICES`); a round that would be the 4th sounds nothing (the shot itself still fires). Four Hunters holding fire cost 12 pool voices, the SFX bus budget of `ext/sfx-voice-cap`. Neve's heavy darts and skating beams, and Cinder's hose (its roar already plays one breath a 0.4 s), keep their own path.

## Leaving a place: `--music-leave-check`
Music and ambience follow the screen: after each change only the new place's score is heard. The check walks Training Grounds -> duel room -> farm -> title, a match -> title, and the farm -> title, and reads every Sfx player on the Music / Ambience buses (playing and above -45 dB). What each row protects:
- **training: the layered score plays** / **match: layers and ambience beds are up**: the setup is sane (stems and abyss / ruins beds really come up), so the leave rows below are not passing on silence.
- **duel: a duel match is up** / **duel: the score is the Training Grounds's, one set of stems**: walking into the duel room keeps one set of layered stems, not a second set stacked on the first.
- **leave duel: no stem of the match score is left**: `Sfx.reset_scene_audio` stops the layered score when a match ends.
- **leave duel: only the farm's own music + ambience**: the farm starts its own track (`home*`, or a zone's) after a match.
- **leave duel: no match ambience beds**: the abyss / ruins beds `MusicDir` sets are cleared on leave, not left looping.
- **leave duel: nothing stray on the Music bus**: no player that Sfx no longer points at (an orphaned crossfade or sting) keeps playing.
- **match -> title: the score fades out rather than cutting**: the leave is a fade; right after the change the old score is still ringing out.
- **X -> title: music is the title track / no match layers, no amb layers / exactly one music player is audible** (after the duel, a match and the farm): the title never inherits a stem, a bed or a second music player from where you were.
- **all 18 rows ran**: a step that errors out (a script error ends that coroutine) can't skip its rows and still exit 0.

The check opens screens the way the game does (`Game.goto`). With a display (xvfb too) the old screen stays current until the 0.3 s ink fade has covered it, so it waits for a new current screen rather than a fixed frame count. Settles run at `Engine.time_scale` 4: the fades are game-time tweens.

## The closing zone (`scripts/match/zone_sfx.gd`, `tools/gen_sfx_zone.py`, set `content/sfx/zone.json`)
`ShrinkZone.audio` (`ZoneSfx`) works from the match clock and this game's own player, so every game hears its own and nothing goes over Net. The zone's wall is a cold blue band, so its sounds are electric and glassy: a tonal buzz, not the Blight tide's dark rumble. `zone_warn`: one 1.5 s sting before each close (with the feed line and the kin, `warn_s` = 20 s ahead): the wall's buzz charging up under two cold pings a fourth apart, flat, score ducked 1.5 s, -6 dB. It is one sting, never a countdown; the Ways Out ticks (`HeartSfx`) stay the only countdown. `zone_hum`: a held loop (`Sfx.loop_hold`) on the edge's nearest point to you, carrying 60 m, from either side of the wall; `Sfx`'s distance roll-off makes it grow as you near it (-8.6 dB at 12 m, -25 dB at 50 m). `zone_cross`: a whoosh, zap and soft thump as you pass the edge, flat, at 0.82 pitch going out and 1.15 coming back in; you have to be 0.6 m past the edge before it counts, so standing on it stays quiet. `zone_tick`: a 0.3 s sizzle and electric bite each time the zone's damage lands on you (4 takes). The damage carries `"tick": true` (no flinch, no hitstop, a small ember) and `Match.on_damage` plays no hurt voice for `src` `"zone"`, so a zone tick never sounds like a hit taken. None of these has words, so a solo hears nothing about a squad. `--shrink-zone-check` asserts one warning per close at `warn_s` and no other cue while you stand inside, the hum on the wall and louder near it, one crossing cue each way and none on the edge, and a tick with no hurt voice for zone damage against a hurt voice with no tick for a hit. `--sfx-set-check` checks the files, levels and the seamless loop.
- `scripts/match/heart_sfx.gd` (`Heart.audio`) owns the Heartspark cues (`tools/gen_sfx_heart.py`, set `content/sfx/heart.json`) and the Ways Out calls: `call_out(event, seat, team)` with `open`, `start`, `taken`, `warn`, `last` plays one flat UI sting (`ways_*`) and writes the same line to the match feed and the toast lane, so sound and banner fire together. Each call plays once per seat (a squad's `start` again after 10 s); the quest seats call it the same way when they are built. The countdown: `ways_tick` once a second over the last 10 s before the Sealed Lair or the Uplink shuts or the islands fall (the Heart's final seat), the last 3 louder and higher, all under the calls (-12 / -6 dB against -2). Each game ticks for its own player only and only while the seat is still reachable for them: still in the match, the seat not taken or shut, no rival on it (your own Uplink charge still ticks; a lair fight you are sealed in does not, the clock leaves it be). `--ways-out-sfx-check` asserts it.
- The quest seats have cues of their own: `scripts/match/quest_sfx.gd` (`QuestSeats.audio`, `tools/gen_sfx_quest.py`, set `content/sfx/quest.json`, cool blue-violet glass so it never reads as the Heart) watches the Sealed Lair's and the Uplink's states each tick, so the host and a client mirroring `["xc", "seat", ...]` hear the same for their own squad. `quest_take` when your squad takes on a seat (a takeover too); `quest_rival_rise`, a tense rising flutter, when a rival squad passes 75 % of its seat (`QuestSeat.progress`: the uplink's charge or the health off the lair's boss) while your squad is on a quest (holds a seat, or stands at that seat contesting it), again only after it falls under 50 %; `quest_win` when your squad takes a seat; `quest_lose` when a seat your squad was on goes to another squad or your hold ends without a take (the boss fight lost, the uplink drained or taken over, the window shut). The stings are flat UI one-shots at -2 dB, one per seat change; `quest_site` is a quiet positional loop (-8 dB, carries 30 m) at each seat while it is open or in use, a keyed `loop_hold`, so at most two quest loops play and they end on their own. `--ways-out-sfx-check` section 5 asserts every cue once, the loop on and off, the host's own moves and a client's mirror.
## The Blight tide (`scripts/match/tide_sfx.gd`, `tools/gen_sfx_tide.py`, set `content/sfx/tide.json`)
The tide ring closing on the Heart decides who gets out, so it is heard before it is seen. `TideSfx` is ticked by `HeartSfx.update` and reads only `Heart.tide_stage` and `Heart.tide_r`, so a client hears what the host does (no Net change). `tide_onset`: a 3 s rising swell when the tide starts (`tide_start_s`), flat, with the score ducked under it (`Sfx.duck`, 3 s, -8 dB). `tide_rumble`: a held loop (`Sfx.loop_hold`) on the ring's nearest point to you, carrying 70 m; `Sfx`'s distance roll-off makes it rise as the wall closes on you, and past 9 m it plays through the muffled bus so only the low end carries. `tide_warn`: a short falling two-note sting at each new close stage (11 stages, about 46 s apart), flat, score ducked 1 s, -5 dB. Joining mid-stage plays no cue. `--blight-check` section 7 asserts the onset once, a sting per stage and none inside one, the rumble on the ring and louder at 12 m than at 60 m; `--sfx-set-check` the files and levels.
## The garden's ambience (`scripts/home/home_ambience.gd`)
One bed for the part of the day on the home clock (`Home.day_t`): `amb_garden_morning` birds (0.2-0.4), `amb_garden_midday` a quiet hush (0.4-0.6), `amb_garden_evening` insects (0.6-0.8), `amb_garden_night` a slow wind; `amb_rain` while it rains. `amb_pond` comes up as you near water (full within 2 tiles, silent past 9, from `layout["water"]`). The beds go through `Sfx.amb_layer`, so they sit on the **Ambience** bus and follow its volume setting; a bed fades out as the next fades in over 20 s. At most 3 loops sound at once (the pond steps out during a crossfade), and the garden never plays `amb_match` (the title's wind). `Sfx` frees a bed once it has faded to nothing. Under them sits the bed of the zone you are in (GARDEN.md 10.2), `amb_zone_<zone>`, up in 3 s and kept: `hub` a quiet Waystone hum, `bramblebank` stream and nap-tree leaves, `glasswater` still pools and ice ticks, `cinderstep` warm cracks and embers, `hushwood` moss-soft hush with echoing knocks, `ridge` wind and humming stones. Hushwood's rule has `"clock": 0.7`, so its time-of-day bed stays at dusk. A hop plays `garden_hop` (0.64 s, swelling for the 0.5 s `hop_fade_s` light-up) from `HubWaystone.hop`; `GardenZones.wake(id)` opens a zone and plays `garden_wake`, once (the unlock step calls it). Regenerate the beds with `tools/gen_garden_ambience.py`, the two sounds with `tools/gen_sfx_garden.py`; `--garden-audio-check` asserts the zone beds, the hop and the wake. `--sfx-set-check` section 7 asserts all of this.

## Boki ability impacts (`scripts/match/boki_impact.gd`, `content/rules/boki_impact.json`)
A Boki's k1 / k2 / zone hit (`src` kin, `ab` in the rule's `slots`; not its strike or throw, which keep `kinhit`) plays ONE impact for its type, wherever it lands: `ki_<type>`, the take set of the type's cast cue `k_<type>` (`Sfx._lazy` loads a `ki_` name from the `k_` files, so the impact has its own gap `Sfx.IMPACT_GAP` 90 ms and its own 3-per-90 ms stack cap, and never eats the cast). Types are labels for identity and colour (DECISIONS 11-10), so the map type -> sound is data (`types`, with `default` normal for an unknown type) and no ability code names a sound. Levels (rule `db`): your Boki -8 dB (crit +2), a rival's Boki hitting you -9, a hit between two others -14, `pitch` 1.1. Played by `Match.on_damage` for your Boki's hits and `Juice.hit` for the rest; a type whose file is missing plays the shared `hit` / `crit` as before (`BokiImpact.play` returns false).
The 19 types and their files: normal `ki_normal` (`k_normal*.wav`), fire `ki_fire` (`k_fire*.wav`), water `ki_water` (`k_water*.wav`), grass `ki_grass` (`k_grass*.wav`), spore `ki_spore` (`k_spore*.wav`), electric `ki_electric` (`k_electric*.wav`), ice `ki_ice` (`k_ice*.wav`), rock `ki_rock` (`k_rock*.wav`), psychic `ki_psychic` (`k_psychic*.wav`), flying `ki_flying` (`k_flying*.wav`), dragon `ki_dragon` (`k_dragon*.wav`), dark `ki_dark` (`k_dark*.wav`), ghost `ki_ghost` (`k_ghost*.wav`), poison `ki_poison` (`k_poison*.wav`), bug `ki_bug` (`k_bug*.wav`), steel `ki_steel` (`k_steel*.wav`), fairy `ki_fairy` (`k_fairy*.wav`), crystal `ki_crystal` (`k_crystal*.wav`), sound `ki_sound` (`k_sound*.wav`).
`--sfx-set-check` row 11 asserts all 19 map to an existing cast set Sfx loads, every species resolves, the slot filter, and the caps; `--sfx-silence-audit` plays all 19 types through `Juice.hit` (the impact, not the shared `hit`) and asserts every `hit kin>*` ability row (k1 / k2 / zone, 10+ events) shows a `ki_*` cue.

## Breakables and chests (`content/sfx/breakables.json`, `tools/gen_sfx_breakables.py`)
Wired in `scripts/match/breakables.gd` and `chest_loot.gd`. The names live in the rules, and `--sfx-set-check` (item 11) asserts every material, powder keg and chest tier resolves to its own sound; `--breakables-check` (item 6) and `--keys-check` hear them play.
- **Breaks**: one sound per material in 3 takes (`Sfx` plays a different take each time), `content/rules/breakables.json` `debris.<material>.sound`: `break_wood` (crate, barrel), `break_clay` (pot), `break_metal` (a keg's hoops and staves, under its blast).
- **Powder keg**: `keg_fuse` from the hit to the blow, then `keg_blast` (3 takes); rules `explosive.fuse_sound` / `blast_sound`. The fuse file is exactly the keg's `fuse` long, so a chain-lit keg plays it `fuse / chain_fuse` times faster and it still ends on the blast.
- **Chest lid**: by the chest's tier, its rarity bonus (`ChestLoot.rarity_bonus`: region, danger and match tier steps), `content/rules/keys.json` `chest.creak`: `chest_creak_plain`, `chest_creak_rich`, `chest_creak_grand` (the last for anything richer).
- Levels: breaks at impact, the fuse and lids at cast, the blast at heavy_impact; the call sites (`Breakables.BREAK_DB`, `FUSE_DB`, `KEG_BLAST_DB`, `ChestLoot.CREAK_DB`) set intent. All are pooled one-shots on the SFX bus (far ones muffled); `Sfx.MIN_GAP` merges a crowd of breaks or a row of kegs blowing together into one of each.

## The Training Grounds (`scripts/match/training_sfx.gd`, `content/sfx/training.json`)
The grounds (opts `training`: the practice island, the Arena and the duel room) don't borrow the match's sound: the match starts no `amb_match` there, and `MusicDir` hands its quarter-second ambience step to `TrainingSfx.ambience` instead of the abyss and ruins beds and the creature calls. Two beds by where you stand, through `Sfx.amb_layer` (the **Ambience** bus): `amb_training_wind` full on the open expanse (the island with the most ground, and the open air, a glide or a drop), and on the sky islands round it the wind down to `ISLAND_WIND` (0.35) under `amb_training_island` (a sheltered breeze, leaves, a far wind chime). Cues, on the **SFX** bus through `TrainingSfx.cue`: `training_dummy_hit` when your hit lands on a practice dummy (70 ms apart at most), `training_reset` on a station reset (Backspace), `training_bot_spawn` when a sparring bot appears (the Arena's N, the duel room), and the duel ring's count-in: `training_count` at 3, 2 and 1 (each a step higher) and `training_horn` as the round starts; nobody takes damage before the horn. Regenerate all of them with `tools/gen_sfx_training.py`. `--sfx-set-check` section 11 asserts each cue is its own sound of the set (a shared fallback such as `revive` fails), the count-in, the beds by position and their bus; `--arena-check` asserts the stations play their cues in a real training match.

## The garden's music (`scripts/home/garden_music.gd`)
`GardenMusic` picks the home's track every 0.5 s. In the garden the hub and each zone have their own, keyed by zone in `content/rules/garden_zones.json` (`"music"` on a zone, `"hub_music"` by day and night): the hub plays Hearthwind (`home_day`/`home_night`), Bramblebank `orchard`, Glasswater `glasswater`, Cinderstep `cinderstep`, Hushwood `hushwood`, Kitewind Ridge `summit`. The three zone tracks no older one fitted are in `tools/compose.py` (`python3 tools/compose.py glasswater cinderstep hushwood`). A hop loads the zone and the music crossfades to its track over `music_fade_s` (2 s): `Sfx.play_music` fades the old track out while the new one fades in. While a garden game is on (each game's `playing()`) the music is ducked by `music_duck_db` through `Sfx.duck`, and it comes back about a second after the game ends. Off the garden the music follows the island you stand on, as before. A track picked in the music list (`Sfx.music_lock`) plays instead, and `hold()` gives the evolve lullaby 25 s. `--garden-audio-check` asserts a distinct track per zone and the hub, the crossfade after a real Waystone hop and the duck.
