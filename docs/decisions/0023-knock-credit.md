# 0023: One knock credit rule

**Problem.** Credit for a knock was decided four ways: `Match.knock` (last hit, a Boki's blow to its owner, a burn to whoever lit it), each kit's own 5 s tracker (`ArchKit.touched`, which read `last_hit_by`, so a burn finished by a teammate's later hit gave the burner only an assist and a hit on a wisp gave a fresh assist), the Vex's own "target is downed after my hit" test (any hit on a wisp reset Slipstep), and the kill feed.
**Source.** Data: Salvage "knocks and assists", Sable's/Dredge's heal on a knock or assist, Kael's "knocking an enemy resets Volcanic Rebuke", Vex's "Knocks reset the cooldown". The wiki gives no assist window; owner item 159 says damage "in the last ~4 s".
**Choice.** `KnockCredit` (scripts/match/knock_credit.gd): `Match.on_damage` and the burn tick note every hunter-on-rival damage; `Match.knock` settles it. The knocker is the credited last blow (Boki to owner, burn to its lighter); an assister is any other rival hunter who damaged the victim within `ASSIST_WINDOW` = 4 s. Each gets `ArchKit.credited` -> `on_knock(victim, mine)`. Hits on a wisp note nothing.
**Effects.** Kit hooks keep their meaning: `mine` heals and resets, assisters get Salvage and Sable's heal only. Kael's Riposte reset is now the knocker's only (the data says "knocking"), the Vex's Slipstep reset runs from `on_knock`. Kill and wisp-finish credit (`eliminate`) is unchanged. Not built: pure crowd control with no damage does not count as an assist (no hook sees it), and a third team's damage counts as an assist.
**Check.** `--knock-credit-check`.
