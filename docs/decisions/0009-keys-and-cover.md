# 0007: One locked chest, keys carried by monsters, the Shrubling, and hiding cover

- **Status:** accepted (owner design of Nov 2 2026, the 09:50 and 10:00 ET entries of ext/equal; part C of the ext/equal split)
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Area:** match flow / loot / AI / map / networking

## Problem
Chests came in three tiers, so where you opened one decided what you got, and any hunter could open any chest for free: nothing sent a squad out to fight for loot. Tall grass was nearly absent (17 clumps, under 1% of the ground), so there was little to hide in or ambush from, and the map had one cover density everywhere.

## Options
1. Keep the tiers and add keys per tier: rejected, it doubles the tables and keeps the map reading as "go to the rich chest".
2. Keys as a shop item or a fixed pickup per region: rejected, a key you can buy or walk to is not worth fighting over.
3. One chest type, locked. Each open costs one key. Keys are carried by monsters, so you fight for a key before a chest (decided).

## Decision
- **One chest.** The chest tiers (`chest0/1/2`, tier colours, tier text) are gone: one model, one table (`content/rules/gear.json` "chest"). What is inside follows the area (`BiomeRun` rarity bonus and slot lean) and the match difficulty tier. `ChestLoot.perk_hook` is the one call site for perk orbs: it calls `PerkOrbs.drop(m, "chest", pos, 0, h)` on the host.
- **Keys.** Opening a chest takes one key and a one-second hold (`KeyChest`). Per region the number of key carriers equals the number of chests there (`KeyRun.assign_carriers`), rolled on a seeded stream of its own, so the same match seed gives the same carriers and no other roll moves. A region with too few monsters (a quiet landing) is carried for by its nearest neighbours, so there is always one key per chest in the world. A carrier wears a spinning key glint. A key is an item (`chest_keys`): it sits in the inventory, goes into the deathbox, and lies on the ground to be picked up. It is separate from the Rift Keys that open rift hatches. Keys live in the utility belt (owner decision, 17:55 ET): chest keys stack to 3 there (`content/rules/keys.json` "keys" cap) and Rift Keys to 1, each type in its own slot. `h.chest_keys` and `KeyChest.spend` stay the source of truth; the belt (part A) reads and spends them through `KeyChest.count(h, kind)` and `KeyChest.take(h, kind, n)` (kind "chest" or "rift"). Nothing is ever thrown away: with a full stack a key stays on the ground with its glint, and a deathbox keeps the keys that do not fit. Until the belt lands, a pickup shows a small "Chest key found" toast; the old standalone HUD counter is gone. Bots go for a key first (`BotKeys`), and walk past keys while their stack is full.
- **Bonus keys** (`KeyBonus`, `content/rules/chest_keys.json`, owner addition). The carrier quota is the baseline; on top of it, at low odds so a spare key feels like luck: a lair boss or the Warden 40% (a quarter of those give 2), an elite 25% (sometimes 2), any other monster 3%, a pot or barrel 2%, a crate or a sky-train crate 4%. No bonus from the Shrubling (it always has one) or the Blight swarm. Rolled on the keys' own seeded stream (`KeyRun.bonus_rng`), so no other loot shifts. A bonus key drops on the ground like a carrier's and bots pick it up. `--keys-check` sums the expected bonus keys over everything in the world at the start of six seeds and asserts the mean is 20-30% of the locked chests (measured: +22%, range +20 to +25%, 25 chests a match). Extraction-wave monsters can add a little on top late in a match. The sky train only runs on Skyreach, so the Hollow Crown figure has no train crates in it.
- **No keys in shops, ever.** Keys come only from monsters, breakables and crates. The in-match shops (still to come) must not stock them; `--keys-check` fails if any content table other than the key rules lists one.
- **Two kinds of key stay separate** (owner confirmed): chest keys (`chest_keys`, the deathbox's `chest_keys` field) and Rift Keys (the existing `keys` field, rift hatches).
- **Shrubling** (`Shrubling`, a `Mob`; revised after the owner's feedback). A bush mimic built from the map's own tall-grass mesh, tinted with the grass colour of the island it hides on (re-tinted each time it hides again). Disguised, it IS a bush: its tile counts as cover (`World.live_bush`, read by `World.in_bush`, so every concealment rule applies), it has no body to bump, and hunters walk into it and hide there. Being close does nothing. Only a hit wakes it: any damage (shot, melee, ability, a Boki's strike), or a fresh root, slow, stun, shove or grab. Then it pops up with a rustle, and anyone hiding in it is in the open at once (the cover goes, and they are seen for 1.2 s, with a "Your bush ran off!" note). It flees faster than a hunter walks and slower than a dash, weaving to break line of sight, dives into the nearest cover patch and hides again. The tell: every 5 to 10 s, on its own seeded schedule, the blades wobble and the leaves shimmer for about half a second; no eyes while it hides. Always carries a key and counts toward its region's quota. Low health, no attack. Slows, roots, Grounded and grabs work on it. Not capturable. 1 to 3 per match, only in bloom, meadow, gloom and storm, only where three or more cover patches lie within sprint range. It uses the ordinary mob systems: `KinPath` for the paths, seeded spawn spots, `Mob.hurt` and `on_mob_dead`.
- **Cover.** `tools/make_hollowcrown.py` now computes the tall grass (`place_cover`), a fixed seed, so the JSON is reproducible. Targets are shares of walkable ground per biome (`content/rules/keys.json` "cover"): bloom 15 to 20%, meadow about 10%, gloom about 6% (Shard Perch bare), storm about 3%, ember 2 to 3%, frost under 1%. Cover is a patch of 3 to 6 overlapping clumps, planted beside chests and camps, along the flanks of the lanes between landmarks, at cliff edges and bridge heads, around the Crown's pillars, and always in sight of every exit in the bush biomes. Never on stairs or ramps, bridges, landing drop circles, lair floors, or across a lane (each lane keeps a clear corridor). Rendering is the existing batched tall-grass path (`World._chunked`, one MultiMesh per chunk).
- **Net:** the carrier flag and the Shrubling state ride the mob snapshot (two more numbers), the chest event carries no tier; `Net.VERSION` 0.16.0 to 0.16.3 (part C's slot on top of sync66; the main session reconciles). A disguised Shrubling's cover tile and the exposure of a hider follow its synced state on every game, and each game exposes the hunters it owns.
