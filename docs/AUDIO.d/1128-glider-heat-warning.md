## Glider heat warning (yours only)

`GliderSfx` plays `glider_tick` (a short rising tick, `tools/gen_sfx_glider.py`, `content/sfx/glider.json`) once as your glider's heat bar drops under `TICK_FROM` (25%), and runs a quiet `glider_ember` crackle loop while the tank is empty, stopped when the glider folds on landing or cools. Both read the bar as a fraction (`Hunter.glide_fuel`, 1 cool, 0 overheated), so a longer tank (`glide_heat_s`, a perk, a Hunter's `glide_cap`) moves them in time but not in level. Other players' gliders make neither (their heat is not synced); the shared overheat crackle and `glider_burn` loop stay. They are ordinary `Sfx.play` / `loop_hold` voices: yours rank `YOU`, so the enemy-priority rule (`--sfx-priority-check`) leaves them be. `--glider-heat-check` asserts the tick fires once at any tank length, the ember loop starts at empty and stops on folding or cooling, and a remote glider makes neither.
