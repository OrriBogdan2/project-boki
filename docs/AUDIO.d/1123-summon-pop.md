## Summon pop sounds

The Boki's burst out of its wisp and its fold back in (`BokiWispPop`, played by `KinSummon.popped` and `KinSummon.render`) have two cues of their own in the wisp set (`content/sfx/wisp.json`, `tools/gen_wisp_sfx.py`, "boki" level): `wisp_burst`, a bright rising bloom (0.45 s), and `wisp_fold`, a soft inward swish (0.3 s). Both play at the Boki on every game (-6 dB, reach 20 m and 16 m) through `Sfx.play`, so `Sfx.MIN_GAP` applies. The burst layers the species' own happy cry on the bloom (`KinVoice.cue`, the shared `kin_happy` when it has none), pitched by `Unique.voice`; this replaces the bare `kin_happy` the burst used to play. A re-bloom beside you plays the burst again, a fold the swish. `--sfx-set-check` section `summon:` asserts the order, the position and the lengths.
