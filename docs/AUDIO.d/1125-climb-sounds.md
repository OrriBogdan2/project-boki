## Climbing sounds

The wall kick, ledge grab and mantle each have a sound by weight class (`ClimbSfx`, `scripts/match/climb_sfx.gd`; set `content/sfx/climb.json`, `tools/gen_sfx_climb.py`, synthesised in code, "step" level): `wallkick_<light|medium|heavy>` (a foot planted on the wall and pushed off: a scuff, a thud, a push of air), `ledgegrab_<...>` (a palm on the lip, then a gritty grip), `mantle_<...>` (the body hauled over the lip: a cloth and stone drag, a belly and knee thump, no voice). A light Hunter is quick and high, a heavy one longer, lower and 1.5 dB louder per class (`StepSurface.WEIGHT_DB`). Wired where the move fires: `WallMoves.try_kick` (every kick, and a wall bounce plays the same plant), `Hunter` at the ledge grab and as the rise goes over the lip. They replace the stone scuff, the bounce's `land` and the stock `grab`. Played at the Hunter, reach 20 m (`StepReach.RIVAL_M`, 8 m crouching) like a footstep, and online `SfxCast` sends them with the Hunter's other sounds (the old "fx" "whop" event is gone; `Net.VERSION` 0.24.27). `--sfx-set-check` rows `climb:` assert the files, the reach, the weight spread, the range and the call sites.
