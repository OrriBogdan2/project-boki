## Garden Boki footsteps

A walking Boki in the garden steps (`BokiSteps`, `scripts/home/boki_steps.gd`, stepped from `home.gd _move_kin`; played through `GardenSfx.cue`). Three sets, by body mass from `Unique.size_info`: `boki_step_small` (under 4 kg), `boki_step_medium` (4 to 25 kg), `boki_step_large` (25 kg and up); a soft pad and a swish of turf, 4 takes each, synthesised in `tools/gen_sfx_steps.py` (set `content/sfx/steps.json`, "step" level), the small quick and high, the large long and low. A rigged Boki steps on the frames its walk or run clip plants a paw (the landing times `BokiPlant` reads off the clip); a Boki without plants (another clip, a primitive body) steps on a cadence from its ground speed (`CADENCE_M`, metres per step by set). Paws landing within `MIN_STEP_S` of the last sound as one. Fliers aloft, Boki napping, indoors, leaping or held by a game are silent. Only walkers inside their set's reach (`reach` in the set: 7, 9 and 12 m) step at all, and only the `MAX_HEARD` (4) nearest of them, on top of the Sfx pool's own voice cap. `--garden-sfx-check` section 15 asserts the sets, the cadence and plant paths, silence out of range or standing still, and the cap.
