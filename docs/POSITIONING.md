# Project Boki: positioning

Owner-approved framing (Oct 31 2026). Everyone working on the game, its store page, its website or
its marketing works from this. When a feature, screen or line of copy could go either way, pick the
side this doc points to.

## The problem we are steering away from

"Creature-catching game" reads as mobile gacha to a lot of players: summon screens, rarity
explosions, collect-them-all counters, energy timers, paid rolls. Playtesters have said it out loud.
The creatures are not the problem. Those cues are.

## What Project Boki is

**A skill-based extraction PvPvE game where your companion is your loadout, and you can lose it.**

- Tarkov-style stakes, SUPERVIVE-style combat, a creature you raised riding on every run.
- Tagline: **Raise them. Risk them. Bring them home.**
- Comps when pitching: Escape from Tarkov / SUPERVIVE for the runs; Monster Rancher, Digimon World,
  Nintendogs for the bond. Not Pokémon GO, not a gacha.

## Pillars

1. **Stakes, not collection.** A Boki that comes on a run can be lost to another squad, and that
   loss is the point. Bringing one home matters because it might not have come back.
2. **Raise one, don't hoard hundreds.** Boki are companions with memory, personality and a body
   no other Boki shares. The game rewards knowing one well over owning many.
3. **Wildlife, not cards.** Boki live in the world: habitats, behaviour, seasons, scarcity. The
   roster is an ecosystem to explore, not a checklist to complete.
4. **Earned, never bought.** No paid eggs, rolls, pity timers, energy or stamina. Rare Boki come
   from playing: bosses, breeding, exploration, events. Money buys the game (or a subscription) and
   cosmetics, never a Boki and never power.
5. **Skill decides fights.** Archetype kits, movement and positioning win; a Boki shapes how your
   kit plays (typing, effects, companion utility), it does not out-stat a better player.

## Scarcity and limited editions

Scarcity is good when it is *earned*: an event Boki you have because you were there, a numbered
serial and a provenance line ("hatched by Orri, Nov 2026, from the Sunken Forge egg"). Trading is
allowed. **Boki are never sold for real money and there is no cash market**: paid scarcity plus
trading is what reads as gacha or NFT, and it carries loot-box regulation risk.

## Words

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| tame, bond, raise, companion, partner | catch, collect, summon, pull, roll |
| expedition, run, extract | daily, grind, farm (as a verb for currency) |
| rare, one of a kind, numbered | SSR, legendary drop, jackpot, pity |
| Bokidex (field notes) | collection %, "catch 'em all" |

## Look and feel

- **Hatching** is intimate and grounded: the Boki waking up, looking around, imprinting on you.
  No light beams, star bursts, rarity colour flashes or slot-machine spins.
- **Rarity** shows in the Boki itself (its colours, markings, how it moves), not in the UI chrome
  around it. No gold/purple/orange rarity frames on Boki.
- **UI**: one currency in view at a time, no pop-up offers, no timers asking you to come back, no
  red notification dots for things that aren't urgent.
- **Key art and screens** show the world and a run (a squad mid-fight with their Boki, a Boki being
  carried to extraction), not a lineup of creatures on a white background.
- **Lore up front**: the Wyrm, the Sundering, the Skylands. A place with history, not a menu.

## Checklist for any new feature or screen

- Would this look at home in a mobile gacha? If yes, change it.
- Does it reward skill, bond or risk? If it only rewards time or money, rethink it.
- Could a player pay to skip it or get it? Then it can only be cosmetic.
- Does a Boki feel like a someone here, or a something?
