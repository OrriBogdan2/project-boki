# Project Boki: tester guide

Thank you for testing. This page is short on purpose. The title screen also shows a small
"What to try today" card (it comes from `content/rules/try_today.json`); the list below is the long version.

## Install (itch app)
1. Install the itch app from itch.io and sign in.
2. Open the Project Boki page we sent you and press **Install**. The app keeps the game up to date;
   when we ship a new build it updates by itself next time you open it.
3. Press **Launch**. Windows or Linux are both fine. A mouse and keyboard are what we tune for.
4. The version number is bottom left on the title screen. Put it in any note you send us.

## What the game is
- You are a Hunter. You wear the Relic of a fallen Hunter and fight as them.
- You drop into a floating-island map with your squad, fight rival squads and monsters, loot, and get out alive.
- Your Boki, the creatures you raise at home, ride with you and fight beside you.

## The three modes
1. **Extraction (trios).** The main game: a squad of three, rival squads, monsters, chests, and three ways out.
2. **Arena (squads, 4v4).** Short team rounds.
3. **Training Grounds.** A safe place to try every Hunter, Boki, item and ability. On the title menu it is
   still labelled **Arena** for now.

## Keys (the defaults; all can be rebound in Settings > Controls)
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Space | Jump (hold in the air to glide) |
| Shift | Your Hunter's dash |
| Left mouse | Weapon |
| Right mouse | Your Hunter's ability |
| E | Your Hunter's utility |
| R | Your Hunter's ultimate (at level 6) |
| X | Fuse with your Boki (level 6 and a full bond meter) |
| Q / V | Active glove / second glove |
| G | Send your Boki out, or call it back |
| F | Use what is in front of you (open, pick up, revive) |
| 1 - 4 | Your belt: tonics and armour kits |
| Ctrl | Sneak |
| Middle mouse | Ping for your squad |
| M | Full map |
| Tab | Scores |
| Esc | Menu |

## What we want tested this week
1. **The four starter Hunters:** Vex, Kael, Lace and Cinder. For each one: can you tell what every button does
   without reading? Does anything feel late, sticky or unfair?
2. **The first match.** Play one whole match from the drop to the exit, ideally your first one with no help
   from us. Tell us where you got lost or bored, and what you would have done next.
3. **The garden.** If your build lets you go home to your island (Continue from the title screen), plant
   something, feed your Boki, and tell us whether it was clear what to do. If your build has no garden, skip this.

## How to leave a note
- **The rate card.** After about ten minutes of play on a version, leaving for the title screen or quitting shows
  "Rate this build": four rows (Combat feel, Boki, Onboarding, Look), 1 to 10 each, and an optional short note.
  Skipping is fine. It is sent to us when you press send.
- **Discord.** The title screen has a "Join the Discord" button. Longer notes, clips and screenshots go there.
- **F8.** There is no F8 feedback key in this build. When one exists this page will say so.

Useful in any note: the version, the Hunter, the mode, and what you pressed just before it went wrong.

## Known issues
- Numbers (damage, cooldowns, unlock goals) are placeholders and change often.
- Some models and animations are still being replaced, so a few Hunters and Boki look rougher than others.
- The Training Grounds is labelled "Arena" on the title menu.
- Online play needs our server to be up. If "Play with friends" is missing or will not connect, play offline and tell us.
