# Rigged Boki (Blender model, emoting face, clips)

A species with `art/kin/<id>/<id>.glb` and a row in `BokiRig.FRAME` is drawn from a skinned model instead of
`KinModels`' primitives: Vespyx (`cindle`), Prismander (`puddlet`), Mycroll (`burrlet`), and Glintmole (`glintmole`) and
Rimeshrew (`rimeshrew`) (roster 50 stubs, in `content/kin_v2/`; `KinModels.build` hands every species with a rig to
`BokiRig`, the other 29 get the size-class placeholder, counts in `--roster-check`).
Also Zephyrak (`zephyrak`). Also Gravadillo (`pebbit`). Also Cairnback (`cairnback`). Also Lunowl (`wisplet`). Also Gloamshell (`gloamshell`). Also Selenfin (`selenfin`). Also Echolop (`zapple`). Also Hivara (`hivara`). Also Glomwort (`glomwort`, no legs: FRAME `"hip": 0.0`), Kestrike (`kestrike`), Kindlewren (`kindlewren`), Lodestick (`lodestick`), Marchleaf (`marchleaf`), Mossgrade (`mossgrade`), Nettlynx (`nettlynx`) and Thrumbird (`thrumbird`). Everything is generated by
script, so a change to a shape, colour or clip is an edit and a rebuild:

    B=~/tools/blender-4.5.15-linux-x64/blender
    $B -b --factory-startup --python tools/boki/build_boki.py -- --species=<id> --blend=<file.blend>
    $B -b --factory-startup --python tools/boki/build_fusion.py -- --species=<id>
    godot --headless --path . --import

Clips: idle, walk, run, attack, ability, hop, sleep, eat, pet, chop, mine, carry, tend. Faces (`BokiRig.FACES`): neutral,
happy, sad, sleepy, angry, focus, cheer, bliss, asleep, chew. Set by the metas `kstate`, `happy`, `pulse`, `pose`,
`dazed` (as for `KinPuppet`), plus `kwork`, `kact` and `face`.

Props (axe, pick, berry) are part of the skinned mesh on their own bones, collapsed in every clip that doesn't use
them; a prop named in the species' `GLOW_PIECES` wears the glow (Lunowl's dazzle over its eye-spots). Materials:
`boki_coat` (atlas, ink), `boki_glow` (the species' glow), `boki_face` (lids, brows, mouth), `boki_eye`. Face LODs
stay off (`FACE_LOD_BIAS`): Godot cuts them from the rest pose, where the lids lie folded.

## State coverage

Every one of the 21 rigged species has all 13 clips (`--boki-rig-check`), so a state is covered the same way on each; the
gaps were in what asked for them. `--boki-state-check` cycles every state below on every species and asserts the
clip (or layer) plays, is not the rest pose (a T-pose), is not frozen, and shows within 0.2 s (`BokiRig.FADE` 0.14 s
crossfade; the flinch eases in over 0.125 s). `--boki-state-check --shots-dir=<dir>` draws `states.png`, a species-by-state sheet.

| State | Where | Asked by | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle, walk, run | both | speed | `idle`, `walk`, `run` |
| nap | garden | `kstate` sleep (Home `nap_t`) | `sleep`, eyes shut |
| eat | garden | `kact` eat (`KinAct`, at the bowl) | `eat`, chewing face |
| pet | garden | `kact` pet (`KinAct`, held and joyful) | `pet`, bliss face |
| play | garden | `happy` (Home `joy_t`) | `hop`, happy face |
| work (chop, mine, carry, tend) | garden | `kwork` | the work clip |
| attack, ability | match | `kstate` attack, `pulse` | `attack`, `ability` |
| perch | match | `kstate` perch | `idle` (the shoulder pose is on `Kin`) |
| hit | match | `hit` (`Kin.flash_t`), or pose `flinch` (its hunter hit) | flinch layer on Flex over the running clip: rocks back, squats, sad face; eases in and out |
| faint | match | `kstate` faint (downed) | `sleep` with the eyes shut, laid over by `Kin` |

The 29 primitive species (`KinPuppet`): idle, walk, run, attack (`att_k`), nap and faint (`sleep_k`), perch (`perch_k`),
play (`happy_k`), hit (a squash-and-ears jolt on the "hit" meta, as on its hunter's flinch; checked per species). They have no eat or pet pose beyond Home's chew squash and the joy wiggle.

### A summoned Boki (out of the wisp)

The Boki bursting out of its wisp (decision 0039) is the same `Kin` with the same rig, so it plays the clips above;
nothing is a separate "summon animation". Which clip each thing the AI does asks for:

| AI does | Code | Clip |
|---|---|---|
| chase, follow, return, flee | `_sim_active` moves it | `run` / `walk` from the rig's measured speed |
| melee strike | `kin_strike` sets `lunge_t` | `kstate` attack: `attack`, restarted while the lunge lasts |
| any ability (`_cast`: projectile, cone, beam, burst, zone, aura, summon, trap, wall, tether) | `Kin._cast` sets `pulse` | `ability` |
| dash, leap (`start_rush`, `start_leap`) | `_cast` pulse, then state "flying" | `ability` (longer than the flight) |
| blink, swap, burst out of the wisp, re-bloom beside you | `Kin.teleport` bumps the `tp` meta | none: the jump is not speed, so it stands (`idle`) |
| takes a hit | `hit` / flinch layer | flinch over the running clip |
| knocked down | `KinSummon.knocked`: `FAINT_S` 0.9 s lying | `sleep` (faint), laid over; then the 0.35 s shrink, then the wisp |

`BokiRig._track_speed` skips the frame after a `tp` change, which is what stopped a wisp's burst and a 30 m re-bloom
from reading as 100 m/s and running in place. While it lies fainted the Boki cannot be hit, targeted or act
(`Kin.fainting`). `--boki-rig-check` runs `tools/boki_summon_clips.gd` for one species per body plan (hivara biped,
cindle quadruped, wisplet flyer, zephyrak serpent) in a headless summoned fight: no speed spike, no run or walk on the
spot, no slide, strike = attack, cast = ability, faint first. `--summon-clips-shots --shots-dir=<dir>` (GPU) draws
`summon_<id>.png`: burst, run, attack, cast, hit, faint, shrink.

Checks and review: `--boki-rig-check`; `--boki-rig-shots --shots-dir=<dir>`, then `tools/boki/review_gifs.sh <dir>`.

Eyes: a visible eye about 18-20% of the head's width (the owner asked for smaller eyes in game). The dome is bigger
than what shows: the head covers its rim. Past that the high cameras and `CamRig.char_lean` make it take the face.

Palettes live at the top of each species' coat section (`PLUM`, `SHELL`...); tools held in the tail are Vespyx's `chop`
and `mine` clips, and `boki_clips.chop_mouth` / `mine_mouth` are the mouth versions the others use.

A Boki without legs (Zephyrak) writes all its own clips: the body is a chain bent along a curve sent down it
(`zephyrak_anim.bend`); its FRAME row has `"hip": 0.0` and `"hover": true`, which gives it a soft shadow on the ground.

To add a species: copy `prismander*.py` to its own shapes, coat and clips, add it to `SPECS` (`build_boki.py`),
`MODULES` (`build_fusion.py`), `BokiRig.FRAME` and `BokiFusion.SPECIES`.
