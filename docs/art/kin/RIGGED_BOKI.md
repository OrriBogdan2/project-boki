# Rigged Boki (Blender model, emoting face, clips)

A species with `art/kin/<id>/<id>.glb` and a row in `BokiRig.FRAME` is drawn from a skinned model instead of
`KinModels`' primitives: Vespyx (`cindle`), Prismander (`puddlet`), Mycroll (`burrlet`), and Glintmole (`glintmole`) and
Rimeshrew (`rimeshrew`) (roster 50 stubs, in `content/kin_v2/`; `KinModels.build` hands every species with a rig to
`BokiRig`, the other 29 get the size-class placeholder, counts in `--roster-check`).
Also Zephyrak (`zephyrak`). Also Gravadillo (`pebbit`). Also Cairnback (`cairnback`). Also Lunowl (`wisplet`). Also Gloamshell (`gloamshell`). Also Selenfin (`selenfin`). Also Echolop (`zapple`). Also Hivara (`hivara`). Also Glomwort (`glomwort`, no legs: FRAME `"hip": 0.0`), Kestrike (`kestrike`), Kindlewren (`kindlewren`), Lodestick (`lodestick`), Marchleaf (`marchleaf`), Mossgrade (`mossgrade`), Nettlynx (`nettlynx`) and Thrumbird (`thrumbird`). Everything is generated by
script, so a change to a shape, colour or clip is an edit and a rebuild:

    B=~/tools/blender-4.5.15-linux-x64/blender
    $B -b --factory-startup --python tools/boki/build_boki.py -- --species=<id> --blend=<file.blend>
    $B -b --factory-startup --python tools/boki/build_fusion.py -- --species=<id>
    godot --headless --path . --import

Clips: idle, walk, run, attack, ability, hop, sleep, eat, pet, chop, mine, carry, tend. Faces (`BokiRig.FACES`): neutral,
happy, sad, sleepy, angry, focus, cheer, bliss, asleep, chew. Set by the metas `kstate`, `happy`, `pulse`, `pose`,
`dazed` (as for `KinPuppet`), plus `kwork`, `kact` and `face`.

Props (axe, pick, berry) are part of the skinned mesh on their own bones, collapsed in every clip that doesn't use
them; a prop named in the species' `GLOW_PIECES` wears the glow (Lunowl's dazzle over its eye-spots). Materials:
`boki_coat` (atlas, ink), `boki_glow` (the species' glow), `boki_face` (lids, brows, mouth), `boki_eye`. Face LODs
stay off (`FACE_LOD_BIAS`): Godot cuts them from the rest pose, where the lids lie folded.

Checks and review: `--boki-rig-check`; `--boki-rig-shots --shots-dir=<dir>`, then `tools/boki/review_gifs.sh <dir>`.

Eyes: a visible eye about 18-20% of the head's width (the owner asked for smaller eyes in game). The dome is bigger
than what shows: the head covers its rim. Past that the high cameras and `CamRig.char_lean` make it take the face.

Palettes live at the top of each species' coat section (`PLUM`, `SHELL`...); tools held in the tail are Vespyx's `chop`
and `mine` clips, and `boki_clips.chop_mouth` / `mine_mouth` are the mouth versions the others use.

A Boki without legs (Zephyrak) writes all its own clips: the body is a chain bent along a curve sent down it
(`zephyrak_anim.bend`); its FRAME row has `"hip": 0.0` and `"hover": true`, which gives it a soft shadow on the ground.

To add a species: copy `prismander*.py` to its own shapes, coat and clips, add it to `SPECS` (`build_boki.py`),
`MODULES` (`build_fusion.py`), `BokiRig.FRAME` and `BokiFusion.SPECIES`.

## Gait audit

`godot --headless --path . -- --boki-anim-audit [--boki=a,b] [--json=<file>] [--old-rate]` drives every rigged Boki
(`tools/boki_anim_audit.gd`) through the real locomotion: the root moves at 1.5 (a following walk), 2.4 (home wander),
4.4 (home fetch) and 6.5 m/s (a match Boki), `BokiRig._process` reads its own speed and picks clip and rate, and the
feet bones are read in the Kin's frame, hand-stepped at 30 Hz.

- **Slide**: a foot is planted while it is low and moving backward; slide is how fast it moves against the ground, as a
  share of the move speed (0 = planted, 100 = the foot skates with the body).
- **Steps/s**: twice the foot cycles per second, at 2.4 m/s. **Seam**: largest pose jump from a clip's last frame to its
  first (share of body height). **Pop**: the same jump on the switch idle to walk and walk to run.
- **Gate**: slide over 25% fails, unless the legs already run at the cadence cap (`BokiRig.MAX_STEP_HZ`, from 80% of it
  up): marked `*`, stride-limited. Flyers, serpents and blobs have no stepping legs: listed, not gated.
- `--old-rate` plays the rate BokiRig had before `GAIT` (speed over 1.5 / 2.9 m/s, at most 1.6x): the before column.

Cause: `RUN_STRIDE` 2.9 and `WALK_STRIDE` 1.5 assumed every clip plants its feet at those speeds. Measured at rate 1
the clips plant theirs at 0.1-1.5 m/s. The fix is in the hand-off, not the rig: `BokiRig.GAIT` holds per species how
fast the walk and run clips draw the planted foot back (m/s) and the clips' step rate; the clip rate is speed over
that, capped at `MAX_STEP_HZ` (8 steps/s) so the legs never blur. `--boki-anim-audit` prints a `BOKIGAIT` row per
species to paste back after a clip is re-keyed.

**What is left**: at walk speeds the feet now mostly plant (cindle, puddlet, hivara under 30% at 1.5 m/s). At run
speeds the clips' strides are too short for the cadence cap: every row at 4.4 and 6.5 m/s is stride-limited (`*`),
slide stays 45-95%, and no rate alone fixes that. The gate passes those rows only through that exception. The next step is a stride gain (the run clips re-keyed with longer reach, or the legs
scaled in the clip rate rule) per species, with this table as the gate. Slide in % of move speed, before → after:
