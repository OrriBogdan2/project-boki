# Rigged Boki (Blender model, emoting face, clips)

A species with `art/kin/<id>/<id>.glb` and a row in `BokiRig.FRAME` is drawn from a skinned model instead of
`KinModels`' primitives: Vespyx (`cindle`), Prismander (`puddlet`), Mycroll (`burrlet`), and Glintmole (`glintmole`) and
Rimeshrew (`rimeshrew`) (roster 50 stubs, in `content/kin_v2/`; `KinModels.build` hands every species with a rig to
`BokiRig`, the other 29 get the size-class placeholder, counts in `--roster-check`).
Also Zephyrak (`zephyrak`). Also Gravadillo (`pebbit`). Also Cairnback (`cairnback`). Also Lunowl (`wisplet`). Also Gloamshell (`gloamshell`). Also Selenfin (`selenfin`). Also Echolop (`zapple`). Also Hivara (`hivara`). Also Glomwort (`glomwort`, no legs: FRAME `"hip": 0.0`), Kestrike (`kestrike`), Kindlewren (`kindlewren`), Lodestick (`lodestick`), Marchleaf (`marchleaf`), Mossgrade (`mossgrade`), Nettlynx (`nettlynx`), Thrumbird (`thrumbird`) and Zippelith (`caecilith`, no legs, the serpent chain; its body is
the Meshy model of the owner's sheet, see below). Everything is generated by

Water: the root meta `kwater` is the water over its feet this frame (m; `BokiWade` sets it every step in a pond,
the rig takes it, so a Boki lifted out or taken by a game is dry at once). Deeper than `SWIM_LINE` (half) of its
height, the rig lifts the model off the bed to float with half its height under and plays `swim` (a Water type,
`BokiRig.swimmer`: body level, legs tucked back, a wave down the tail, fins sculling) or `paddle` (the rest: nose and
head up, front legs or wings stroking in turn, hind legs kicking); both bob with each stroke. One that hovers stays
over the surface. A pond's deep middle is `swim_depth` (1 m, wade.json) deep under a rigged Boki. A glb clip of the
same name wins over the built one. `--boki-rig-check` has the 1 m and puddle rows, `--wade-check` a Zapple paddling
across a stream; `--boki-swim-shots` draws three species afloat (GPU). Faces (`BokiRig.FACES`): neutral,
happy, sad, sleepy, angry, focus, cheer, bliss, asleep, chew. Set by the metas `kstate`, `happy`, `pulse`, `pose`,
`dazed` (as for `KinPuppet`), plus `kwork`, `kact` and `face`.

Wild Boki in a match (`KinWildPose` sets the `pose` meta, `BokiRig.LAYER` answers it with a layer on Flex over the clip,
bent per body plan by `BODY_FEEL`; no clip is baked, the same for every species): wandering is the walk clip; the first
hit startles it (`alert`, 0.35 s: tall, rocks back, focused face); then a timid one bolts (`whine`: low, run clip, sad
face) and a brave one charges (`growl`: lean in, run clip, angry face, the attack clip when it lunges); knocked out
(`dazed`) it slumps, sways side to side on a slow idle (0.4x) with sleepy lids and never spins or freezes; under the
scan beam (`scan`) it is lifted by `Capture.dissolve` and stretches with its legs kicking. A client sees dazed and scan
(state is replicated) but not the startle, bolt or charge (the host alone knows who it is angry at).
`--wild-boki-states-check` asserts it for all species; `--wild-boki-states-shots --shots-dir=<dir>` draws the row.

Props (axe, pick, berry) are part of the skinned mesh on their own bones, collapsed in every clip that doesn't use
them; a prop named in the species' `GLOW_PIECES` wears the glow (Lunowl's dazzle over its eye-spots). Materials:
`boki_coat` (atlas, ink), `boki_glow` (the species' glow), `boki_face` (lids, brows, mouth), `boki_eye`. Face LODs
stay off (`FACE_LOD_BIAS`): Godot cuts them from the rest pose, where the lids lie folded.

Checks and review: `--boki-rig-check`; `--boki-rig-shots --shots-dir=<dir>`, then `tools/boki/review_gifs.sh <dir>`.

Hit flinch: the glbs carry no hit clip. Every damage event to a Boki (owned, wild, summoned; not burn or other
damage-over-time ticks) reaches `Oomph.react`, which calls `KinFlinch.hit` (meta "hit" on the model root). `KinFlinch`
springs Flex (tilt, shove away from the attacker, squash), and `BokiRig._keep_flex` adds it, as `KinPuppet` does for
the primitive bodies. Size of the jolt follows the hit's share of max health and falls with mass; the time to settle
grows with mass (0.20 s light to 0.38 s heavy); each body plan jolts its own way (`KinFlinch.PLAN`). Checks:
`--boki-react-check`; `--boki-flinch-shots --shots-dir=<dir>` (GPU strip of 6 species).

Eyes: a visible eye about 18-20% of the head's width (the owner asked for smaller eyes in game). The dome is bigger
than what shows: the head covers its rim. Past that the high cameras and `CamRig.char_lean` make it take the face.

Palettes live at the top of each species' coat section (`PLUM`, `SHELL`...); tools held in the tail are Vespyx's `chop`
and `mine` clips, and `boki_clips.chop_mouth` / `mine_mouth` are the mouth versions the others use.

A Boki without legs (Zephyrak) writes all its own clips: the body is a chain bent along a curve sent down it
(`zephyrak_anim.bend`); its FRAME row has `"hip": 0.0` and `"hover": true`, which gives it a soft shadow on the ground.

To add a species: copy `prismander*.py` to its own shapes, coat and clips, add it to `SPECS` (`build_boki.py`),
`MODULES` (`build_fusion.py`), `BokiRig.FRAME` and `BokiFusion.SPECIES`.

Stride: `BokiRig.STRIDE[species]` is `[walk, run]`, the m/s a paw on the ground moves back along the body at
`speed_scale` 1 and model scale 1: the median over the clip's contact samples (`boki_anim_audit.contact_steps`: low
in its lift and moving back; the legs swing from the hips, so a paw also passes low on its way forward), the rate
that keeps a planted paw closest to the ground most of the stance. `_pick_clip` sets
`speed_scale = speed / (stride x the model's world scale)`, so a size roll's bigger Boki steps longer, kept under
`MAX_CADENCE` cycles/s. A new or re-keyed species: run `--boki-anim-audit`, copy the printed "measured" numbers into
`STRIDE`.

## Attack contact

A Boki's bite lands `Kin.STRIKE_WINDUP` (0.3 s) after it starts (decision 0061). `BokiRig.CONTACT` holds where the
attack clip connects, per starter line (cindle 0.333 s, puddlet and burrlet 0.367 s at speed 1): the first frame the
front-most bone is 90% of the way to its farthest reach. The rig plays the attack at `CONTACT / STRIKE_WINDUP`, so
the snap lands on the hit; a species without a row plays at speed 1. `--boki-anim-audit` reads the contact off each
clip (must match `CONTACT` to a frame) and drives the bite live (must connect within a frame of the hit). A re-keyed
attack clip: copy the measured time into `CONTACT`.

## Gait audit

`--boki-anim-audit` drives every rigged Boki through the live `BokiRig` hand-off (clip and rate from its speed) at
1.5 (a stroll), 2.4 (a home wander), 4.4 m/s (a home fetch) and its own match speed (`kin_speed`), at scale 0.8, 1
and 1.25. Per gait it measures the planted feet's slide as a share of the move speed and the steps per second,
then the loop seams (the sharpest change in any bone's step across the wrap, over the sharpest inside the clip: at
most 1 the loop point is no rougher than the clip itself) and the idle-to-walk pop (the biggest bone jump while the
rig hands idle over to walk, over the biggest in the steady walk after it: 1 is smooth). It fails a slide over 25%,
except the gaits in its `OVER` ratchet (each gait's ceiling; they only come down, `docs/DEBT.md`), a seam over 1.0,
except the clips in `SEAM_OVER`, and a pop over 3.0. Flyers, serpents and blobs are exempt from the slide: the check
asserts they are exactly the Boki whose feet do not sweep.

Clips start at 0 s. Until 11-13 every glb keyed them from Blender's frame 1, so Godot held each clip's first pose
for 1/30 s and every loop stopped a frame at its wrap (and ran a frame long). `boki_rig.export` sets
`export_anim_slide_to_zero`, and `tools/art1/glb_clip_start.py` slid the glbs already in `art/kin` without a
rebake (only the key times change; `--check` lists any glb that still starts late). That took most seams from
1.1-1.9 to under 1.0. What is left over 1.0 is a real break: a bone turns or changes speed at the loop point
(on the 11-14 merge with main's planted-paw and scan glbs: gloamshell walk 2.0, cairnback walk 1.6, the
three glomwort-rig scans 1.0-1.4, caecilith walk 1.4, kestrike walk 1.3, puddlet, lodestick, mossgrade,
burrlet and kindlewren walk 1.0-1.15; `SEAM_OVER`, `docs/DEBT.md`).

A seam counts the wrap's jump, so a quick beat that runs up to the loop's end reads as a hitch: wisplet's idle wing
shuffle now settles by 0.94 of the clip, and kindlewren's walk loop starts at the bottom of a landing's crouch
(`WALK_FROM` in its clips; its 1.5 m/s walk slides 33%, was 30%, under its 35% ceiling). Thrumbird hovers, so its
gaits differ in stance, not steps: `hover()` pitches it about the wing root (`PIVOT`), its walk (slow flight) keeps
near its hover tilt, and `BokiRig.GAIT_FADE` gives it 0.45 s between idle, walk and run; the pop window follows that
fade. What is left of its 2.1 is the idle's own head dart still showing at the end of the blend.

Slide at scale 1, before (origin/main: speed over 1.5 / 2.9 m/s, at most 1.6x, run from 3.2 m/s) → after (measured
`STRIDE` per species, run from 2.2 m/s, `MAX_CADENCE` 6 steps/s). The rate fix only matches the paw's average
pace to the ground: the clips' sine stance and their short steps at match speed keep most gaits over 25%. The fix
for those is re-keying the clips (`docs/DEBT.md`), not the rate.

Planted paws (`tools/boki/boki_gait.py`): cindle, rimeshrew, wisplet, zapple, kestrike, kindlewren, lodestick and
mossgrade key each paw's path in the model's space instead of swinging the leg on a sine. While a paw is down it
goes back in a straight line at one speed; while it is up it lifts fast (past half its lift within a frame or two),
swings forward and lands moving back at that speed; the leg's own two angles are solved to put it there through
the body's bob and pitch. The runs take a short stance (10-20% of the cycle) with a long reach, so the paw sweeps
back fast enough for match speed within `MAX_CADENCE`; their clips are keyed at 0.75-1.4 s a cycle so a stance has
enough frames (`BokiRig` sets the rate from `STRIDE`, so the length only sets the key spacing). Their rows below
read "before planting → after". A paw aimed past 97% of a two-bone leg's reach from the hip is aimed that far
instead, along the same line (`boki_gait.within`): kestrike's and wisplet's short legs under a body that skims up
in the air used to whirl the hip round and flip the knee to get there, and now tuck. Three runs still pass 25% at
scale 0.8 and match speed (kestrike 60%, rimeshrew 47%, wisplet 28%): their legs are too short for the reach
6 steps/s needs (`docs/DEBT.md`). Kestrike's run seam reads 2.0 because its stance is 3 frames at the wrap: the
paws kick back fastest there; the wrap step itself matches the frames on either side of it.

On a Meshy-scan species (`boki_scan`) the bones move through the fit, so a planted clip is solved again on the
fitted rig (`planted(...).refit`, called by `Scan.CLIPS`): the paws aim from the fitted rest places and the reach,
lift and drop scale with the first leg's new length (kestrike's scan legs are 0.62 of the proxy's). Kestrike and
cairnback (four_legs, a trot) walk and run that way; their glbs keep main's scan model with only walk/run spliced
in (`glb_add_clips.py`), from builds on the machine that holds the dense scans. Kestrike's and cairnback's rows read
"main's sine clips on the scan → planted".

Clips start at 0 s: Blender keys a clip from frame 1, and Godot fills the gap before the first key with the first
pose, so every Boki loop used to hold still for a frame each time round (a planted paw stopped while the body went
on). `boki_rig.export` now sets `export_anim_slide_to_zero`, and `tools/art1/glb_clip_start.py` slid the glbs

Planted paws (`BokiPlant`, `scripts/match/boki_plant.gd`): the clips swing each leg from the hip on a sine, so a
paw on the ground slows to a stop at both ends of its step and no single rate keeps it still. Once per species and
clip, `BokiPlant` reads each paw's step from the clip (from furthest forward to furthest back, and whether a leg steps
two or three times a loop); every frame, after the clip, `BokiRig` re-times each leg's bones on their own: the stance
moves the paw back evenly at the Boki's ground speed (found against the body bones where they are now, at the clip's
own time), the swing brings it forward in the rest of the step. The stance's share of the step follows the speed
(0.25 to 0.75): at full cadence a paw stays down for less of the step instead of sliding. `STRIDE` and the clip rate
are unchanged. A paw that does not sweep once back and once forward (zapple's walk hop, kindlewren, mossgrade's
shuffling middle legs) keeps the clip's timing. It blends in over `FADE` as a walk or run starts and out as it ends.
Cost: about 10 µs per visible rigged Boki per frame (headless, cindle and lodestick), no allocation.
