Warden block:
> Redesign the character from the first two reference images at full production quality, keeping its design (hunched boiler-hull body, brass diving helm with one porthole and a single red eye, winch drum on one shoulder, flared blunderbuss right forearm, three-fingered grab-claw, backward-bent legs), but make it far more broken, corrupted and menacing than the reference: Dredge, the Broken Abomination, a huge deep-sea salvage machine that dragged something out of the abyss and should have stopped working long ago. It is clearly falling apart: the dented tarnished-brass hull is torn open in several jagged gashes with bent rivets and peeled-back plates, showing a ribcage of steel struts, sparking snapped cables and a pulsing red light inside; one shoulder plate hangs loose by a single bolt; barnacles, rust streaks and old seaweed cling to it. Thick black tar-like ooze leaks from every crack and gash, glowing from within with a sick crimson rot-light, dripping in long strings from the helm, the hull seams and the claw, and pooling under its feet. The porthole glass is cracked across, the one red eye inside glaring. Heavy hauling rope runs from the shoulder winch drum to a big iron cargo hook. The blunderbuss forearm is a wide flared brass barrel with ooze dribbling from the muzzle. A machine, not an animal or a mascot: no face paint, no hat, no bow tie, no cute features. Haunted and wrong, like something still moving that should be dead.
