# Tarek, the Rock Elementalist: art kit (owner-approved 2026-11-04)

Files: `tarek_portrait.png` (1:1), `tarek_splash.png` (16:9), `tarek_turnaround.png` (front, 3/4, side, back), `tarek_side.png` (cut from the turnaround, for Meshy), `tarek_before_after.jpg`.

## Design
A young, nimble, cheerful blind cliff-runner who "sees" through stone.
- **Signatures:** three floating geode seeing-stones with glowing amber irises that always orbit his head; amber ripple rings in the ground under his feet (VFX); stone shards orbiting his right fist.
- **Head:** a cloth bandana over his eyes; long locs with stone beads, geodes, copper rings and glowing amber crystals.
- **Arms:** stone bracers grown around both forearms, glowing amber knuckle cracks.
- **Outfit:** an open slate-grey vest, a terracotta sash, baggy cropped sand trousers with patched knees, a climbing harness with pitons, a chalk pouch, bare wrapped feet.
- **Palette:** terracotta and amber glow first, then sand and slate.

## Notes
- The seeing-stones are part of the character: model them as three floating props on an orbit.
- In the turnaround's BACK view, the stones show their plain rock backs; that is correct.
