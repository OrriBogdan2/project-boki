# Claire, the Icy Dancer: art kit (owner-approved 2026-11-04)

Files: `claire_portrait.png` (1:1), `claire_splash.png` (16:9), `claire_turnaround.png` (front, 3/4, side, back), `claire_side.png` (cut from the turnaround, for Meshy), `claire_before_after.jpg`.

## Design
A frost demoness figure-skater. Her innocent, blushing, big-eyed face is the lure; her body is clearly demonic. She reads as a young adult woman.
- **Signatures:** dark ice ram horns, dark frost bat wings, a long spaded tail, long dark wavy hair in loose low pigtails with ice-crystal clips.
- **Face:** big glowing pale-blue eyes, rosy cheeks, frost freckles.
- **Skin:** pale blue-white with faint glowing frost runes, ice claws.
- **Costume:** sculpted entirely from ice: a faceted crystal top, frozen ribbon wraps, a skirt of jagged ice petals, sheer frosty legs, white skates with clear ice blades.
- **Weapon:** ONE slim ice spear (it grows from her palm), held in her right hand.
- **Palette:** ice blue, white, silver, deep navy, dark slate (horns and wings).
