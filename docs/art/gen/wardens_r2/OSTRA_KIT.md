# Ostra, the Void Jester: art kit (2026-11-04)

Files: `ostra_portrait.png` (1:1), `ostra_splash.png` (16:9), `ostra_turnaround.png` (front, 3/4, side, back; labels removed), `ostra_side.png` (cut from the turnaround, for Meshy), `ostra_before_after.jpg`.

## Design
A tall, thin, male void jester, menacing and unnervingly still, leaning forward with his head tilted.
- **Mask:** cracked white porcelain with an unnaturally wide painted grin whose mouth glows violet, pitch-black eye holes with violet pinpoints, a painted violet tear, and void-light leaking from the crack.
- **Signatures (aura):**
  - a giant cracked violet crescent-moon halo floating behind his head
  - a STARFIELD of the Void visible through the tears in his coat and inside his smoky lower body
  - glowing violet marionette strings from his long spidery gloved fingers to orbiting playing-card shards
- **Costume:** a black-and-violet crescent jester cap with bells, a grey ruff with violet ribbons, a tattered harlequin coat with dagged sleeves.
- **Lower body:** no legs; he dissolves into a smoky violet wisp full of stars, hovering above the ground with a shadow gap.
- **Hand prop:** a swirling void orb in one hand.
- **Palette:** black, deep violet, porcelain white, violet glow.
