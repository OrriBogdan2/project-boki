# Vex, the Hired Gun: art kit (round 3, owner-approved 2026-11-04)

Files in this kit: `vex_portrait.png` (1:1), `vex_splash.png` (16:9), `vex_turnaround.png` (front, 3/4, side, back), `vex_side.png` (true profile cut from the turnaround, for Meshy), `vex_before_after.jpg`.

## Design summary
A dangerously pretty young East Asian cyborg gunslinger, hot in a haunted "I could save her" way: a sultry crooked smirk and moody smoky eyes, not crazy.
- **Signatures (must read from above):** the machine-gun arm, the long green braids, the green cyber eye.
- **Hair:** vivid toxic green, choppy bangs, two very long braids wrapped in copper wire and steel bolts.
- **Cyber eye:** a brass eye-plate with a glowing green lens over her RIGHT eye, with circuit lines to the temple.
- **Gun-arm:** her RIGHT arm is mechanical from the shoulder, and the forearm IS a six-barrel rotary machine gun (no hand). There is a small ammo feeder box on top of that shoulder, plus an ammo belt and armoured hoses behind the upper arm.
- **Cyborg plating:** a brass and gunmetal collar and neck plating with green-glowing seams.
- **Body and outfit:**
  - slim and toned, with a bare midriff and a full bust
  - green circuit, gear, star and tally-mark tattoos
  - a black crop top with green trim, a cropped dark jacket (left sleeve only)
  - studded belts, fitted dark trousers, chunky combat boots, a fingerless glove on her human LEFT hand
- **Palette:** black, gunmetal, brass, toxic green, with magenta in the environments.

## Rules for other agents
- The gun-arm is ALWAYS her right arm. The turnaround is the source of truth for 3D.
- **Known nit:** in the splash, the cyber eye sits on her left eye (the splash was mirrored to put the gun on her right). Model the eye on her RIGHT, as in the portrait and turnaround.
- **No candle**: the owner dropped it together with the fire motifs.
