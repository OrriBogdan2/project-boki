## Design
An undead drowned ferryman that the frozen sea remade into a monstrous sea-ogre. He is the biggest Warden (about 1.5x a normal hero), warm and protective, and furious when his people are threatened.
- **Body and head:**
  - a hunched ogre build with enormous shoulders and forearms, and a small head low between the shoulders
  - an underbite with short ice-crusted tusks, a broken nose, small white eyes, a kind face at rest
  - drowned blue-grey skin with frost cracks; barnacles, frozen kelp and icicles on his shoulders and back
  - his red beard frozen into stiff icy braids
- **Gear:** a fur-and-sailcloth mantle with rope lashings and a brass ship's compass, and a teddy-bear trinket on his belt.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Hammer:** a plain block of glacier ice bound in iron ship-bands, with NO pick or anchor (owner).
  - **Shield:** the huge iron-banded wooden prow of his ship, with a figurehead carved FLAT in the wood (no statue sticking out).
- **No lantern** (owner).
