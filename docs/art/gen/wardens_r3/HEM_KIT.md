## Design
The Tyrant's former royal mask-stitcher, now an assassin-archer who hunts his loyalists. She is slim, athletic and alluring, cold, with feet planted.
- **Head:** a sleek black bun pierced by silver needles; sharp dark eyes with liner (NOT red), pale skin.
- **Mask:** a half-mask over her mouth and nose, stitched from cracked fragments of old porcelain theatre masks with red thread: mismatched shards, a painted fragment of an old smile, a gilt edge.
- **Outfit:** a tailored crimson high-collar long coat with black lining and an asymmetric tail, worn open over a fitted black corset top (a little skin at the neckline); a red-threaded needle pinned to her lapel (her calling card).
- **Gear:** silver thimble claws on her right (draw) hand, a tape-measure wrap on her forearm, a needle bandolier, tailor's shears at her hip, boots.
- **Bow:** a black recurve in her LEFT hand, with a glowing red-thread string from a spool at the grip and loose threads drifting off the limbs.
- **Arrows:** arm-length iron sewing needles threaded with glowing red thread, which leave a stitched red trail in flight; they ride in a quiver on her back.
- **Shadow:** her OWN shadow is wrong: the same silhouette, but too long, with clawed fingers and faint glowing red seams. It is not a separate creature.
- **At rest:** drifting red threads and tiny floating needles around her.
- **Palette:** crimson, black, porcelain, silver, red glow.

## Notes
