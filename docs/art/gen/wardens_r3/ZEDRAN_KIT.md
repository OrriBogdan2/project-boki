# Zedran, the Unbroken (Arena King): art kit, final design (owner-approved 2026-11-04)

Files: `zedran_portrait.png` (1:1), `zedran_splash.png` (16:9), `zedran_turnaround.png` (front, 3/4, side, back), `zedran_side.png` (cut from the turnaround, for Meshy), `zedran_before_after.jpg`.

## Design
The Tyrant's undefeated arena champion and a former slave, with a demon in his blade and mind that must be fed. Grim, intimidating, enormous aura, never cocky.
- **Body and head:** very dark skin; a long wild WHITE mane (no horns); a huge scarred shirtless gladiator build.
- **Scarf:** a maroon scarf ALWAYS over his nose and mouth; only his cold, dark, narrowed eyes show (no glow).
- **Signatures from above:** the white mane, the broken gold collar, the chain-hook.
- **Collar and harness:** a BROKEN GOLDEN SLAVE COLLAR worn like a torc; a leather chest harness with a central ring.
- **Right (sword) arm:** glowing magma cracks down this arm only. A heavy iron chain is wrapped around the forearm and the sword hilt, continuing into a long chain that ends in a heavy iron HOOK with a red-hot tip (his Warchain, throwable).
- **Left wrist:** a plain iron shackle cuff.
- **Weapon:** a HUGE jagged, cracked, iron-banded greatsword with magma glowing in its cracks and demonic smoke (no eye).
- **Lower body:** a red pleated champion's skirt, a gold belt with tally marks on the plate, iron greaves, sandals.

## Notes for 3D
- The turnaround shows a second short chain and hook hanging from the LEFT shackle. Model only the shackle cuff and a short broken chain there; the one hook belongs to the right-arm chain.
- In the side view, the greatsword's tip is clipped at the crop edge (it overlapped the back view). Use the front and 3/4 views for the full blade.
- **Splash:** at night in the ruined arena, among fallen gladiators, his hook rips through a charging gladiator's breastplate as he drags him in (no blood).
