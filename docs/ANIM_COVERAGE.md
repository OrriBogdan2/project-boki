# Animation coverage

Which states each Hunter and Boki animates, as a hand-keyed clip in its .glb (`clip`), as a procedural pose
(`proc`: `HunterAnim` for Hunters, `KinFlinch` and the body for Boki) or not at all (`missing`).
The table is regenerated by `--anim-coverage-check` (`tools/anim_coverage_check.gd`) from
`content/rules/warden_anim/*.json`, the clip lists of the .glb files and `BokiRig`; it fails, and leaves this file as it
was, when a cell goes from `clip` or `proc` to `missing` or a row disappears. Run it after any clip change and commit
the new table.

How a cell is read (Hunters): idle, walk, run, jump (`air.rise`), glide, land and the hit pair are the json's `loco`,
`air`, `land` and `states.hit` clips found in the .glb; attack is any kit move mapped to a clip; dash and cast are a
mapped kit move whose key names a dash/skate/vault (or has `"dash": true`) or an ability (words in the check); death is `states.dead` (none
procedural: a downed Hunter is a Wisp, docs/decisions/0019, so a death clip is rarely seen); stomp is always `proc`
(`HunterAnim.stomp_k`, no clip anywhere). strafe, crouch and upper are the blends: the 4-way walk and run rings, the
crouch layer, and kit moves that play on the upper body over the legs. Boki: the .glb clips idle, walk, run, attack,
ability (cast) and hop (jump: a cheer, and any leap, throw or dive in the air, `kact` hop from `Kin.render`; a
species without a hop clip runs through the air); a hit and a landing spring `KinFlinch`; a downed Boki topples
onto its side (`Kin.render`).

Boki garden states (`tools/anim_coverage_boki.gd`): every species with a rigged .glb in the game (a `BokiRig.FRAME`
row and `art/kin/<id>/<id>.glb`, the Bokidex 3D models, 48 today) through idle, walk, run, attack, cast (`ability`),
hop, eat (the feeder's `munch`) and sleep (the garden `nap`), read off the clips of a live rig. `clip` is the species'
own clip; `shared` a clip whose motion (each bone's turn at 16 samples) is the same as another species' (the generic
set every Meshy batch-1 model was built with: no motion of its own); `proc` a clip `BokiHomeClips` builds at runtime
(munch from idle, nap from sleep); `missing` no clip, a clip that holds one pose (no bone turns 2 deg; not sleep), or
one whose every pose sits within 3 deg of a pose idle passes through. Rows run worst first (most `missing`, then most
`shared`, then the lowest Field Book number), and the five worst are written out as backlog lines.

Bind rows (v95 swaps, `tools/warden_states_check.gd`, run by `--warden-clips-check`): each Hunter's game .glb is
driven through all 15 Hunter states and fails on a T-pose (every contract bone within 2 deg of rest), a state that
shows the idle pose, frozen legs in a walk, run or strafe (Motley has no legs: his hips), a clip track that names no
bone, or a walk/run clip that keys no leg bone. `--verbose-states` prints every state; `--warden-states-shots
--shots-dir=<dir>` (GPU) renders one frame per Hunter and state for a contact sheet.

## Gaps, worst first

1. **Gil has no authored clips** (empty `gil.glb` animation list): every state is `proc`, and the Hunter has no
   crouch, strafe or kit-move clips. Needs a Blender rig pass (days), same as the others got.
2. **Boki glide and stomp are `missing`**: the Boki is carried curled up through a glide
   and has no stomp (the Wisp stomp is the Hunter's). The dash is `proc` now: `KinFlinch.dash` leans the body into
   the move and the landing squash settles it.
3. **Hunter dash is `proc` for Gil only.** Albert (`shift_whirl`, `shift_charge`, `dash_short`, `dash_air`), Grimble
   (`lunge`, `lunge_out`, `pounce`), Motley (`swap_aim`, `swap`, `swap_air`) and Meeko (`skip`, `float`) already play a
   hand-keyed clip on their v95 .glb for the whole dash, with none of the procedural pose under it (`--dash-frames-check
   --dash-only=<archetype>` logs the clip, its weight and the rig's: 0 every frame). Measured at 60 fps: Albert's whirl
   owns the body through the 0.17 s dash and its spin (0.73 s); Meeko's skip (0.233 s) spans her 0.22 s hop; Grimble's
   lunge holds through the 0.18 s shove and its gap, then `lunge_out`; Motley's Trade is no travel dash (no roll), the
   aim then the lob. Their cells read `proc` only because the move names do not say dash, so `warden_anim/*.json`
   marks them `"dash": true`. Cinder's ram and poof (`leap`, `hop`) and Lace's roll and flick (`roll`, `flick`) are
   clips, re-keyed to their felt dashes (KIT_PARITY 'Felt dash per kit'); claire, kael, ryzz, sable, tarek and vex key
   theirs too.
4. **25 of 48 rigged Boki play one shared clip set** for idle, walk, run, attack, cast and hop (the Meshy batch-1
   models, 9116769e95: casquebill to velvorm in the table; hoarheron has its own idle only). Nothing is `missing` on
   any Boki, but the shared set is the same wobble on a heron, an okapi and a rope wyrm. That includes glomwort,
   whose `glomwort_anim.py` clips the batch-1 model replaced (docs/art/kin/RIGGED_BOKI.md still lists them). Eat and
   sleep are `proc` on every Boki (munch and nap built from idle and sleep); the baked `eat` clip is never played.
5. Hunter death clips are mostly unseen while the Wisp stands in for a death (DEBT: downed Wisp).
