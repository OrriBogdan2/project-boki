# Animation coverage

Which states each Hunter and starter Boki animates, as a hand-keyed clip in its .glb (`clip`), as a procedural pose
(`proc`: `HunterAnim` for Hunters, `KinFlinch` and the body for Boki) or not at all (`missing`).
The table is regenerated by `--anim-coverage-check` (`tools/anim_coverage_check.gd`) from
`content/rules/warden_anim/*.json`, the clip lists of the .glb files and `BokiRig`; it fails, and leaves this file as it
was, when a cell goes from `clip` or `proc` to `missing` or a row disappears. Run it after any clip change and commit
the new table.

How a cell is read (Hunters): idle, walk, run, jump (`air.rise`), glide, land and the hit pair are the json's `loco`,
`air`, `land` and `states.hit` clips found in the .glb; attack is any kit move mapped to a clip; dash and cast are a
mapped kit move whose key names a dash/skate/vault or an ability (words in the check); death is `states.dead` (none
procedural: a downed Hunter is a Wisp, docs/decisions/0019, so a death clip is rarely seen); stomp is always `proc`
(`HunterAnim.stomp_k`, no clip anywhere). strafe, crouch and upper are the blends: the 4-way walk and run rings, the
crouch layer, and kit moves that play on the upper body over the legs. Boki: the .glb clips idle, walk, run, attack,
ability (cast) and hop (jump: a cheer, and any leap, throw or dive in the air, `kact` hop from `Kin.render`; a
species without a hop clip runs through the air); a hit and a landing spring `KinFlinch`; a downed Boki topples
onto its side (`Kin.render`).

## Gaps, worst first

1. **Gil's dash is `proc`**: his `gil.glb` now has clips for every other state in the table.
2. **Boki glide and stomp are `missing`**: the Boki is carried curled up through a glide
   and has no stomp (the Wisp stomp is the Hunter's). The dash is `proc` now: `KinFlinch.dash` leans the body into
   the move and the landing squash settles it.
3. **Hunter dash is `proc` for 5 Hunters** (breaker, dredge, ostra, pip, and gil): the procedural roll,
   phase and charge poses stand in; Cinder's ram and poof and Lace's roll and flick are clips re-keyed to their felt
   dashes (KIT_PARITY 'Felt dash per kit'), as are claire, kael, ryzz, sable, tarek and vex.
4. Hunter death clips are mostly unseen while the Wisp stands in for a death (DEBT: downed Wisp).
