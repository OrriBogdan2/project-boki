- **--boki-follow-check**: "at a rival it Sics (state active)". Green on the base, red already at the
  early-camps merge c076e1e95. Likely cause: decision 0048 makes XP a kill reward only (the hunter.gd XP
  trickle is gone), and main's run-level features expect levels to rise over time. Not confirmed.
- **--clip-check**: "ostra: a hit mid-run leaves the legs on the run" (foot slide 0.41 m). Same
  bisect result as --boki-follow-check: green on the base, red at c076e1e95. Cause not found.
- **--dash-frames-check**: "juggernaut dash: 5 frames posed of 7" fails the "at least 5" rule. Green on
  the base and at c076e1e95, red after camp-parity and camp-clear (3 runs out of 3). Cause not found.
- **--bot-sanity-check**: 4 windows where bots flip their facing, on seeds 3 and 5 (goals loot, roam,
  keyhunt). Green on the base and at c076e1e95, red on the candidate (3 runs out of 3). It needs both the
  camp leash and the early camps.
- **--camp-clear-check**: brute@1 times out for tender (and on the first run also bulwark and ostra), and
  skitter@1 spread is 16.6x against the 14x ratchet. Green on Ways Out plus combat-camp-clear. Cause:
  ext/camp-parity's leash (LEASH_M 13 m). Kiting kits pull the brute past the leash, and it walks home
  and heals. With LEASH_M set to 999, tender's brute medians are 14.1/9.7 s with no 30 s timeouts
  (15.3/20.4 s with the leash). This is real game behaviour, so the check was not loosened. The bots'
  kit use, or the leash, needs a decision.
