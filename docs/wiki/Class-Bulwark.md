# Bulwark (heavy)

A heavy shield fighter. Blocks to build Guard, then bashes, charges and smashes people into walls and
into each other. Heals the squad from where its mace lands.

## How it plays

1. **Hold RMB** to raise the shield: it has its own health, regenerates, and blocks from the front
   while you walk slower. Damage it blocks becomes **Guard**.
2. Let go (or tap) to **Bash**. At 50 Guard the Bash is a **Shield Slam** that stuns.
3. Knock someone into a wall or into another foe with a Bash, Shield Charge or full Crushing Blow and
   they're **Crushed**: stunned, and you heal.
4. **Left click**: tap to throw the mace (it lies where it lands, shows foes near it to your team, and
   comes back hitting on a second tap); hold for a **Crushing Blow**.
5. **Whirl** (Shift) knocks foes aside; with the shield up it's a **Shield Charge**, invulnerable for its length.
6. **Mending Ground** (E) heals allies and mends your shield at your aim or where the mace lies.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Bash, Shield Charge or full Crushing Blow into a wall or another foe | Crushed: stun 0.5, +8 damage, you heal 24 |
| Bash with 50+ Guard | Shield Slam: push 12, stun 0.6 (spends 50) |
| Shift with the shield raised | Shield Charge: invulnerable, heavier knock |
| Throw the mace, then E | Mending Ground lands where the mace lies |
| Hold LMB, then Shift | A short plain dash for half a charge; the wind-up goes on |
| RMB in the air (Shield Surf) | Block from the front while gliding |

## Tips

- Raise the shield into their fire first: the Guard pays for the Slam.
- Fight near walls and pillars. A Crushed stun sets up a full Crushing Blow.
- Throw the mace round a corner to scout it, then heal there.
- A broken shield locks you out for 3s: drop it before it breaks.

### Abilities in full

**Guard (passive).** Damage your shield blocks becomes Guard (1 per 2 blocked, max 100); at 50 your Bash is a Shield Slam (push 12, stun 0.6, spends 50). Crushed: a Bash, Shield Charge or full Crushing Blow that knocks a foe into a wall or another foe stuns it 0.5 (8 damage) and heals you 24.

**Whirl (dash, Shift, 10s cooldown).** Whirl: a 3.6m spinning dash that knocks foes aside (6 damage, push 7). With the shield raised it's a Shield Charge: 4.2m behind the shield, invulnerable for its length, 10 damage and push 11 (into a wall or a foe: Crushed). Shift while winding up a Crushing Blow: a short plain dash that costs half a charge.

**Shield (RMB, 6s cooldown).**

- *Hold:* Raise the shield: 160 HP, regenerating; it blocks from the front and you walk slower.
- *Tap:* Bash: a 2.6m cone, 12 damage, push 9 (into a wall or a foe: Crushed). Letting go after a block bashes too.
- *In the air:* Shield Surf: hold to block from the front while gliding.
- *Resource full:* Guard 50+: Shield Slam, 18 damage, push 12 and stun 0.6 (spends 50).

**Mending Ground (E, 16s cooldown).** Mark ground up to 7m away (or where your thrown mace lies): 1s later it bursts, healing allies inside 26; if your shield is raised it mends 60. Foes inside are slowed.
