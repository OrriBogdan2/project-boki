# Marlowe (Medium)

The burr-and-needle marksman. A clinging charge leaves a foe Rattled, and the rifle and one long needle
of light do the rest, while every landed ability feeds a lifesteal.

## How it plays

1. **Quill Rifle** (LMB): a steady auto rifle. A long string loses half its punch, and Rattled foes take 28% more.
2. **Burr Charge** (RMB): sticks to what it hits and pops after 0.7s, leaving them Rattled (slowed). On a wall it blasts a cone back out, and a hunter shoved into more terrain is stunned.
3. **Longneedle** (E): stand and charge 0.8s, then fire a needle that passes through cover. A Rattled hunter hit is Skewered. Each hunter hit takes 30% off its cooldown.
4. **Slipstep** (Shift): tap to skid, hold to slide and shoot. Let go early for part of it back.
5. **Fever** (passive): each ability that lands adds a stack of lifesteal for 3.5s (a Burr Charge counts twice). A knock heals a quarter of your health and refreshes Slipstep.
6. There is no R: the Boki Merge takes that key.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Burr Charge, then the rifle | The target is Rattled: +28% rifle damage |
| Burr Charge, then Longneedle | +20% and a Skewered payoff on a hunter |
| Burr Charge in a wall beside a foe | A cone, Rattled for longer, a stun into more terrain, and the cooldown drops 40% |
| Longneedle through a wall | Still hits; the cooldown drops 30% per hunter |
| Knock a hunter | Heal 25%, Slipstep back (Burr Charge back too at rank 3) |

## Tips

- Burr first. Everything else hits harder on a Rattled foe.
- You are slowed while Longneedle charges: use cover, since the needle ignores it.
- Fever heals off rifle damage too: build it before a long string.

### Abilities in full

**Fever (passive).** Each ability that lands (Burr Charge, its blast, Longneedle) adds a stack, up to 8, lasting 3.5s. Every stack makes your damage heal you for 6%, rifle included. A Burr Charge counts twice, once when it sticks and once when it goes off. Knocking a hunter down heals a quarter of your health and refreshes Slipstep.

**Slipstep (dash, Shift, 11.5s cooldown).** Tap: a quick 3.4m skid. Hold: a 6m low slide you can shoot from; let go early and part of the cooldown comes back. A knock refreshes it.

**Burr Charge (RMB, 9s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* Lob a burr 11m. On a hunter or monster it sticks, then pops after 0.7s: 13 damage in 1.9m and Rattled for 1.2s.
- *Resource full:* On a wall it blasts a cone back out: 10 damage, a hard shove, Rattled for 2s; a hunter shoved into more terrain is stunned, and the Burr Charge's cooldown drops 40%.

**Longneedle (E, 12s cooldown).** Stand and charge for 0.8s (slowed, a lane shows), then fire a needle of light 14m that passes through cover and everything in its line: 36 damage, 20% more on a Rattled foe, and a Rattled hunter hit is Skewered. Hitting a hunter takes 30% off its cooldown.
