# Classes

A class is a whole character: its own dash, a passive with a resource it builds, a weapon (left
click), a right-click ability with several variants, a utility on E and an ultimate on R. There are
twelve, four in each weight class:

- **Light:** [Lace](Class-Red-Stitch.md) (pin people, then the big shot), [Neve](Class-Snowglider.md) (skate on ice, chill foes, shelter the squad behind ice), [Sparkle](Class-Storm-Chaser.md) (load Sparks, then Wirerun through them) and [Sable](Class-Silver-Thief.md) (stay on one target from range, cartwheel and reset).
- **Medium:** [Vex](Class-Hired-Gun.md) (burr charges and one long needle), [Meeko](Class-Dawnfox.md) (heal, carry the downed), [Tenzo](Class-Stone-Seer.md) (raise rocks, punch them into the fight) and [Motley](Class-Void-Jester.md) (swell, plant a seed, trade places).
- **Heavy:** [Kael](Class-Unbroken.md) (rage, chain in, crush), [Cinder](Class-Flame-Queen.md) (build Char, flash foes over), [Grimble](Class-Scrap-King.md) (haul a foe in, drop the anchor) and [Mossy](Class-Barnacle-Knight.md) (block, then smash them into walls).

The weight class sets health, speed, mass (how far hits push you) and how many dash charges you get.
Everything else comes from the class kit.

## Levels and ranks

Every expedition starts at **level 1** with one skill point and every ability locked. You earn XP by
fighting (damage, knocks) and slowly over time; each level gives a point.

- **Press a locked ability** to spend a point on it. **Alt + its key** puts another point into an
  ability you already have.
- RMB, dash, E and your glove each go to **rank 3**; the ultimate opens at **level 6** and takes rank 2
  at **level 9**.
- **Rank 2** is the same shape for every class: a shorter cooldown (10 to 15%) and +15% damage.
  **Rank 3** adds a special (an extra bounce, a third charge, a refund...). Your class page lists them.
- Your level shows on the HUD; a gold ring flashes on you when you level, and everyone nearby sees it.

## Your Boki and your class

Your Boki doesn't replace any of your class abilities. It brings its own ability (C), fights on its
own when you send it out (G), and adds a little of its type to your kit: each class ability has
**sockets** (hit, control, guard, utility, modifier) and your Boki's parts fill them at a fraction of
their strength (35% on RMB, 30% on E and the dash, 50% on R). Hard control from a socket is capped. An
empty socket gives +10% to that ability's main number instead.

**R is the Merge** for every class. When your **bond meter** is full, press it and you and your Boki
become one for a while: you wear its look, and your abilities change by its type and power (its merge
form, see [Boki species](Boki-Species.md)). The class's own ultimate is held back for now.

## Choosing

Each weight class has a default: Sable (light), Vex (medium) and Kael (heavy). You pick
your class in the Expedition menu, and you can try every class against dummies in the
[Arena](Arena-and-Training.md) at any time.

### How Hunters unlock

A new save picks 1 of 1 starters (the Hired Gun) before the egg pick and plays the tutorial as it. The other starters open after your first expedition. Every other Hunter opens through its own challenge, counted across all your expeditions whoever you play. A done challenge never opens a Hunter by itself: you hold the button on the Hunters screen for 1.6s, and letting go early cancels it. Tiers: easy is about 1-2 expeditions, medium a handful of expeditions, hard real effort, once other Hunters are open. Locked Hunters stay playable in the Arena and the Training Grounds, and saves from before Hunter unlocks keep every Hunter they had.
