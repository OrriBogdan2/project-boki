# Linnet, the Tender (medium)

A lantern-carrying support. Linnet keeps the squad on its feet: she heals with Hearthlight, carries
downed friends out of danger and roots foes that chase them. She has no ultimate; R is your Boki's Merge.

## How it plays

1. **Hold left click** to throw embers from the Lantern Crook. Each ember **Kindles** a foe (up to 5);
   Kindle adds damage to your embers, and an ember into a Kindled foe winds Hearthlight back. Pause a
   second and the next ember flies further.
2. **RMB** throws a Burrseed that sticks to the first foe and bursts into roots 2s later.
3. **E** raises Hearthlight, a ring of light that heals the squad and flares at the end. Press E again
   to toss it onto a teammate.
4. **Shift** (Relay Step) is a short hop. Pass through a teammate to get half a dash back.
5. **Bearer** (passive): run into a downed teammate to carry them (up to two). You get a shield and move
   slower; set them down with interact.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Ember into a Kindled foe | Hearthlight winds back 0.2s |
| Relay Step through a teammate | Half a dash back and 1s off Hearthlight |
| Hearthlight on a downed teammate | Their bleed bar fills five times over |
| Toss Hearthlight at yourself while carrying | It lands on the one you carry |
| Rank 3 flare | A downed teammate in the ring stands back up |
| Stun or shield break while carrying | You drop them and cannot pick up for 4s |

## Tips

- Burrseed a chaser, then carry your friend out while they are rooted.
- Keep a foe Kindled to cut the Hearthlight cooldown.
- A stun puts Hearthlight out: stand behind cover.

### Abilities in full

**Bearer (passive).** Run into a downed teammate to shoulder them (up to 2; never one an enemy is standing on). You carry a Bearer shield (40, +6 a level) that refills each time you pick someone up. While you carry them they don't bleed out and can't be stomped, and you move 30% slower. Press crouch to set them down. If the shield breaks or you're stunned, rooted or hexed, you're stunned 0.6s, drop them, and can't pick anyone up for 4s. Your embers Kindle foes (up to 5, 4s): each stack adds 1 damage to your embers, and an ember into a Kindled foe winds Hearthlight back 0.2s (0.4s for a long-range ember).

**Relay Step (dash, Shift, 7.5s cooldown).** A quick 4.2m hop (in the air: your long dash). Pass through a teammate on the way and half a charge comes back, and Hearthlight's cooldown drops 1s.

**Burrseed (RMB, 10s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* A slow seed (12 m/s, 11m) that sticks to the first foe it hits (9 damage). Everyone sees the burr on them. 2s later it bursts: 35 damage and root 1.1s to them and every foe within 2.2m, and each is Kindled.

**Hearthlight (E, 11s cooldown).** A 3.5m ring of lantern light round you for 2.5s: it heals teammates inside 10 over its life, then flares for 22. Press E again to toss it up to 9m: for 1s its healing is 50% stronger, and it latches onto the first teammate it passes. It heals you at a third, and a downed teammate five times over. A stun while it's on you puts it out.
