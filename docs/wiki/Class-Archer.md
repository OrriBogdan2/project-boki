# Archer (light)

A light marksman who controls space. Snares pin people in place, and a pinned target eats the big
shot. Holds high ground and grapples onto ledges.

## How it plays

1. Draw the longbow in stages; letting go right at full draw (the **perfect window**) adds 25%.
2. **Tap RMB** for a snare: it roots a foe, or plants a Tripwire on the ground. **Hold RMB** for a
   Heavy Shot that pierces and, driven into a wall, stuns (PINNED).
3. Snares and Heavy Shots that hit build **Focus**. At 100 your next shot is drawn at once with more
   range, and if it hits a rooted or stunned foe it's a **Deadeye**: a gold crit for +18.
4. **Arrow Rain** (E) across a path slows and blights everyone walking through it.
5. **Great Arrows** (R): three shots with no draw that fly far, pierce everyone and knock back; the third hits hardest.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Snare, then a full-Focus shot on the rooted foe | Deadeye (+18, crit) |
| Heavy Shot that pushes a foe into a wall within 3m | Stunned 0.8 (PINNED); fills Focus |
| Tap snare while your Arrow Rain is falling | It roots 1.0s instead of 0.6s |
| Tap snare at 100 Focus | Roots 1.0s |
| RMB in the air (Plunge) | Shoots straight down, pushes foes, lifts you 3m and cools your glider |
| Grapple Arrow into a ledge | You zip up and climb on top; Focus builds faster up high. Into nothing: a hop back |
| Third Great Arrow into a wall | A payoff hit that stuns 1.0s |

## Tips

- Focus builds while you stand still or sneak and drains while you run; a hit on you knocks 40 off.
- Lay Tripwires on the routes people use to reach you, then fight from above them.
- The Grapple works from a glide and cools it: use it to save a bad jump.
- The sniper alternative trades the draw for a laser and one heavy round.

### Abilities in full

**Focus (passive).** Builds while you stand still (full in 1.4s), sneak (2.8s), 50% faster on high ground; a snare or Pinning Shot that hits adds 40 (a Pin into a wall fills it). Drains while running and drops 40 when hit. At 100 your next shot is fully drawn or aimed at once, with +20% range, and if it hits a rooted or stunned foe it is a Deadeye: +18 damage, a crit.

**Grapple Arrow (dash, Shift, 7s cooldown).** A line arrow at the cursor (9m). If it bites a wall, rock or ledge you zip to it and climb on top; if it bites nothing you hop 3.2m back. Works from a glide and cools it.

**Snare (RMB, 9s cooldown, 2 charges).**

- *Tap:* A snare arrow 11m: 8 damage and root 0.6 on a foe; on the ground it plants a Tripwire (root 1.2, reveal 3s).
- *Hold:* Heavy Shot: charge 0.5s -> full at 1.1s (rising creak, a ring, a ping at full): 16 -> 30 damage, push 6 -> 12, pierces 2; driven into a wall within 3m it stuns 0.8 (PINNED).
- *In the air:* Plunge: shoot straight down, push 8 around you, lift yourself 3m, cool the glider.
- *During E:* With your Arrow Rain falling the tap snare roots 1.0.
- *Resource full:* At 100 Focus the tap roots 1.0.

**Arrow Rain (E, 15s cooldown).** Lob a volley up to 9m: 0.35s later a wall of arrows rains along a 6m line across your aim for 3.75s. Foes inside take 5 every 0.5s, are slowed 35% and blighted (healing barely takes).

**Great Arrows (R, 100s cooldown).** A 0.5s fuse, then for 6s your next 3 shots need no draw: great arrows that fly 22m, pierce everyone, knock back and hit for 28, 28 and 44 (the third a payoff hit that stuns 1.0 into a wall).
