# Ashwick (heavy)

A flue sweep in a sooty top hat with a bellows pack. Soaks foes in Char with the torch lance, flashes them
over, and hits back harder after every knock.

## How it plays

1. Every hit builds **Char**; at 31 the foe **Flashes over** and burns for 3s. Stoking a burning foe upgrades the burn.
2. **Torch Lance** (LMB, hold): a cone of fire that builds Char fast. 8 seconds of fuel, then a short refill.
3. **Cinder Shot** (RMB): on a burning foe it upgrades the burn and spreads it.
4. **Spark Leap** (Shift, 2 charges): through the foe with a burst of Char; rank 3 leaves a fire trail.
5. **Furnace Cloak** (E): scorch, drag foes in, shield up. A knock resets it.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Hold the lance about a second | Flashover level 1 |
| Cinder Shot on a burning foe | upgrade and spread |
| Cloak, then lance | dragged-in foes catch fire fast |
| Lance or cloak behind a wall | nothing |
| Fire trail over the abyss | never forms |

## Tips

- Open with the lance, finish the upgrade with Cinder Shot, and let a knock refresh the cloak.

### Abilities in full

**Flashover (passive).** Your fire builds Char on foes (it all falls off after 5s). At 31 Char the foe Flashes over: 3s of burning for 2% of their health a second; fed again to full Char it becomes a fiercer burn (4%). Anything that stokes a foe already burning upgrades it. A knock heals you 16% of your health.

**Spark Leap (dash, Shift, 9s cooldown).** A 4.4m leap through the first foe: 13 damage, a nudge and 17 Char (half a Flashover). A knock restores one charge only.

**Cinder Shot (RMB, 7s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* Fire a cinder 14m: 14 damage and it Flashes the foe over. On a foe that is already burning it upgrades the burn and spreads the burn to foes within 4m.

**Furnace Cloak (E, 10.5s cooldown).** A 4.5m wave of heat: 9 damage, 16 Char and Anti-Heal for 3s, and foes in sight are dragged toward you (walls stop them). Hit anyone and you wear a 3s shield of 6.4% health plus 5.1% per foe. A knock resets it.
