# Expeditions

An expedition is a PvPvE extraction run: rival squads, monsters, wild Boki, the Blight and loot on a
big map of floating islands. Get out with what you found, or lose what you brought.

Pick it from the Expedition menu at home: your class, your Boki, a **tier** and a **mode**.

- **Tiers:** the Meadow (Boki are never lost), the Dusk Isles (opens after 2 extractions) and the
  Hollow (after 5). Higher tiers have tougher monsters, more loot, rarer Boki and real risk to your
  Boki. See [Rules](Rules.md#expedition-tiers) and [Boki](Boki.md#losing-a-boki).
- **Format:** always 10 squads of three (30 hunters). Bots fill every spot no player takes; there is no solo or duo queue.
- **One life.** Health and armor don't regenerate. Tonics (1) heal and armor kits (2) repair armor.
- **Clock.** A match runs 6 minutes, then the islands fall.

## The drop

The match opens with a short **planning phase**: the full map is up and everyone can ping where they
want to land (bots ping too). Then the skyship crosses the map.

- Your squad has a **drop leader**. The leader jumps and the rest of the squad follows in formation.
  **P** passes the lead to a squadmate (or click a name on the map); **X** separates you to drop alone.
- After jumping you glide down; a ring on the ground shows where you'd land. Anyone still aboard at
  the far side is dropped.
- Flying low over a rolled spawn spot on the way down scouts it for your squad (see below).

## Moving around

- **Jump and glide.** Hold Space in the air (or press Mouse 5, which opens the glider at once without
  a jump). Speed is what keeps you up: fast gliders barely sink. Steer hard against your heading to
  brake. The glider heats up (about 14 seconds of level gliding); it cools fastest on the ground.
- **Feathering.** Opening and closing the glider is instant with no penalty. With it closed in the air
  it cools a little, and for a moment after closing you keep the glide's speed and steering, so
  quick taps cover more ground per heat than holding it.
- **Long dash.** Shift in the air (or with your dash on cooldown) is a long dash.
- **Ledge rise.** Touch the side of an island above you while pushing into it, in the air or gliding,
  and you float up its face and pop over the top, steering the whole way. Jump just before the top for
  a fling. Moving off the face, dashing, a new glide, a stun or a knock ends it.
- **Walls.** Jump at a wall in the air to kick off it. Anyone knocked hard into a wall is stunned.
- **Bridges, air rings, jump pads, updrafts and wind roads** link the islands. Glide through rings for a
  burst of speed. Some maps have a **sky train** you can ride (it carries a crate of early loot); its
  front kills on contact, and a horn and lit rails warn you.

## The abyss

Below island level the view tints rose and deepens toward red the further you fall, with a whoosh, a
heartbeat and a warning near the end. There's room to recover:

- **Abyss lift.** Gliding under an island's lip pulls you back up, harder the deeper you are. It burns
  glider heat about four times as fast.
- **Point of no return.** About 13 m under the lip (a little more while your glider still has heat).
  Past it you're gone.
- Ledge rise, updrafts and some Boki abilities (a loyal Lunowl will lift you) also save a fall.

## Fighting

- **Armor** sits over your health. It soaks hits first and shatters with a blue flash. Neither comes
  back on its own: armor kits (2) repair armor, tonics (1) heal, and armor shards drop from crates
  and big monsters. Some Boki give you a small shield that refills a few seconds after you stop
  taking damage.
- **Accuracy.** Shots spread more while you move, jump or dash. Plant your feet.
- **Perfect dodge.** Dash through an attack just as it lands: you get some dash back and your next shots crit.
- **Sight.** You see a cone where you aim plus a small circle around you; rivals outside it are hidden.
  Walls cast sight shadows, teammates share what they see, tall grass hides you, and firing or getting
  hit gives you away for a moment. Sneaking (Ctrl) slows you, steadies your aim and keeps you hidden
  until rivals are close. Red arcs round the middle of the screen point at whoever is hitting you.
- **Levels.** You start at level 1. XP comes from fighting; each level is a skill point. Your ultimate
  opens at level 6. Some chests and events offer **run perks** (pick one of three with 1-3).
- **Stagger.** Big monsters open short purple-ring windows while winding up a slam. Hit one with an
  ability (not a plain shot) in that window: a big hit, a long stun, extra damage the whole time, and
  your Boki's abilities come straight back.
- **Truces.** Hold Y for the emote wheel; the white flag offers a truce to the nearest rival squad. A
  truce stops both squads' bots fighting and lets you share an uplink yard or a skiff. Attacking a
  partner breaks it, is announced, and marks you on everyone's map for a while.
- **Basing (B).** In a squad, channel 3 seconds to teleport to a teammate. Moving, attacking or any
  hit breaks it, you arrive weakened for 3 seconds, and it has a 40 second cooldown.

## What's on the map

The layout of a map never changes. What's live is rolled each match:

- **Big events** (vaults with a hoard, live boss lairs) are shown on the full map (M) from the start.
- **Everything else** (rare Boki, item caches) shows as a faint "?" at every possible spot until your
  squad gets eyes on it (or flies low over it on the drop). Then you see the real thing or an empty cross.
- **Events:** a meteor shower about a quarter of the way in (the stars leave gems), the Warden waking
  in the middle, and halfway through a Glimmerkin that runs: bring it down before it gets away.
- **Loot:** crates, pots and red barrels break when you shoot or dash through them (barrels explode).
  See [Gear and items](Gear-and-Items.md).
- **Chests and keys.** There is one kind of chest and it is locked: holding F opens it for one key.
  Keys are carried by monsters (glinting gold), as many as there are chests in each region; kill one and
  the key drops. Now and then a spare one turns up: elites and bosses sometimes drop one (or two), any
  monster rarely, and pots, barrels and crates very rarely. Keys are never for sale. Keys sit in your utility belt (up to 3), go into your deathbox and
  can be picked up off the ground; with a full stack you leave the key lying there. Bots fight for keys before chests. What a chest holds follows its area and the
  expedition tier.
- **Shrublings.** A bush that is not a bush. While it hides it is real cover: you can walk into it and
  hide in it, and standing next to it gives nothing away. Every 5 to 10 seconds its blades wobble and
  shimmer for a moment. Hit it and it bolts (anyone hiding in it is suddenly in the open), faster than
  you walk but slower than a dash, weaving, and dives into other tall grass to hide again. It never
  attacks, always carries a key, and a slow, a root or a grab stops it. They live only in the
  tall-grass biomes (bloom, meadow, gloom, storm), one to three per match.
- **Tall grass** hides you. It varies by biome: a lot in bloom thickets, some in meadow bands, little in
  gloom, storm and ember, and almost none on frost ice.

Each map and what it rolls is on [Maps and biomes](Maps.md).

## Boss lairs

Walled arenas, each sealed by a ward and holding a boss and the **Rare Egg** it guards. Each match
rolls which lairs are live (the rest lie dormant).

- **Getting in.** Every lair has a **lock dial** at its gate: a needle sweeps round and you press F as
  it crosses each lit arc, through three or four locks that get faster and tighter. A miss resets the
  lock, rings a chime that pings you to everyone nearby, and calls guards. Some lairs have a second way
  in: three **rune pylons** to light (Stormcrown Eyrie) or three **ward stones** to break (the Thornhive).
- **The fight.** Step past the threshold and the boss wakes and the gate seals behind you for up to 90
  seconds. Everyone within 150 m hears the roar and gets a ping, a shaft of light rises over the lair,
  and the seal's clock floats over the gate, so a squad outside can camp it. Bosses have phases at 66%
  and 33% and enrage after 75 seconds. They fight everyone inside, rivals included.
  - **The Stone Colossus:** slams with shockwave rings you jump, thrown boulders, fissures.
  - **Stormcoil, the Sky Serpent:** telegraphed lunges, lightning circles, a coiled discharge.
  - **The Brood Mother:** acid spit and puddles, egg sacs that hatch adds, burrowing.
- **After.** The gate stays shut for 15 seconds unless someone inside pulls the lever by it, and an
  updraft at the back carries the winners over the wall to glide out another way.
- **The Rare Egg** shows on everyone's minimap for a few seconds. It takes a backpack slot, stays in
  your deathbox if you fall, and hatches at home in the Incubator: Cairnback (Colossus), Zephyrak
  (Stormcoil) or Hivara (Brood Mother).

## Getting out

Three ways out, each with its own risk:

| Exit | How | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Skiff dock** (on the outer landings) | Hold F at the horn beacon to call a sky-skiff. It arrives after about 45 s and holds at the pier for a 12 s boarding window. | The horn and a flare tell the whole map; monsters come at the dock in waves; the skiff leaves with whoever is on deck, rivals included. Docks close one by one late in the match. |
| **Rift hatch** (all over the map) | Find a **Rift Key** (rare loot, it sits on your belt) and press F or its belt key: the hatch grinds open over 10 s. | Anyone nearby can see and hear it. Three people can drop through before it seals, and they don't have to be yours. A key you carry goes to your deathbox if you die. |
| **Uplink outpost** (walled yards) | Hold F at the mast to start it, then hold the yard while it charges (about 40 s alone; two or more of your squad charge it faster). The lift opens when it's done. | Starting it brings monsters. A rival in the yard stalls it, and one who holds it alone for 5 s takes it over. An empty yard drains the charge. |

Extract and everything you carry goes home: weapons and gloves to the Armory, valuables to sell, Boki
you scanned to your farm.

## Down, dead and back

On SUPERVIVE-style rules, tuned for extraction (numbers on [Rules](Rules.md#down-death-and-revive)):

- **Downed.** At 0 health you become a **wisp**: you crawl, can't be shot, and bleed out in 15 s.
  A teammate standing on you brings you back in 4 s at a quarter of your health (extra teammates make
  it faster). An enemy standing on you drains you faster and blocks the revive; holding F on you
  starts a stomp that finishes you. Your Boki can pull you up too (8 s) and goes after anyone who
  comes to stomp you.
- **Squad wipe.** Knock the last standing member of a squad and the whole squad is out.
- **Takedowns pay.** Knocking or killing a rival hunter (mobs do not count) heals you 5% and brings every dash back at once, with a bright chime.
- **Dead.** Everything you carry goes into a **deathbox** where you fell (its colour shows how good the
  loot is). For 90 s a teammate can hold F there for 8 s to bring you back at a quarter of your health
  with your loot. Anyone can loot it.
- **Revive beacons** (green pillars, six per map, single use) bring back every dead or downed teammate
  after 6 s of standing on one: full health, none of what you carried, and 12 s of -25% damage. Two
  squads on one beacon pause it.
- While you're dead the camera follows a teammate. Once nobody can bring you back, your expedition is over.

## The Blight

The Wyrm's corruption, a faction that hunts every squad by **noise and light**.

- **Skitters** are fast and fragile, hunt noise in packs later on, and give up when they lose you.
- **Wardens** are slow sentries sweeping a cone of light. If one spots you it shrieks: every Blight
  nearby comes to the noise, and from mid-match new Skitters claw out of the ground around it. Early
  on the shriek is only a warning.
- **It starts weak and ramps.** It's weakest in your squad's first minutes on the ground (there's a
  grace period after landing) and on the outer islands; stronger with time, toward the core, at night
  and in storms. Early Blight die fast and hit soft.
- **Noise draws it.** Gunfire, blasts, opened chests, dashing and running all add up; quiet squads see less of it.

## Raid conditions

Each match rolls the weather and light it's played under:

| Condition | What changes |
|---|---|
| Clear | A clear evening with a low amber sun (the most common) |
| Dusk | Deep violet, sight a little shorter |
| Night | Moonlight; sight much shorter, light gives you away, the Blight is bolder |
| Ash | An ash storm; warm grey haze, sight shorter |
| Fog | Grey and close; sight much shorter |

The Arena, training, the tutorial and the duel room are always clear.

## The Demo match

On the title screen once you have a save: a real expedition set up for showing the game. A trio with bot
squadmates, on the Dusk Isles (so a Boki in a deathbox can be lost), on a fixed map roll with clear
weather and no Blight. Rivals leave you alone for a minute after you land, and your squadmate leads
the drop to land you between a live boss lair and an uplink outpost. It plays on your save like any
expedition: what you bring can be lost, and what you extract is kept. **Settings > Demo** applies the
same calm options to your own offline matches.

## Maps

Expeditions are played on hand-made maps. The current one is **Skyreach**: the Heart in the middle,
four large regions around it, calm corner islands, four outer landings, a ring road of bridges, three
boss lairs and a sky train. Full details on [Maps and biomes](Maps.md).

**Coming next: the Hollow Crown** (branch `handmap`, set to replace Skyreach as the default). Four
quiet outer isles with the exits (Larkhollow and Duskmoor have skiff docks, Highwatch and Saltmere
uplinks), a mid ring of four regions each with one raised plateau reached by stairs (Frostgate,
Cinder Steps, Stormbreak, Thornwood), and a raised core, **the Crown**, with the vault on the Throne,
two boss Gaols and the most loot and Blight. Every route into the Crown is a bridge or a glide over
open air. Lairs: Stormcrown Eyrie (Stormcoil), the Sunken Forge (Stone Colossus) and the Thornhive
(Brood Mother), two of them live each match.
