# Dancer (light)

A light duelist who picks one target and never lets go, from range. Every bolt marks them, every
cartwheel empowers the next bolt, and landing that bolt gives the cartwheel straight back.

## How it plays

1. Hold left click: a bolt leaves each hand in turn every 0.45s and lands at your cursor. Each hit adds a
   **Mark** to the target (up to 8, each one +12% damage from your bolts).
2. **Vault** (Shift): a short cartwheel. Your next bolt is **empowered**: a big blue chakram for 3.5 times
   the damage, two Marks and Shred. The aim line turns gold while it's ready.
3. Land it and Vault comes back. Cartwheel again. Keep the chain going and the Marks never drop.
4. **Bola** (right click): the lane shows for a moment while you aim, then the Bola grounds the first foe
   and flies on; catch a second and both are stunned. It bounces once off a wall.
5. **Encore** (X, from level 6): a violet ring grows round you and rides with you; for 2s blades fly at every
   foe inside it, harder from its outer edge (which also blights them), and the Vault comes back. Landing it
   on a rival hunter fills your bond meter a long way. R is the Boki Merge.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Vault, then a bolt that hits within 3s | Empowered: 3.5x, 2 Marks, Shred; Vault comes back and the speed boost ends |
| Vault, then a bolt that misses | The empowered shot is spent |
| Bola into a single foe | Grounded 1.5s: no dash, jump or glide, slowed; yellow rings bind them |
| Bola catches a second foe | Both stunned 0.9s and +13 damage (TANGLED). At rank 3 their Marks pool |
| Shadow Knife, then E again (or wait 6s) | You blink to the knife through a trail of afterimages |
| Shadow Knife sticks in a foe (rank 3) | Vault comes back and half the Knife's cooldown comes back (a quarter on the ground) |
| Encore (X) | Up to 5 blades at each foe in the ring over 2s; every blade refreshes your Marks; you keep moving and shooting |
| Bola, then Encore | The Grounded foe can't leave the ring and takes every blade |
| Keep a foe at the ring's edge | Harder blades and a 6s blight (healing barely takes) |
| Knock or assist | You heal (a quarter of your health in all on a knock) |

## Tips

- Marks last 4s and refresh on every hit. Switching targets throws your stacks away.
- Vault is your damage as much as your escape: don't spend it running unless you must.
- Throw the Shadow Knife past a fleeing target, then blink to it to cut them off.
- Put the cursor on them: a bolt stops where you aim, so aiming short misses.

### Abilities in full

**Marks (passive).** Every bolt that hits puts a Mark on the target (up to 8, each lasting 4s and refreshed by the next): your bolts on them deal +12% per Mark. The empowered shot adds 2, the Bola 1. A knock heals you 15% of your health on top of the usual knock heal (25% in all) and bursts a crimson star on them; an assist heals 25%.

**Vault (dash, Shift, 4.5s cooldown).** A 2.4m cartwheel the way you move (shots pass through it) and +30% speed until your next hit (2s at most). Your next bolt within 3s is empowered: it flies 12% further for 3.5 times the damage, 2 Marks and Shred; firing it ends the speed (+15% damage taken for 3s). Landing it gives the Vault back.

**Bola (RMB, 7s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* A 0.4s wind-up with the lane showing (you keep aiming), then the Bola flies 7m: 5 damage and a Mark; the first foe it wraps is Grounded for 1.5s (no dash, jump or glide; slowed 25%) and the Bola flies on 3.8m (a crate it breaks counts as that first catch). It bounces once off a wall. If it catches a second foe, both are stunned 0.9 and take +13 (TANGLED).
- *In the air:* Thrown from the air the same way.

**Shadow Knife (E, 15s cooldown).** Throw a dagger to your cursor (14m at most): it sticks in the first foe on the way (8 damage, slowed 50% for 2.5s) or lands where you aimed. Press E again to blink to it at once, leaving a trail of afterimages; after 6s you step to it anyway.

**Encore (X, level 6, 24s cooldown).** A violet ring grows round you (0.4s) to 5.4m and rides with you; for 2s a blade flies every 0.4s at each foe inside it (5 at most): 8 each, 12 from the outer band (past 4m), which also blights them for 6s (healing barely takes). You keep moving, shooting and dashing; casting it gives the Vault back, and every blade refreshes your Marks. Allies are untouched.
