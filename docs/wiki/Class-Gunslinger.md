# Gunslinger (medium)

A rhythm shooter. Every hit loads a chamber; six chambers arm a called shot that stuns, crits and
reloads you. Trick shots bounce off walls, and the slide reloads on the move.

## How it plays

1. Each weapon hit loads a **Chamber** (one per 0.25s); a tap roll loads two. A gold six-pip
   cylinder over your hat shows the count, to everyone.
2. At 6 the gun glows gold: your next **Trick Shot** (or Rail) is a **Called Shot**: +70%, a crit, a
   short stun, and a full magazine spun in when it lands.
3. **Trick Shot** (RMB tap) bounces off up to two walls and on to a second foe. **Hold** for a piercing Rail Charge.
4. **Slide Reload** (Shift): tap to roll (reloads part of the magazine), hold to slide and shoot, full reload at the end.
5. **Quickdraw** (E): 3s of faster fire, an instant reload and a dash charge back.
6. **Last Round** (R): lock on to every rival in sight, then fire one round per lock.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Six Chambers, then RMB | Called Shot: +70% (a Rail +35%), crit, stun 0.4, full reload on hit |
| Roll through a hit just as it lands | A perfect dodge: your next shots crit (rank 3: also loads 2 Chambers) |
| Trick Shot during Quickdraw | Two rounds, the second at 60% |
| RMB in the air (Recoil Shot) | You're kicked up and back, the target pushed away |
| Last Round held on a target for the full channel | The round also stuns 0.5 |

## Tips

- Chambers drain after 4s without a hit: keep shooting the same fight.
- The stun from a Called Shot is your window: unload the fresh magazine into it.
- Bank Trick Shots off walls to hit people behind cover.
- Last Round needs line of sight the whole channel; breaking it drops the lock.

### Abilities in full

**Six Chambers (passive).** Each weapon hit loads a chamber (one per 0.25s); a tap roll loads 2. At 6 the gun glows gold and your next Trick Shot is a Called Shot: +70% (a Rail +35%), a crit and stun 0.4, and when it lands the cylinder spins a full magazine in. Chambers drain after 4s without a hit.

**Slide Reload (dash, Shift, 9s cooldown).** Tap: a 3.4m roll that slips 35% of the magazine in (roll through a hit as it lands: your next shots crit). Hold: a 6m low slide you can shoot from that fully reloads at the end; let go early to refund 30%.

**Trick Shot (RMB, 7s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* A ricochet round 9m: 12 damage, bounces off up to 2 walls and on to a second foe (+20% a bounce).
- *Hold:* Rail Charge: hold up to 1s, a piercing rail 10 to 34 damage, 9 to 17m; full charge stuns 0.6.
- *In the air:* Recoil Shot: fire down and forward, kicked 3m up and back, the target pushed 6.
- *During E:* During Quickdraw it fires two rounds, the second 0.1s later at 60%.
- *Resource full:* At 6 Chambers: a Called Shot (+70%, crit, stun 0.4; a full reload when it lands).

**Quickdraw (E, 16s cooldown).** For 3s: fire 25% faster, an instant reload, a dash charge back, and your next Slide Reload's perfect-dodge window is twice as long.

**Last Round (R, 100s cooldown).** Channel up to 1.2s (slowed): gold lock lines draw to every rival hunter within 12m in sight. Press R again (or wait) to fire one round per lock: 16 + 12 per second locked; a full lock stuns 0.5. Breaking line of sight drops the lock.
