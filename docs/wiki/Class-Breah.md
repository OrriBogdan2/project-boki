# Breah (medium)

The quarry-hand. Raise rocks out of the ground, stand on them and climb them, then punch them into the enemy. A slam that knocks resets itself.

## How it plays

1. Tap the attack to hurl a boulder (it bursts and splashes), or hold it to see the punch lane and let go to punch everything in it.
2. **Stone Row** (E) raises three rocks after a red flash on the ground. Jump into one to climb it; punch along the row and every rock flies, one after another.
3. **Quakefall** (RMB): hold to gather (everyone sees the circle and its growing centre), let go to hop and slam. Jump while holding to leap forward; punch to lunge. The centre stuns.
4. **Rubble Rush** (Shift): a rock bursts up and rides ahead of you, shoving whoever it meets, then rolls on and slows.

### Abilities in full

**Tremor Sense (passive).** While sneaking, a narrow cone ahead (25 degrees, widening to 40 over 2s of sneaking, 16m long) shows every enemy in it through walls and fog as a molten ring, refreshed every half second.

**Rubble Rush (dash, Shift, 9s cooldown).** A rock bursts up in front of you and you barrel 4.2m behind it, shoving whoever it meets. Then it rolls on 6m: 12 damage, a shove, Shred (+15% damage taken for 3s) and a 1.5s slow.

**Quakefall (RMB, 9s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* A quick hop and slam just ahead: 14 damage and a shove, the centre stuns, rocks in the ring fly outward.
- *Hold:* Hold up to 1.2s: a bigger, harder slam (up to 30) whose growing centre stuns up to 1.2s. Jump while holding to leap forward; punch to lunge.
- *In the air:* In the air you dive, and slam where you land. A foe caught in the air is spiked down for +40%, +20 more over the abyss.

**Stone Row (E, 13s cooldown).** A red flash on the ground, then three rocks rise in a slight curve 2.2m ahead (on solid ground only, never into bodies). They stop foes and shots, hold 9s, you can stand on and climb them, and any punch, slam or dash-launch sends them flying.
