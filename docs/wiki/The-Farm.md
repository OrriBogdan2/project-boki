# The farm

Your farm is a floating island you lay out yourself. It's where your Boki live, where crops grow,
where Boki work stations turn wood and stone into things you need, and where you deal with what you
brought home. Every buildable, work element, tool and crop is listed on
[Farm reference](Farm-Reference.md).

## Starting out

A new farm is nearly empty: wild meadow, trees and boulders, the portal you arrive by, and 200 gems.
Open **build mode (B)** and place the essentials: the **house** (90 gems), a **crop patch** (30) and
the **cooking pot** (20). The **market stall** (30) is a good next step. Older saves keep the farm they had.

## Build mode

- Pick something from the catalog bar (Buildings, Work, Fences, Paths, Garden, Lights, Furniture,
  Statues, Island). A green ghost fits; a red one doesn't, and says why.
- **R**, the mouse wheel or right click turns it. Drag fences out in lines; paint paths and pond water
  tile by tile.
- Click anything already placed, buildings included, to pick it up and move it. **X** puts a
  decoration back into storage. **Ctrl+Z** undoes.
- Nothing may block a path the farm needs: every door, the portal, the pot, the stall, the crop
  patches and the feeders must stay reachable from your house.
- The **Island** tab has colour themes, expanding the island (costs wood and stone) and a layout reset.
- Indoors, B decorates instead.

## Materials

| Material | Where it comes from | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Gems | Selling crops and valuables, expeditions, challenges | Almost everything |
| Wood | Chopping trees (Axe), the Lumber Camp | Stations, buildings, expansions |
| Stone | Mining boulders (Pickaxe), the Quarry | Stations, buildings, expansions |
| Scrap | The Workbench (2 wood + 1 stone) | Upgrades and statues |
| Kibble | The Hearth Oven (from firewood), the Ranch Pen | Feeding Boki |
| Ember Coal | The Kiln (3 wood) | Warming Rare Eggs in the Incubator |

**Chopping and mining.** Buy an **Axe** or a **Pickaxe** at the market (Farm tab), walk up to a wild
tree or boulder and press F: three swings and it comes down. Trees grow back in about 15 minutes and
boulders in about 25, in real time, so the wild never runs out. Cliffs stay put.

## Boki at work

Every Boki has a few **work knacks** (Ember, Tide, Bloom, Forage, Timber, Stone, Craft, Spark, Frost,
Haul, Mend, Ranch), each at level 1 to 4. Farm Boki pick up jobs on their own: the best-suited Boki
takes each job, walks over, works (faster at higher levels) and leaves what it made in your stores.

- **Stations** are placed in build mode (the Work tab): Lumber Camp, Quarry, Storage Chest, Hearth
  Oven, Workbench, Spark Mill, Cold Store, Ranch Pen, Infirmary, Seed Garden and Kiln. Each one is
  worked by Boki with the matching knack.
- **The Job Board** sets a priority per job (Off, Low, Normal, High) and shows who's doing what. You
  can pin a Boki to one kind of job, keep it resting, or **assign** it to a station: an assigned Boki
  keeps working that station while you're away (up to a cap).
- Hungry or worn-out Boki stop to eat and nap. One pushed too hard on a High-priority job with no
  breaks gets overworked and glum. Owls and bats work at night; the rest leave it for morning.
- Boki asleep in the Boki Burrow never work.

## Crops and cooking

Plant seeds in the crop patch and water them (F). A watered plant grows a stage every 90 seconds
while you're home, and every expedition grows everything a stage. Rain waters for you, and a Tide
Boki will too. A plant watered every stage gives a bigger harvest and a seed back. Crops become
tonics, treats for your next match, snacks, a level for your partner (Starfruit), or gems at the stall.

## Buildings and places

- **House:** rest and decorate; Boki nap here.
- **Nesting Nook** and its **Incubator:** eggs from your own Boki, and Rare Eggs from boss lairs (see [Boki](Boki.md#getting-more-boki)).
- **Boki Burrow:** where Boki not out on the farm sleep. The farm holds 6 Boki at first, more with expansions.
- **Training post:** train a Boki's Power, Guard, Speed and Focus.
- **Feeders:** fill them and hungry Boki queue round to eat, even while you're away.
- **Armory:** what you brought home. Pick your loadout, sell or salvage the rest, upgrade, and get a bigger backpack.
- **Ransom House:** Old Pell's place, where lost Boki turn up and you deal with rivals who took yours.
- **Challenge House:** challenges, their rewards and what they unlock at the market.
- **Graveyard** and **Soul Shrine:** a grave for every Boki that died; with a Soul Ember the Shrine brings one back, a little different.
- **The training area:** open it from the portal's "Training area" button or the Esc menu. See [Arena and training](Arena-and-Training.md).
- **The portal:** expeditions, and (on the web build) the ferry to other players' islands and the town.
