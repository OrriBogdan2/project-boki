# Linwick (heavy)

A flue sweep in a sooty top hat with a bellows pack. Hoses foes down with fire until they Flash over, rams them
head first, and hits back harder after every knock.

## How it plays

1. Every hit builds **Char**; at 31 the foe is **Ablaze** (one flame over their head) and burns for 3s. Fed to 31 again they **Flash over** (three flames, a fiercer burn).
2. **Torch Lance** (LMB, hold): a cyan pilot flickers, then a hose of fire 7m long. It trails behind a sweep, narrow at the nozzle and billowing at the end, and hits harder on a burning foe. It stops the moment you let go.
3. **Cinder Shot** (RMB, hold to aim, release): a lane to your cursor, then a fireball that lands there (14m at most). On a burning foe it upgrades the burn and spreads it.
4. **Soot Ram** (Shift, 2 charges): head first toward the cursor; you stop on the first foe with a fiery hit. Rank 3 lays a lava carpet behind you.
5. **Furnace Cloak** (E): a ring flashes for a moment, then the blast drags foes in and wraps you in a swirling fire shield. A knock resets it.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Hold the lance on a foe about a second | Ablaze |
| Cinder Shot on a burning foe | upgrade and spread |
| Cloak, then lance | dragged-in foes catch fire fast |
| Ram, then lance | they are pinned at the nozzle |
| A knock | a heal, the cloak back, one ram charge back |
| Lance or cloak behind a wall | nothing |
| Lava carpet over the abyss | never forms |

## Tips

- Sweep slowly: the fire lags behind the nozzle, so lead a running foe.
- Open with the lance, finish the upgrade with Cinder Shot, and let a knock refresh the cloak.

### Abilities in full

**Flashover (passive).** Your fire builds Char on foes (it all falls off after 5s). At 31 Char the foe is Ablaze: 3s of burning for 2% of their health a second, one flame over their head. Fed again to full Char they Flash over: a fiercer burn (4%), three flames and a glow. Anything that stokes a foe already burning upgrades it. A knock heals you 16% of your health.

**Soot Ram (dash, Shift, 9s cooldown).** A quick, short head-first dash (fastest at the start) toward the cursor that stops on the first foe: 16 damage, a shove (you bounce back a little) and 17 Char (half a Flashover). Two charges. A knock restores one charge only. In the air it is a long, fast poof that launches you far and keeps your speed.

**Cinder Shot (RMB, 7s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* Hold to aim down a lane, release: a cinder that lands at your cursor (14m at most): 14 damage and it sets the foe Ablaze. On a foe that is already burning it upgrades the burn and spreads the burn to foes within 4m. A miss fizzles at the cursor.

**Furnace Cloak (E, 10.5s cooldown).** A thin ring flashes at 4.5m for 0.2s, then a blast of heat: 7 damage, 16 Char and Anti-Heal for 3s, and foes in sight are dragged toward you (walls stop them). Hit anyone and you wear a swirling 3s shield of 6.4% health plus 5.1% per foe. A knock resets it.

**Flue Flight (hunter ultimate, from level 6, 32s cooldown).** Lift off in a ring of embers and fly 1.4s toward the cursor, steering as you aim (up to 120 degrees a second). A 1.8m lava carpet forms under you and lasts 4s: 4.5 damage a second, a 30% slow, and 4 Char every 0.3s (Ablaze in about 2.4s). Press the ultimate again to land early. The Furnace Cloak works mid-flight and a knockback speeds you up. The carpet never forms over the abyss. 32s.
