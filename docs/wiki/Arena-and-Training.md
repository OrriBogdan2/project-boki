# Arena and training

Three places to practise, none of which cost you anything: nobody dies and nothing is lost.

## The Arena

From the title screen (no save needed). A round floor ringed by low ruined walls with four gates,
four pillars to slam people into, a raised dais on the north side and two patches of tall grass.

- **Dummies** that are real hunters, so Marks, wall slams and every ability work on them: one stands
  and watches you, one strafes, a heavy one waits by a pillar. Each shows damage per second, last hit
  and total over its head, and heals up when left alone.
- **A sparring bot** you switch between Off, Idle, Moving and Fighting (**N**), with any class.
- **X** resets. **O** turns cooldowns off (dash, RMB, E, R and the glove). **U** unlocks the ultimate.
- **2-7** swap class on the spot. **L** opens the loadout (class, weapon, Boki, glove, toggles). **K** shows the kit card.
- A downed player or bot is back up after a beat.

## The training area

From the Expedition menu at home. A safe island with target dummies and a checklist that walks you
through every button: walk, dash, glide, shoot, E, your ultimate (open from the start here), right
click (tap, then hold), your glove (take one from the rack first), sneak, throw your Boki, land a
combo, heal, stagger a big dummy, break a crate, take a found weapon off the ground, and dash off an
edge and float back up.

## The Training Grounds and the duel room

A small isle on your farm, across a short bridge south of the portal. Your whole kit works there
against dummies (one still, one strafing, one that lobs slow orbs to dodge) and a row of range
targets, with a climbing wall, a gap to glide across and a pad that resets your cooldowns.

A pad on it takes you to the **duel room**: you against one sparring bot on an empty island. Pick its
class, weapon, glove, difficulty and behaviour (fight, dodge only, stand still), reset whenever you
like, and watch your numbers: damage dealt and taken, how many of your shots land, how many of its
shots you dodge, and knocks. A knock resets the round.

## The tutorial

**First Flight**, played once after your first egg hatches: three small islands with nothing random
on them. See [Getting started](Getting-Started.md).
