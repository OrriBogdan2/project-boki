# Pin (light)

A light marksman who controls space. Every bow draw is timed, snares pin people in place, and a pinned
target eats the big shot. Spirit Walk carries her through rocks and barriers.

## How it plays

1. **Hold LMB** to draw: a V of two lines closes on the cursor. At 0.6s it's **Empowered** (one bright
   line, a flash at the bow) and the arrow lands where your cursor is. At 1.15s it's **Supercharged**
   (a thick white line past the cursor) and the arrow flies its full 16m. A quick tap lands at the cursor too.
2. Let go right as a stage flashes for a **perfect release** (a gold star by your bow hand): your next
   draw reaches Empowered 60% faster. Hold more than 0.5s past Supercharged and the arrow is
   **overdrawn**: half damage.
3. **Tap RMB** for a snare: it roots a foe, or plants a Tripwire on the ground. **Hold RMB** for a
   Heavy Shot (a gold V and a gold bar grow from the bow) that pierces and, driven into a wall, stuns (PINNED).
4. Snares and Heavy Shots that hit build **Focus**. At 100 your next shot is Supercharged at once with
   more range, and if it hits a rooted or stunned foe it's a **Deadeye**: a gold crit for +18.
5. **Hail Wall** (E): arrows rain for 4.4s on a curved wall around you at the cursor distance; it slows
   and blights (a blue burn: healing barely takes) everyone inside.
6. **Spirit Walk** (Shift): a dodge roll, then 2.8s walking through rocks, trees and barriers. Damage
   ends it; ending inside something costs 30 Focus and puts you back where you went in.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Release right on a stage's flash | The next draw is 60% faster to Empowered |
| Snare, then a full-Focus shot on the rooted foe | Deadeye (+18, crit) |
| Heavy Shot that pushes a foe into a wall within 3m | Stunned 1.2 (PINNED); fills Focus |
| Tap snare while your Hail Wall is falling | It roots 1.0s instead of 0.6s |
| Tap snare at 100 Focus | Roots 1.0s |
| RMB in the air (Plunge) | Shoots straight down, pushes foes, lifts you 3m and cools your glider |
| Spirit Walk through a rock | Out the other side where they can't follow |

## Tips

- Aim at the ground where they're going: Empowered arrows stop at your cursor.
- Count the flashes: perfect releases keep your draws quick all fight.
- Focus builds while you stand still or sneak and drains while you run; a hit on you knocks 40 off.
- Don't end a Spirit Walk inside a rock: it costs Focus and drops you back where you started.
- The sniper alternative trades the draw for a laser and one heavy round.

### Abilities in full

**Focus (passive).** Builds while you stand still (full in 1.4s), sneak (2.8s), 50% faster on high ground; a snare or Heavy Shot that hits adds 40 (a wall pin fills it). Drains while running and drops 40 when hit. At 100 your next shot is Supercharged or aimed at once, with +20% range, and if it hits a rooted or stunned foe it is a Deadeye: +18 damage, a crit. Hawk Sense: rivals under half health are seen through cover within 22m. A knock heals 18 and gives a dash charge back.

**Spirit Walk (dash, Shift, 6s cooldown).** A quick dodge roll in the direction you move (or aim), then 2.8s as a spirit: rocks, trees, pillars and ability barriers don't stop you (cliffs and ledges still do) and you move 15% faster. Taking damage ends it. If it ends while you're inside something you lose 30 Focus and are put back where you went in.

**Snare (RMB, 9s cooldown, 2 charges).**

- *Tap:* A snare arrow 11m: 8 damage and root 0.6 on a foe; on the ground it plants a Tripwire (root 1.2, reveal 3s).
- *Hold:* Heavy Shot: charge 0.5s -> full at 1.1s (rising creak, a ring, a ping at full): 16 -> 30 damage, push 6 -> 12, pierces 2; driven into a wall within 3m it hits again (up to 12) and stuns 1.2 (PINNED).
- *In the air:* Plunge: shoot straight down, push 8 around you, lift yourself 3m, cool the glider.
- *During E:* With your Hail Wall falling the tap snare roots 1.0.
- *Resource full:* At 100 Focus the tap roots 1.0.

**Hail Wall (E, 9s cooldown).** Lob a volley: 0.35s later arrows rain for 4.4s on a curved wall 120 degrees wide round you, at the cursor distance (2-9m). Foes inside take 5 every 0.5s, are slowed 35% and burn blue (healing barely takes).
