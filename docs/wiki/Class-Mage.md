# Mage (medium)

A tricky spell duelist. Runes a target with a bolt that bends round cover, detonates it with the next
spell, turns people into harmless critters, and blinks in and back out.

## How it plays

1. **Guided Bolt** (RMB): it fires on the press; keep holding and it bends toward your cursor. The
   longer it flies, the harder it hits. A hit **Runes** the target.
2. Your next spell on a Runed foe (a Bolt, a Hex or an echo) **detonates** it: a Runeburst that roots.
3. Casting builds **Heat**. At 70+ you're Overcharged: a detonation becomes a **Flashpoint** that also
   hits everyone near them. At 100 you Vent (a short nova) and lose your Heat.
4. **Hex** (E): a dodgeable bolt that turns a hunter into a critter for 1.3s (no attacks, casts, dashes or glides).
5. **Blink** (Shift) up to 5.5m through walls; within 3s, dash again to snap back to where you were.
6. **Encore** (R): two mirror images recast your last spell, then echo every Bolt and Hex for 4s.

## Combos and interactions

| Do this | And you get |
|---|---|
| Bolt hits, then Hex the same foe | The Hex detonates the Rune (Runeburst), then they're a critter |
| Detonate while Overcharged (70+ Heat) | Flashpoint: 30 damage plus 14 to everyone within 2.6m, root 0.8, Heat spent with no Vent, Bolt back |
| Blink, then dash again within 3s | Snap back to the sigil for free; the sigil also slows foes near it |
| RMB in the air (Starfall) | A bolt into the ground under you: pushes foes, lifts you, cools your glider |
| Encore, then cast | Both mirrors echo every Bolt and Hex at 60%; their bolts steer with yours |

## Tips

- Bend the Bolt round a pillar instead of trading shots in the open.
- Hex is a projectile: it can be dodged or blocked. Throw it after a Bolt lands, not before.
- Watch your Heat: an accidental Vent locks your RMB for a second.
- Blink in, detonate, snap back. The snap-back costs nothing.

### Abilities in full

**Runes and Heat (passive).** A Guided Bolt that hits Runes the target (4s, a ring over its head everyone sees). Your next spell on a Runed foe (a Bolt, a Hex, an echo) detonates the Rune: a Runeburst, 16 damage and root 0.5. Casting builds Heat (a Bolt 15, a Hex 25); at 70+ you're Overcharged (+25%) and a detonation is a Flashpoint: 30 damage, 14 to everyone within 2.6m, root 0.8, all your Heat spent with no Vent, and the Bolt back. At 100 you Vent: a 0.4s ring, then a 3m nova (14, push 9), Heat 0, RMB locked 1s.

**Blink (dash, Shift, 9s cooldown).** A 0.12s windup (everyone sees the sigil where you'll land), then you teleport up to 5.5m, through walls, never into the abyss. The sigil you leave slows foes 0.6s later, and for 3s you can dash again to snap back to it (free).

**Guided Bolt (RMB, 2.5s cooldown).**

- *Tap:* A slow bolt (15 m/s, 2.2s); keep holding and it bends toward your cursor (round cover). 10 damage, up to 22 after 1.6s in flight; it Runes what it hits.
- *In the air:* Starfall: a bolt into the ground under you: 10 damage, push 7, lifts you and cools the glider.
- *During R:* During Encore both mirrors echo it at 60%.
- *Resource full:* Overcharged (70+ Heat): +25%.

**Hex (E, 14s cooldown).** A dodgeable bolt (20 m/s, 11m): a hunter it hits becomes a harmless critter for 1.3s (half speed, no attacks, casts, dashes or glides); monsters are stunned 1s. On a Runed foe it detonates the Rune too.

**Encore (R, 100s cooldown).** A 0.55s fuse, then two mirror images appear at your sides and recast your last spell (Bolt or Hex) at full power; for 4s they echo every Bolt and Hex you cast at 60%. Their bolts steer with yours.
