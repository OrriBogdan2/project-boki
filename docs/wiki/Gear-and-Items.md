# Gear and items

Full numbers: [Weapons](Weapons.md) and [Gloves](Gloves.md).

## Your loadout

- **Weapon.** Each class carries one of its own weapons (its first one is the default; see the class
  pages). Left click is the weapon; right click is your class's RMB ability.
- **Gloves.** Two slots: one **active** glove on Q and one **passive** glove that just gives stats. A
  new one replaces the one in its slot. You can take gloves from your Armory or find them on the run.
- **Tonics** (1) heal; with full health, 1 uses an **armor kit** instead. **Armor kits** (2) repair armor.
  Tonics are brewed from Tidebloom at home or at the Infirmary.

## Found on the islands

- **Found weapons.** Some crates, chests, vaults and the Glimmerkin drop weapons with a rarity on
  top (Fine, Rare, Epic, Legendary). They're stronger than the Common version and yours until the
  expedition ends, unless you extract with them.
- **Elements.** Some found weapons carry an element whose hits burn, chill, arc or poison.
- **Named weapons.** Always Legendary and elemental, with a perk of their own. Wardens and vaults drop them, rarely.
- **Gloves**, **tonics**, **armor kits** and **armor shards**.
- **Chest keys.** One opens one chest. Carried by monsters and Shrublings; they drop with them, lie on the
  ground and go into your deathbox if you fall. Sometimes a spare drops from an elite, a boss, any
  monster, a pot, a barrel or a crate. Never sold in shops. They sit in your utility belt, up to 3; with
  a full stack a key stays on the ground for later.
- **Rift Keys.** Rare, found in matches only. Open a rift hatch (see [Expeditions](Expeditions.md#getting-out)). One sits on your utility belt; lost to your deathbox if you die.
- **Valuables** to sell at home: Old Coin, Sky Pearl, Boki Idol, Crown Shard, and the **Soul Ember**
  the Soul Shrine needs.
- **Rare Eggs** from lair bosses.
- **Gems** from monsters, chests and meteor stars.
- **Perk orbs.** Glowing orbs from chests (one), shrines (one or two) and bosses (a fan of three): walk into one
  to take it. Faster reloads, an extra dash, a longer glide, more health or armor, weapon upgrades and so on.
  They last the run.

## The backpack

A few slots for weapons, gloves and valuables you mean to carry home. Gems, seeds, tonics and kits are
counted, not packed, and scanned Boki (capsules in your box, 3 at most) ride along apart. What's in your hands doesn't take a slot.

- Picking up a better weapon or glove puts it in your hands and the old one in the pack.
- A full pack asks what to drop.
- At a deathbox, F opens its loot window; F again **smart loots** (best things first).
- A weapon you pick up arrives ready to fire (a full magazine, no reload running).
- Extract and it all goes to the Armory. Die and it goes into your deathbox.

**Keys on the belt.** Rift Keys and chest keys take a utility slot each (keys 1-4) and stack in it
(1 Rift Key, up to 3 chest keys), so you always see what you carry. With a full belt a key stays on the
ground: press a slot's key to swap that slot for it. Press the key's slot beside a hatch or chest to
open it, or anywhere else to ping the nearest one on your map (F still works too). Keys are found in
matches only, and go in your deathbox if you die.

**Backpack tiers.** Everyone has the free **Pouch**. A **Satchel** (+2 slots), **Pack** (+4) or
**Rucksack** (+6) is bought at the Armory for gems and scrap and picked on the expedition board; the
biggest you may bring depends on the difficulty (Satchel, Pack, Rucksack). It is at risk: die and it
is in your deathbox, and if you're revived you're back on the Pouch. Backpacks never drop at random.
In a match you get a bigger one by taking it from an enemy's deathbox (yours stays in its place) or
by buying the next tier at a shop. Extract wearing one and it's yours, even an enemy's.

## Cosmetics

**Glider skins** (canopy shape, colours, trail and sound) from the glider rack by the portal, and
outfits for Boki from the stall, worn into matches too. Cosmetic only.
