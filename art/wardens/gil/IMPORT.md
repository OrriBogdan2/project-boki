ART-1 pipeline (docs/art/wardens/PIPELINE.md steps 5-9). Raw files in ~/meshy/gill/ (never in git).

    B="~/tools/blender-4.5.15-linux-x64/blender -b --factory-startup --python"
    $B tools/meshy/bake_warden.py -- art/wardens/src/gil_bake.json        # s_g2 scan -> 30k low + 4096 atlas
    godot --headless -- --warden-rig-json --warden=gil                    # -> art/wardens/src/gil_rig.json

- **Scan:** Meshy s_g2 (hat, polka-dot coat, satchel, striped curl-toe boots, the fish face). No new Meshy credits.
- **Rig:** the body numbers in BODY.json (size 1.0, head 0.71, legs 1.03, torso 1.0, shoulder 1.14, arms 0.92,
  hit_r 0.38) put the rig's joints on the scan's; they belong in content/archetypes/gil.json `body` (kit's file).
- **Fit:** slots by region + colour (cloth coat and hat, skin face/hands/legs, leather satchel and boot soles),
  14k body triangles, fit weights, ink room in COLOR_0 alpha, atlas 2048.
- **Swordfish (skinned, its own mesh):** Meshy's m_sf swordfish cut to 1.6k triangles, texture 1024, 0.74 m bill to
  fork, held by the root of the tail in the RIGHT fist, bill forward and down (as Kael's sword); weighted 1.0 to
  hand.R, material `metal-swordfish`. `weapon_empty` hides the procedural weapon; FX empties weapon_muzzle (bill
  tip), anchor_blade_mid and anchor_blade_root sit on hand.R. The kit may want `skinned_weapon` in
  content/rules/warden_anim/gil.json, as Kael has.
- **Fish tail and dorsal fin (turnaround; scripted, gil_parts.py):** a tapered tube from under the coat's back hem,
  down and back to a forked caudal fin at boot height (`skin-tail` / `skin-fin`), and a ray fin on the upper back
  (`skin-fin`, blue root to orange edge); both rigid on hips. Simple stand-ins: no spring bones, flat from behind.
- **Import:** `meshes/generate_lods=false`, `force_disable_compression=true` in gil.glb.import.

## Skinning fix (2026-11-19, ext/hunter-fix-gil-1118)
Body: byte-for-byte the live v95 mesh (same vertices, normals, UVs, colours, 60000 triangles, skeleton, bind matrices,
textures, materials); only its skin weights change. Nothing is cut, split, added or deleted, so the rest pose silhouette
and every hole are exactly as live. Swordfish: same 3000 triangles, moved rigidly (no rotation, +0.18 x, +0.20 y,
+0.07 z) so its tail grip sits in the right hand instead of hanging 20 cm away by the knee, and skinned 50/50 forearm.R +
hand.R, the same blend as the hand skin, so the hand and the sword move as one.
Gil has no clips: the game poses his bones from the rig, so the fix is measured by skinning offline over a dump of the
real game's bone matrices (idle, walk, run, jump, glide, both dashes, LMB, RMB, E and its recast, X wave and leap; 900
frames).
- Live fault: the fused scan's weights left arm weight on hip skin and hips/thigh/tail mixed on the hem; the two copies of
  a UV-seam vertex carried different weights (827 seam points opened over 1 cm, up to 0.73 m).
- Right arm: the left arm's clean weight field (sleeve and the robe band beside it) is mirrored onto the right side; the
  hand is 50/50 forearm.R + hand.R with no body bone, the cuff blends into it. The right hand follows its bones like the
  left (live had it 8 cm off, dragged by hip weight).
- Then a stretch-minimising solve over the pose frames around the changed region (hand and cuff locked; arm skin may use
  only arm bones; body skin beside the sleeve may share upper_arm.R; no root weight), every UV-seam copy forced to the
  same weights.
- Result (900 frames): see the PR body for the measured numbers (edges past 3x, hand-to-bone, sword-to-hand).
